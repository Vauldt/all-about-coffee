## What's in scope

- **Reference** — distilled, durable knowledge that should outlive any single project.
- **How-to** — step-by-step procedures you'd want to follow under pressure.
- **Notes** — working notes that may eventually graduate into reference or how-to.

## What's *not* here

Daily journals, ephemeral meeting notes, and anything containing sensitive client or operational data. Those stay in the private Obsidian vault.
