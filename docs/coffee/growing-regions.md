# Growing Regions

Write a note for each coffee growing region in Taiwan. The note to have sections on:
1. General Introduction to the Region, where it is in the country, its history, its people, its neighbours, its terrain and its major population centres
2. Regional Coffee Terroir
3. Major coffee varieties grown.
4. How coffee is farmed and processed.
5. The quality of its coffee
6. The major market for its coffee.
7. Anything else of note about the region.
