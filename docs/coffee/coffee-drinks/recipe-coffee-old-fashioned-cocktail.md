# Coffee Old Fashioned

A rich, bourbon-forward twist on the classic Old Fashioned, enhanced with coffee liqueur and a citrus finish.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 oz bourbon  
- ½ oz coffee liqueur  
- ¼ oz simple syrup (or maple syrup)  
- 2 dashes orange bitters  
- 1 dash Angostura bitters  
- Orange peel (garnish)  
- Coffee beans (garnish)

---

## Instructions

1. Add bourbon, coffee liqueur, simple syrup, and bitters to a mixing glass or shaker with ice.
2. Stir (or shake) until well chilled.
3. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
4. Express the orange peel over the glass, rub around the rim, and drop it in.
5. Garnish with a few coffee beans.

---

## Tips

- Stirring (rather than shaking) keeps the drink spirit-forward and clear.
- Try maple syrup for a richer, autumnal flavor.
