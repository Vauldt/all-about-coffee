# White Russian

The creamier sibling of the Black Russian — smooth, indulgent, and easy to love.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz coffee liqueur  
- 1 oz heavy cream (or milk)  
- Ice

---

## Instructions

1. Fill a rocks glass with ice.
2. Pour vodka and coffee liqueur over the ice.
3. Float the cream on top by pouring slowly over the back of a spoon, or stir it in for a fully mixed drink.

---

## Tips

- Swap cream for oat milk or coconut cream for a dairy-free version.
