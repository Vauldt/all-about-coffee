# Black Russian

A simple, two-ingredient cocktail with bold coffee and vodka flavors.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz coffee liqueur (Kahlúa or Tia Maria)  
- Ice

---

## Instructions

1. Fill a rocks glass with ice.
2. Pour vodka and coffee liqueur over the ice.
3. Stir gently to combine.

---

## Tips

- Use quality coffee liqueur — it's the star of this drink.
- For a lighter version, add a splash of soda water.
