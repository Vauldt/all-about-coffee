## Overview

Bulletproof coffee is a blended coffee drink prepared by combining brewed coffee with butter (typically unsalted, grass-fed) and medium-chain triglyceride (MCT) oil, then blending until emulsified and frothy. The drink was popularised by entrepreneur Dave Asprey in the early 2010s and is associated with high-fat, low-carbohydrate dietary approaches. It is consumed as a meal replacement and claimed by proponents to sustain energy and cognitive performance, though these claims are not supported by clinical evidence.

## Key Facts

- Core recipe: brewed coffee + unsalted butter + MCT oil, blended until emulsified
- Associated with ketogenic and low-carbohydrate dietary frameworks
- High in calories and saturated fat; nutritionally unlike standard black coffee
- Health benefit claims lack robust clinical evidence
