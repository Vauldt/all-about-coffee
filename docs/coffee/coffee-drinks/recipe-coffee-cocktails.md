# Coffee Cocktails

Coffee cocktails combine the rich, roasted complexity of coffee with the craftsmanship of mixology. They range from silky dessert-style drinks to bold, spirit-forward concoctions. Whether you prefer hot, cold, or shaken variations, there's a coffee cocktail for every mood and occasion.

---

## Classic Coffee Cocktails

These drinks have stood the test of time — simple, satisfying, and globally loved.

- Irish Coffee — Hot coffee with Irish whiskey, brown sugar, and a layer of lightly whipped cream.
- Espresso Martini — Vodka, coffee liqueur, and fresh espresso shaken to a foamy finish.
- Black Russian — Vodka and coffee liqueur served over ice.
- White Russian — The Black Russian plus cream or milk, adding a smooth texture.

---

## Modern Creations

Contemporary bartenders and baristas have pushed coffee mixology forward with inventive twists.

- Coffee Old Fashioned — Bourbon, simple syrup, bitters, and a hint of coffee liqueur.
- Cold Brew Negroni — Replaces gin or vermouth partly with cold brew concentrate for a smoother, bitter-sweet blend.
- Espresso Tonic — Espresso layered over chilled tonic water with a citrus twist.

---

## Dessert-Style Cocktails

Perfect for after dinner or when you want a drink that doubles as a treat.

- [Carajillo](../cafe-drinks/carajillo.md) — Espresso paired with Licor 43 or brandy, often served on ice.
- Amaretto Affogato — Vanilla ice cream "drowned" with espresso and a shot of amaretto.

---

## Tips for Making Coffee Cocktails

- **Use fresh espresso or cold brew.** Stale coffee dulls the drink's flavor balance.  
- **Mind the temperature.** Hot cocktails need slightly cooled spirits to prevent flash evaporation.  
- **Balance sweetness and bitterness.** Coffee's natural bitterness pairs beautifully with sugar syrups, vanilla, or cream.  
- **Experiment with cold brew.** Its smooth, low-acid profile mixes better with many spirits than espresso's sharper edge.
