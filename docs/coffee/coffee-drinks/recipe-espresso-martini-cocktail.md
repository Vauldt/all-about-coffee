# Espresso Martini

A modern classic: smooth, bold, and topped with a silky crema. Perfect as a pick-me-up or after-dinner drink.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 oz vodka  
- 1 oz freshly brewed espresso (cooled) or cold brew concentrate  
- ½ oz coffee liqueur (Kahlúa or Mr Black)  
- ½ oz simple syrup  
- Ice  
- 3 coffee beans (garnish)

---

## Instructions

1. Add vodka, espresso, coffee liqueur, and simple syrup to a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
2. Shake vigorously for 15–20 seconds until the outside is frosty and foam forms.
3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe or martini glass.
4. Garnish with three coffee beans on top.

---

## Tips

- Fresh espresso produces the best crema — let it cool slightly before shaking.
- For a sweeter drink, increase simple syrup to ¾ oz.
