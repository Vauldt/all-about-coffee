# Espresso Tonic

A refreshing, non-alcoholic (or spiked) drink with a beautiful layered presentation.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 shot espresso (freshly brewed, cooled)  
- 4 oz tonic water  
- Ice  
- Lemon or orange twist (garnish)  
- *Optional:* 1 oz vodka or gin

---

## Instructions

1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
2. Pour tonic water over the ice.
3. Slowly pour cooled espresso over the back of a spoon to create layers.
4. Garnish with a citrus twist.
5. Stir before drinking.

---

## Tips

- Chill the espresso quickly in the freezer for 2–3 minutes before layering.
- Add vodka or gin for a boozy version.
