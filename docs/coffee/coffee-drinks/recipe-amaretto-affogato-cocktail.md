# Amaretto Affogato

A dessert cocktail hybrid — vanilla ice cream "drowned" with espresso and almond-flavored amaretto.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 scoop vanilla gelato or ice cream  
- 1 shot hot espresso  
- 1 oz amaretto liqueur

---

## Instructions

1. Place a scoop of gelato or ice cream in a small bowl or coupe glass.
2. Pour amaretto over the ice cream.
3. Top with a shot of hot espresso.
4. Serve immediately with a spoon.

---

## Tips

- Use high-quality gelato for the best texture.
- Try adding a sprinkle of cocoa or crushed biscotti.
