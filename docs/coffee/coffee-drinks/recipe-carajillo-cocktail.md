# Carajillo

A Spanish-Mexican favourite — espresso meets Licor 43 for a sweet, aromatic finish.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 shot espresso  
- 1½ oz Licor 43 (or brandy)  
- Ice

---

## Instructions

1. Fill a rocks glass with ice.
2. Pour Licor 43 over the ice.
3. Slowly pour espresso on top.
4. Stir gently and enjoy.

---

## Tips

- Licor 43's vanilla-citrus notes complement espresso beautifully.
- Serve as a digestif after dinner.
