## Overview

A chai latte is a café beverage made from spiced black tea — typically a masala chai concentrate or syrup — combined with steamed milk and light milk foam. Despite the word "latte" in its name, a chai latte does not contain coffee; it is a milk-steamed tea preparation served in a café context. A variant called a dirty chai latte adds a shot of espresso to the standard recipe. The drink is typically warm, sweet, and gently spiced, with flavour notes of cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, ginger, and sometimes black pepper.

## Key Facts

- A chai latte contains spiced black tea and steamed milk — no coffee unless specified as a "dirty chai"
- Common spices: cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, ginger, black pepper, and star anise
- Café preparation most often uses a pre-made chai concentrate or syrup mixed with steamed milk and foam
- Traditional preparation brews strong masala chai separately then combines with hot milk
- Caffeine comes from the black tea base; typically lower than an equivalent espresso drink
- A dirty chai latte adds a shot of espresso, introducing both coffee flavour and additional caffeine
