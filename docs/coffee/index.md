# Coffee

Notes on coffee — from green sourcing to the cup. The aim is reproducibility: a recipe or reference here should let me (or anyone) get the same result twice.

!!! abstract "Scope"
    Filter and espresso brewing, grinder and machine notes, water chemistry, tasting and QC frameworks. Roast profiling lives in its own subsection once there's enough material to be useful.

## Tasting framework

When evaluating a coffee, score these in order — early failures make later ones moot:

1. **Defects** — any off-flavors? If yes, stop.
2. **Acidity** — bright, balanced, flat?
3. **Sweetness** — present and persistent, or dropping off?
4. **Body** — light, medium, syrupy?
5. **Finish** — clean, lingering, astringent?
6. **Distinctive notes** — what makes this specific coffee *this* coffee?

!!! example "Calibration tip"
    Cup the same coffee at 8.5, 9.0, and 9.5 minutes from pour. Pick the one where sweetness peaks before bitterness arrives — that's your extraction sweet spot for this roast.
