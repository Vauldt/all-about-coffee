## Overview

Amsterdam has a well-developed specialty coffee culture with a concentration of quality-focused cafés and roasters, reflecting the Netherlands' long historical relationship with the global coffee trade — Dutch merchants were among the first to transplant coffee cultivation outside Arabia in the 17th century. The city's openness to international café culture and its significant tourist traffic have supported a thriving independent café scene, complemented by roasters committed to direct and transparent sourcing.
