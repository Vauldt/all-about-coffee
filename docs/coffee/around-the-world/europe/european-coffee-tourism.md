## Overview

European Coffee Tourism refers to the growing trend of travelling specifically to explore specialty café and roastery scenes in cities known for coffee excellence. Cities such as Copenhagen, Oslo, London, Berlin, Vienna, and Lisbon attract coffee-focused visitors, with dedicated café guides, walking tours, and specialty coffee publications serving this audience. The trend reflects the maturation of specialty coffee as a cultural experience akin to food tourism, with informed consumers seeking out particular roasters, brewing styles, and café environments.
