## Overview

Berlin has developed a prominent European specialty coffee scene anchored by roasters such as The Barn — known for ultra-light, Nordic-influenced roasting — and Bonanza Coffee Roasters, alongside a dense network of independent cafés serving a creative and internationally mobile population. The city's affordability, cultural openness, and concentration of young professionals from across Europe and beyond have made it a fertile environment for specialty coffee innovation and café culture experimentation.
