## Overview

London developed one of the world's leading specialty coffee scenes from the mid-2000s, shaped significantly by Australasian migrants bringing the Melbourne café model of skilled, approachable espresso service. Early pioneers such as Monmouth Coffee (founded 1978) laid quality foundations, while roasters such as Square Mile Coffee, Workshop Coffee, and Ozone built on that legacy in the third-wave era. London's scale, international character, and café density make it a global reference point for specialty coffee culture.
