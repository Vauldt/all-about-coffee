## Overview

Japan has achieved exceptional success in international coffee competitions, particularly the World Brewers Cup, where Japanese competitors have won multiple titles. Tetsu Kasuya's 2016 World Brewers Cup victory — in which he presented the 4:6 Method — became one of the most influential competition moments in specialty coffee history, transforming how the global industry approached manual brewing ratios and pour-over technique. Japan's competition success reflects the country's broader culture of precision and mastery.
