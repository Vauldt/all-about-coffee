## Overview

Tokyo is home to one of the world's most refined specialty coffee scenes, blending the precision and ritual of traditional kissaten culture with modern third-wave influences. Neighbourhoods such as Nakameguro, Shimokitazawa, and Koenji have become internationally recognised coffee destinations, attracting visitors seeking both heritage espresso bars and light-roast single-origin pour-overs. The city's culture of meticulous craft and service has made it a significant reference point for specialty coffee globally.
