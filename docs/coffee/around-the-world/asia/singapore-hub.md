## Overview

Singapore functions as a regional coffee hub in Southeast Asia, with a well-developed specialty café scene, significant green coffee trading and importing activity, and a population with high purchasing power and sophisticated coffee tastes. Traditional kopi culture — dark-roasted Robusta prepared with butter or sugar and served in hawker centres — coexists alongside modern specialty coffee, making Singapore distinctive in the region for sustaining both a deep-rooted indigenous coffee tradition and a thriving contemporary third-wave sector.
