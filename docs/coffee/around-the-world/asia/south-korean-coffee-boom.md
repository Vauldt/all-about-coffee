## Overview

South Korea experienced explosive growth in its specialty coffee sector from the 2010s, with Seoul developing one of the world's densest concentrations of independent cafés, high-quality espresso bars, and a fiercely competitive barista culture strongly influenced by international competition and social media. The country's rapid economic development, young urban population, and cultural appetite for premium experiences combined to make specialty coffee a mainstream phenomenon at a pace rarely seen elsewhere.
