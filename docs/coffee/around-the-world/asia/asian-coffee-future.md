## Overview

Asia represents the largest growth market for specialty coffee globally, with rising middle-class income driving demand across a vast and diverse region. Rapidly growing domestic production sectors in Vietnam, China, and Thailand are developing specialty-grade capacity, while increasingly sophisticated consumption cultures in South Korea, Taiwan, Japan, and Singapore continue to set high standards for café quality and innovation. The region's combination of producer and consumer nations within close proximity positions it as a central force in the next era of specialty coffee development.
