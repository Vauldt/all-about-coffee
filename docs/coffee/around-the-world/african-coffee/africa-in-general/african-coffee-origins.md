# African Coffee Origins

Africa is the birthplace of coffee and home to some of the world's most distinctive and complex coffees. This MOC organises African coffee by country, from Ethiopia's wild heirloom diversity to Kenya's blackcurrant brightness, from Rwanda's surging export renaissance to Yemen's ancient traditions under pressure from ongoing conflict.

> **2025 Production Update:** Ethiopia's 2024/25 harvest is forecast at **8.5 million bags** (a 500,000-bag increase YoY), driven by ideal rainfall and on-cycle conditions in Sidamo and Yirgacheffe. The 2025/26 season is projected to set a record at **11.6 million bags** (694,000 tonnes), up 9%, as over 450,000 hectares have been replanted with improved varieties. Coffee exports generated **$1.87 billion** in the ten months to April 2025, an 87% increase in value, with Germany, Saudi Arabia, and the USA as top markets.
> *Sources: USDA / Sucafina / ECTA, 2025*

**Regions:** [Nyeri Coffee](../../../coffee-geography/nyeri-coffee.md) | [Kirinyaga Coffee](../../../coffee-geography/kirinyaga-coffee.md) | [Kiambu Coffee](../../../coffee-geography/kiambu-coffee.md) | Murang'a Coffee | Embu Coffee | Meru Coffee

**Characteristics:** SL28 and SL34 Varieties | Kenyan Double Fermentation | Kenyan Factory System | Kenyan Coffee Profile

> **Variety Note:** The SL in SL28 and SL34 stands for **Scott Agricultural Laboratories**, which developed these varieties in the 1930s. SL28 is prized for its drought resistance and exceptional cup — complex blackcurrant, citrus, and berry. Green coffee is graded by screen size: **E (Elephant), PB (Peaberry), AA, AB, C, TT, T, MH/ML**. The Nairobi Coffee Exchange (NCE) is a non-profit regulated by the government, operating a web-based remote trading platform.
> *Sources: Genuine Origin Kenya Origin Report 2024; NCE*

> **2025 Production Update:** Rwanda's coffee sector is on a record trajectory. Production rose **25% in 2024/25** to 21,295 tonnes, with export revenues surging **47% to $116.1 million**. In 2025, exports grew a further **39% to 23,860 tonnes**, generating a historic **$148.6 million** — well ahead of the 2029 national target of $160 million. Average farm-gate price in 2025 was approximately Rwf 900/kg (above the minimum of Rwf 600).
> *Sources: NAEB / GCR Magazine / USDA, 2025–2026*

## Yemen — The Historic Origin

- Yemen Coffee Overview — Historic significance and current challenges
- Yemen Coffee History — Mocha port, ancient cultivation, global trade routes
- Yemen Coffee Standards — Traditional sorting, limited formal grading
- Yemeni Coffee Crisis — Conflict since 2015, qat competition, infrastructure collapse

**Regions:** Matari Coffee | Sanani Coffee | Ismaili Coffee

**Characteristics:** Ancient Yemeni Varieties | Yemeni Terraced Farming | Yemen Coffee Counterfeiting

> **Current Status (2025):** Coffee cultivation in Yemen dates to approximately 1450 CE — the world's first commercially cultivated and traded coffee. Today, Yemen's coffee sector faces severe disruption from the civil war that began in 2015. Export volumes have nearly halved since 2014. Despite conflict, Yemen's specialty coffees command extraordinary prices: at the **Best of Yemen 2024** auction, the average price reached **$369/kg** and the top lot sold for a record **$1,159/kg**.
> *Sources: Green Coffee Collective; World Coffee Portal 2024; FAO Yemen 2025*
