## Overview

The Cup of Excellence programme has been established in multiple African coffee-producing countries including Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, and Uganda, providing a rigorous competition and online auction mechanism that rewards quality and directs significant price premiums directly to outstanding producers. The programme's expansion into Africa has been particularly impactful in post-conflict economies such as Rwanda and Burundi, where the ability to command premium prices for exceptional lots has supported both farm investment and community development.
