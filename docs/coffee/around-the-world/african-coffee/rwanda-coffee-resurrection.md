## Overview

Rwanda's coffee sector underwent a remarkable recovery and quality transformation in the years following the 1994 genocide, with international investment in washing station infrastructure, the establishment of Cup of Excellence competitions, and growing recognition of Rwandan washed Arabicas for their clean, fruity cup profiles. The country's coffee has become a flagship example of quality-led development in post-conflict agricultural recovery, connecting Rwandan smallholder farmers directly to specialty export markets.
