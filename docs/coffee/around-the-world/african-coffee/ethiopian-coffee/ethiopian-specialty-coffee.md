## Overview

Ethiopia is both the birthplace of Coffea arabica and one of the world's most important specialty coffee origins, offering extraordinary cup diversity across regions including Yirgacheffe (floral, jasmine, bergamot), Guji and Sidamo (stone fruit, complex acidity), Harrar (wild berry, natural process), and Kaffa (the original genetic homeland of arabica). Ethiopian lots consistently attract top scores at Q Grader evaluations and Cup of Excellence auctions. The country's unparalleled genetic diversity — thousands of indigenous heirloom varieties — underpins its singular flavour range, making Ethiopia a benchmark origin for specialty buyers worldwide.
