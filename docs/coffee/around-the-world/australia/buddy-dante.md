# Domenic "Dante" Spadaccini

Domenico Dante Spadaccini — widely known in the Australian specialty coffee industry simply as **Dante** — is a Perth-based master coffee roaster, entrepreneur, and former international barista judge. He is the founder and driving force behind [Dante's Coffee Roasters](https://dantescoffee.com.au), one of Western Australia's most celebrated specialty coffee operations.

---

## Background

Domenic shares his middle name with the renowned Florentine poet [Dante Alighieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dante_Alighieri), whose epic three-part poem *The Divine Comedy* is widely considered the greatest work of Italian literature — an inspiration reflected in Dante's Coffee's signature blends.[^1]

He reportedly began drinking coffee at just five years of age, developing an early and deep appreciation for the beverage.[^2] His commercial roasting career began in **1997**, spanning nearly three decades of hands-on expertise in sourcing, roasting, and blending specialty coffee.

---

## Career

Domenic's career has taken him across both Australia and Asia. Before establishing Dante's Coffee Roasters, he:

- Owned and operated a café
- Ran his own independent roasting business
- Established a [successful coffee chain in Hong Kong](https://food4media.com/showPRPreview/judging-panel-gbc-hofex2011), where he served as General Manager and Roaster for Holly Brown Coffee

His international experience, including serving as a barista judge at events such as GBC@HOFEX 2011 in Hong Kong, reinforced his reputation as one of the more knowledgeable coffee professionals in the Asia-Pacific region.[^3]

---

## Dante's Coffee Roasters

[Dante's Coffee Roasters](https://dantescoffee.com.au) was formally established in Perth, Western Australia — with the roastery located at Unit 3, 34 Prestige Parade, Wangara.[^4] The business is a **family-owned boutique contract coffee roaster** offering retail and wholesale supply across Australia.

### Products & Philosophy

The roastery's product range includes:

- **Signature blends** — including *Dante's Inferno* and *Dante's Paradiso*, both inspired by Dante Alighieri's *Divine Comedy*[^1]
- **Single origin** coffees from leading coffee-growing regions worldwide
- **Organic** and **decaffeinated** options
- **Coffee capsules** — Dante's is noted as the first capsule manufacturer in Western Australia[^4]

Domenic's roasting philosophy centres on precision and timing. He recommends consuming roasted coffee [two to eight weeks after roasting](https://www.brewhub.com.au/from-bean-to-cup-coffee-culturalist-domenico-spadaccini-shares-his-secrets/), noting that coffee begins deteriorating from the moment it leaves the roaster.[^2] His roast profiles, developed by carefully controlling heat and airflow, typically take around 15 minutes — acknowledging the fine line between underdevelopment and over-roasting.[^2]

### Roasting Technique

> "You can easily ruin a roast by going too slow. But go too fast, and you'll end up with a situation where your coffee looks done on the outside but is hard and raw on the inside." — Domenic Dante Spadaccini[^2]

Key principles from his approach:

- Specialty coffee only — single origin arabica and robusta from the world's best growing regions
- Roast profiles are customised per bean, balancing heat introduction and airflow at key stages
- Rest period post-roast is essential for degassing before optimal flavour is achieved

---

## Industry Role & Legacy

Domenic is regarded within Perth's coffee community as the **"Godfather" of Dante's operations** — a title reflecting both his foundational role and enduring influence on the company's culture and standards.[^5][^6]

Under his stewardship, Dante's Coffee Roasters has grown to offer:

- [Wholesale supply](https://dantescoffee.com.au/wholesale-inquiry/) to cafés across Australia
- A [franchise model](https://dantescoffee.com.au/franchise/) enabling independent café operators to partner with the brand
- A retail café space at the Wangara roastery, open Monday–Friday

The business has received multiple awards for its coffee blends and is considered a benchmark for quality among [Perth coffee roasters](https://dantescoffee.com.au/exploring-the-craft-inside-perth-coffee-roaster-culture/).[^7]
