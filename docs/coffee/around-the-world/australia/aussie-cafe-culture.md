## Overview

Australian café culture is defined by independent venues, highly skilled baristas, neighbourhood regularity, and a high standard of espresso and milk drinks presented accessibly without pretension. This model is distinct from both the US third-wave approach — which can trend towards formality and education-heavy service — and European traditions rooted in standing bars and commodity espresso. The culture prizes technical craft alongside hospitality warmth, making quality coffee feel everyday rather than exclusive.
