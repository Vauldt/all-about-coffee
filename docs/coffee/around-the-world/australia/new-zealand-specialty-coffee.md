## Overview

New Zealand developed a sophisticated specialty coffee culture alongside Australia, with strong espresso traditions centred in Wellington and Auckland. The country shares credit with Australia for originating the flat white and has produced world-class barista talent supported by rigorous training programmes. New Zealand's café culture mirrors the Australian model of independent, quality-focused venues with accessible, unpretentious service.
