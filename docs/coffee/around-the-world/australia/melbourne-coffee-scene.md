## Overview

Melbourne is widely regarded as one of the world's premier specialty coffee cities, built on post-war Italian espresso culture and the subsequent development of a dense independent café sector. The city's culture of approachable excellence — high technical standards delivered without pretension — has influenced baristas and café operators globally. Melbourne's neighbourhood café model, with a focus on community, skill, and seasonal menus, became a reference point for the international third-wave movement.
