## Overview

Portland, Oregon is one of the birthplaces of American third-wave specialty coffee, home to Stumptown Coffee Roasters (founded 1999) and Heart Coffee Roasters, and known for an independent, anti-corporate café culture that prioritised quality, local identity, and direct relationships with producers. The city's role in establishing the modern template for third-wave café culture — quality-obsessed, independently owned, community-focused — has been foundational to the broader American specialty movement.
