## Overview

Seattle is the home of Starbucks (founded 1971), which made the city synonymous with the second wave of coffee and the mainstream popularisation of espresso-based drinks in the US. Despite — or in response to — this dominance, Seattle has also developed a significant third-wave specialty scene as a quality-focused independent counterpoint; roasters such as Victrola Coffee Roasters, Caffe Vita, and Lighthouse Coffee represent the city's commitment to craft and origin transparency beyond the Starbucks model.
