## Overview

Los Angeles developed a vibrant specialty coffee scene from the 2010s, driven by Intelligentsia's Silver Lake café, the emergence of Go Get Em Tiger, Verve Coffee Roasters' expansion from Santa Cruz, and a café culture well-suited to the city's diverse, trend-receptive, and health-conscious population. LA's geography — a sprawling city of distinct neighbourhoods — has produced a distributed specialty café landscape rather than a concentrated urban core, with quality venues spread across areas from Silver Lake to Venice and Culver City.
