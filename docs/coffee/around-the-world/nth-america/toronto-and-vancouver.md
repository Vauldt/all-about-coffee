## Overview

Toronto and Vancouver are Canada's two most significant specialty coffee cities, each with dense independent café cultures shaped by different regional influences. Vancouver's Pacific Rim connections have produced strong ties to Australasian café style — including notable milk-forward espresso drinks and quality-obsessed service — while Toronto's scene is shaped by a large and diverse multicultural population that supports a wide range of coffee traditions and contemporary specialty formats.
