## Overview

Carbon-neutral coffee refers to products or operations where greenhouse gas emissions from production, processing, roasting, and transport are measured and offset or eliminated through renewable energy use, carbon credits, or supply chain changes. Several specialty roasters have pursued carbon-neutral or net-zero certifications as consumer interest in environmental impact has grown, though debate continues within the industry about the relative value of offsetting versus direct emissions reduction.
