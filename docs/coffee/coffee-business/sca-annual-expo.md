## Overview

The SCA Annual Expo is the largest specialty coffee trade show and conference in the world, held annually in a rotating US city with a separate European event. It brings together roasters, green coffee buyers, equipment manufacturers, baristas, and educators for product launches, World Coffee Events competitions, panel discussions, cupping sessions, and professional networking. The Expo functions as the primary annual gathering point for the global specialty coffee industry and a key platform for new product introductions and industry trend-setting.
