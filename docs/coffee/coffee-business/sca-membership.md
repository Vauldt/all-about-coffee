# SCA Membership

Membership in the [Specialty Coffee Association](../specialty-coffee/specialty-coffee-association.md) provides coffee professionals and businesses with access to resources, education, community, and industry representation. The SCA offers several membership tiers designed to serve different needs within the coffee community.

## Membership Types

### Individual Membership

**Who It's For**: Coffee professionals, enthusiasts, and industry workers

**Annual Cost**: Approximately $95-150 USD (varies by region)

**Benefits**:
- **Education Discounts**: Reduced rates on SCA Coffee Skills Program courses
- **Event Access**: Discounted registration for SCA Expo and World of Coffee
- **Resource Library**: Access to publications, research, and best practices documents
- **Digital Resources**: Online learning platform and webinar archives
- **Community Connection**: Member directory and networking opportunities
- **Publications**: Industry updates, newsletters, and research reports
- **Voting Rights**: Participate in SCA governance and elections
- **Job Board**: Access to career opportunities listing
- **Member Portal**: Online hub for resources and community

**Ideal For**:
- Baristas and café staff
- Roasters and quality control professionals
- Coffee educators and trainers
- Independent consultants
- Passionate coffee enthusiasts
- Students pursuing coffee careers

### Business/Company Membership

**Who It's For**: Coffee businesses and organizations

**Annual Cost**: Approximately $300-500 USD (varies by company size and region)

**Additional Benefits Beyond Individual**:
- **Multiple Employee Access**: Several staff members can access benefits
- **Company Listing**: Profile in SCA business directory
- **Bulk Training**: Group discounts for staff education
- **Marketing Opportunities**: Sponsorship and promotional options
- **Priority Support**: Dedicated business member services
- **Networking Events**: Exclusive business member gatherings
- **Industry Insights**: Market research and trend reports
- **Recruitment Tools**: Job posting and talent search capabilities

**Covered Employees**: Typically 3-10 individuals depending on membership level

**Ideal For**:
- Cafés and coffee shops
- Roasting companies
- Importers and exporters
- Equipment manufacturers
- Coffee training centers
- Supply and service companies

### Student Membership

**Who It's For**: Full-time students pursuing coffee-related education

**Annual Cost**: Approximately $35-50 USD (significantly discounted)

**Benefits**:
- All standard individual membership benefits
- **Reduced Rates**: Deeper discounts on courses and events
- **Career Resources**: Job search tools and mentorship opportunities
- **Student Community**: Connect with other coffee students globally
- **Scholarship Opportunities**: Access to financial aid programs

**Requirements**:
- Valid student ID or enrollment verification
- Full-time student status
- Annual renewal with updated verification

**Ideal For**:
- Culinary or hospitality students
- Food science students
- Agricultural students in coffee-growing regions
- Business students focusing on coffee industry

### Honorary Membership

**Who Receives It**: Individuals making exceptional contributions to specialty coffee

**Selection**: Awarded by SCA board of directors

**Benefits**: Full membership privileges with no annual fee

**Criteria**:
- Significant lifetime achievement in coffee
- Major contributions to specialty coffee advancement
- Industry leadership and innovation
- Service to the coffee community

- **Network value**: Connections lead to opportunities

**Example ROI**:

**For Businesses**:
- **Team training**: Discounts on multiple employee certifications
- **Marketing exposure**: Directory listing and sponsorship opportunities

## How to Join

### Registration Process

1. **Visit SCA Website**: Navigate to membership section
2. **Choose Membership Type**: Select appropriate tier
3. **Create Account**: Provide contact information and details
4. **Payment**: Credit card or other accepted methods
5. **Confirmation**: Receive welcome email with member number
6. **Access Portal**: Log in to member resources immediately

### Membership Period

**Duration**: 12 months from purchase date (not calendar year)  
**Renewal**: Automatic renewal option or manual renewal  
**Reminders**: Email notifications before expiration  
**Grace Period**: Limited time to renew without losing benefits

### International Membership

**Regional Pricing**: Some regions have adjusted pricing  
**Currency**: Payment in USD or local currency (varies)  
**Global Benefits**: Full benefits regardless of location  
**Local Chapters**: Access to chapters worldwide

## Membership Comparison

### SCA vs. Other Organizations

**Coffee Quality Institute (CQI)**:
- **Focus**: Specifically on Q Grader certification and quality
- **Cost**: Higher, tied to Q calibration
- **Scope**: Narrower (quality assessment focus)
- **Complementary**: Many professionals join both

**Barista Guild**:
- **Relationship**: SCA member community specifically for baristas
- **Membership**: Separate from main SCA membership
- **Benefits**: Barista-specific resources and community
- **Integration**: Works within SCA framework

**National Coffee Associations**:
- **Geographic**: Country or region specific
- **Relationship**: Often affiliated with SCA
- **Benefits**: Local events and community
- **Coordination**: May use SCA standards and curriculum

## Membership Policies

### Renewal and Cancellation

**Renewal**:
- Automatic renewal option (can be disabled)
- Email reminders before expiration
- 30-day grace period for renewal
- Prorated refunds not typically available

**Cancellation**:
- Can cancel auto-renewal anytime
- Membership valid until expiration date
- No partial refunds
- Can rejoin at any time

### Member Conduct

**Expectations**:
- Professional behavior at events
- Respectful community engagement
- Adherence to SCA code of conduct
- Honest representation of credentials
- Ethical business practices

**Violations**: May result in membership suspension or termination
