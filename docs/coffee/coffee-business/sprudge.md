## Overview

Sprudge is a specialty coffee news and culture publication founded in 2009 by Jordan Michelman and Zachary Carlson in Los Angeles. It is one of the most widely read English-language specialty coffee media outlets globally, covering café openings, competition results, industry news, barista culture, and origin travel through a voice that is both informative and distinctly irreverent. Sprudge also publishes the annual Barista Magazine awards and produces the Sprudge Wine vertical, reflecting the cultural overlap between specialty coffee and natural wine communities.
