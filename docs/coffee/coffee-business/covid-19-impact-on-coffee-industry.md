# COVID-19 Impact on Coffee Industry

**Tags:** #coffee/business #coffee/history

## Overview

The COVID-19 pandemic (2020–2022) had severe short-term impacts on the specialty coffee industry, closing cafés globally, disrupting supply chains, reducing café foot traffic, and straining roastery cash flows. It also accelerated several longer-term trends including the home brewing boom, the growth of subscription coffee services, and the normalisation of takeaway-only café operations — reshaping the industry's retail landscape and consumer habits in ways that persisted well beyond the pandemic period.
