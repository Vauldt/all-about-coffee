## Overview

European Coffee Trip is a coffee media and education company founded in 2013 in Prague, Czech Republic, by Aneta Štefanovská and Radovan Štefanovský. It produces educational videos, city café guides, and online courses covering specialty coffee culture across European cities, and has become one of the most widely viewed specialty coffee media channels globally. Its city guide series documents specialty café scenes in cities from London to Istanbul, providing both consumer recommendations and an accessible entry point for people discovering specialty coffee.
