## Overview

The Syndicat Français du Café (French Coffee Syndicate) is a French trade association representing companies engaged in the production and marketing of roasted, soluble, and portioned coffee in France. It represents the industrial and mainstream segment of the French coffee market and serves as a collective voice for its member companies before public authorities on regulatory, labelling, food safety, and trade issues affecting the industry.

## Member Representation

The Syndicat represents member companies in dealings with public authorities on regulation, labelling, food safety, and trade issues affecting the coffee sector in France. It works to promote its members' products and to document the economic importance of coffee consumption across retail and out-of-home channels in France.

## Sustainability and Responsibility

Member companies commit to limiting environmental impact within French territory, including packaging and waste, and to addressing climate-change challenges across coffee supply chains. The Syndicat monitors scientific research on coffee and health to inform member communication and practices.

## Position in the French Coffee Industry

The Syndicat Français du Café operates within the broader structure of French coffee industry representation, complementing more specialty-oriented organisations such as [Collectif café](collectif-cafe.md). Both bodies are influenced by frameworks and initiatives linked to the International Coffee Organization (ICO).

## Key Facts

- French trade association representing producers and marketers of roasted, soluble, and portioned coffee
- Represents industrial and mainstream segment of the French coffee market
- Engages with public authorities on regulation, labelling, food safety, and trade issues
- Member companies commit to environmental responsibility and supply chain sustainability
