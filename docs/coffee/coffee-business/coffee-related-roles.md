## Overview

The coffee industry supports a broad range of specialised roles spanning farming, processing, trade, roasting, equipment, café service, and education. Each stage of the supply chain — from farm to cup — requires distinct technical knowledge and professional skills. This article maps the principal roles across the full farm-to-café pipeline.

## Farm-to-Café Pipeline Overview

| Stage | Typical Roles |
| :--- | :--- |
| Farming | Coffee Farmer / Producer, Farm Manager (Coffee), Coffee Agronomist / Crop Specialist, Coffee Field Worker / Picker, Coffee Processing-Station Manager (Wet Mill / Washing Station) |
| Local processing | Coffee Processing Worker (Sorting / Pulping / Fermenting / Washing / Drying), Coffee Dry Mill Operator, Coffee Cooperative Manager, Origin Coffee Quality Controller / C upper |
| Trade and logistics | Local Coffee Trader / Intermediary, Coffee Exporter, Coffee Importer / Green Coffee Supplier, Green Coffee Buyer, Coffee Export Logistics Coordinator, Coffee Trader (Futures and Physical) |
| Roasting | Coffee Roaster / Production Roaster, Head Coffee Roaster, Roastery Production Assistant, Roasted Coffee Quality Control Specialist, Coffee Production Manager / Plant Manager |
| Equipment | Coffee Equipment Technician, Coffee Roaster Technician, Coffee Equipment Manufacturing Engineer |
| Café and retail | [Barista](../barista/barista.md), Senior Barista / Head Barista, Café All-Rounder (Coffee), Café Supervisor / Shift Leader, Café Manager / Store Manager, Café Owner, Coffee Trainer / Barista Trainer, Coffee Account Manager (Roastery to Café) |

## Key Facts

- The coffee supply chain spans at minimum seven distinct stages from farming to cup, each with specialised professional roles
- The cooperative model in East Africa means washing-station and cooperative management roles are as important as farm-level roles for quality outcomes
- Green coffee buying is a specialist role requiring cupping proficiency, origin knowledge, and supply chain management skills
- Barista is the most visible role in the supply chain but sits at the final stage of a much longer production and logistics pipeline
