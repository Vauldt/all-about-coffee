## Overview

AST Sensory Analysis is the sensory skills pathway within the Authorised SCA Trainer ([AST](ast-certification.md)) certification programme. This qualification enables coffee professionals to officially teach SCA Sensory Skills courses at Foundation, Intermediate, and Professional levels, and to issue official SCA certifications to students.

## Scope of the Qualification

- Teach SCA Sensory Skills Foundation
- Teach SCA Sensory Skills Intermediate
- Teach SCA Sensory Skills Professional
- Issue official SCA certificates
- Assess student competency in sensory evaluation

## Prerequisites

- [SCA Sensory Skills](../tasting-coffee/sca-sensory-skills.md) Professional certification
- Extensive sensory experience (typically three to five years)
- Teaching aptitude and industry involvement
- Strong professional references

## What ASTs Teach

**Foundation Level:** Introduction to sensory science, basic taste and olfaction, [SCA Cupping Protocol](../coffee-tasting/sca-cupping-protocol.md) basics, [SCA Flavour Wheel](../tasting-coffee/sca-flavour-wheel.md) usage.

**Intermediate Level:** Advanced cupping skills, [Defect Recognition Training](../coffee-tasting/defect-recognition-training.md), sensory calibration, introduction to panel management.

**Professional Level:** Expert-level panel management, statistical analysis of sensory data, quality control system design, research methodology.

## Key Facts

- AST Sensory Analysis is one pathway within the broader Authorised SCA Trainer programme (see [AST_Certification](ast-certification.md))
- Only certified ASTs can issue official SCA Sensory Skills certificates to students
- Prerequisites include prior attainment of SCA Sensory Skills Professional level
- Related trainer certification: Q Instructor (Coffee Quality Institute)
