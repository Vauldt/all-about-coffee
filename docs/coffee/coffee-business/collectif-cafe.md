# Collectif café

## Overview

Collectif Café is a French professional federation representing the entire coffee chain from cherry to cup, with a particular focus on quality and specialty coffee. Founded in 1959, it brings together importers, roasters, baristas, and coffee shops under a shared commitment to high-quality coffee, ethical sourcing, and sustainable practice in the French market.

## Mission and Focus

Collectif Café is the primary professional federation for the French specialty coffee sector. Its activities include:
- Representing member interests across the full coffee value chain
- Promoting high-quality and specialty coffees through events including the Paris Coffee Show, Bordeaux Coffee Show, and national barista competitions
- Public communication advancing coffee quality awareness in France

## Ethics and Sustainability

The federation organises members around an ethical charter, emphasising:
- Fair and balanced commercial relationships with coffee producers
- Sustainable, locally anchored consumption practices
- Limiting environmental impact across both producing countries and France
- Socially responsible practices throughout the supply chain

## Position in the French Coffee Sector

Collectif Café represents the specialty-sector pillar within the broader professional coffee landscape in France. It operates alongside other industry bodies covering the industrial and commercial roasting sectors and is indirectly affected by global frameworks led by the International Coffee Organization (ICO).

## Key Facts

- Founded in 1959; France's primary specialty coffee federation
- Represents importers, roasters, baristas, and coffee shops
- Organises the Paris Coffee Show and Bordeaux Coffee Show
- Promotes ethical sourcing and sustainability through a member charter
- Acts as the specialty-sector voice alongside broader French coffee industry organisations
