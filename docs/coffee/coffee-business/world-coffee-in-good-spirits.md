## Overview

The World Coffee in Good Spirits Championship is a competition testing baristas' ability to create coffee-based alcoholic drinks, combining specialty coffee technique with bartending skills. Competitors present two coffee cocktails to sensory judges — one prepared with a specified spirit and one of their own design — demonstrating knowledge of coffee flavour pairing, extraction technique, and mixology. The competition reflects the growing intersection of specialty coffee culture and craft bartending.
