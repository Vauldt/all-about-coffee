## Overview

Coffee Review is an independent coffee rating publication founded by Kenneth Davids and Ron Walters in 1997, offering 100-point numerical scores and tasting notes for commercially available coffees alongside educational articles. It is one of the most widely cited third-party coffee evaluation sources in the English-speaking world, and its ratings carry significant commercial weight for specialty roasters whose coffees receive scores of 93 or above. The site's scoring methodology draws on SCA cupping principles but is applied by its own panel of tasters rather than certified Q Graders.
