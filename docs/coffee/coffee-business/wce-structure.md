## Overview

World Coffee Events (WCE) is the organisation jointly owned by the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) responsible for sanctioning and organising the World Barista Championship, World Brewers Cup, World Latte Art Championship, Cup Tasters Championship, Coffee Roasting Championship, and World Coffee in Good Spirits competitions. National SCA-affiliated member organisations run their own qualifying championship systems, with winners advancing to the WCE global finals held annually at rotating international locations.
