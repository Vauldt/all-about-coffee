## Overview

Latte art throwdowns are informal community events in which baristas compete to pour the best milk art design under time pressure and in front of an audience. Unlike the sanctioned World Latte Art Championship, throwdowns are typically low-key social events organised by cafés, roasters, or barista guilds, serving as a community-building and skill-building exercise within the specialty coffee scene. They lower the barrier to competition participation and have proven effective at introducing newer baristas to the competitive environment in a supportive, accessible setting.
