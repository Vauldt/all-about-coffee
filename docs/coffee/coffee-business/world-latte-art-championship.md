## Overview

The World Latte Art Championship is an annual competition organised by World Coffee Events, testing competitors' ability to create intricate, symmetrical poured milk art designs on espresso. Competitors are judged on visual creativity, symmetry, difficulty, and consistency across multiple pours, highlighting the craft dimension of barista work that is distinct from flavour or brewing skill. The championship has driven the global development of advanced milk-texturing technique and elevated latte art from a decorative extra to a recognised competitive discipline.
