## Overview

Perfect Daily Grind is a UK-based specialty coffee trade publication founded in 2014, producing daily articles, guides, and interviews covering all aspects of the coffee supply chain from producer to barista. It is widely read by coffee professionals globally and known for in-depth reporting on origin, processing, roasting, and café business topics. Perfect Daily Grind has expanded to include Spanish and Portuguese language editions, reflecting its relevance to coffee-producing country audiences as well as consuming markets.
