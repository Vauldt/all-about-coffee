## Overview

Standart is an independent specialty coffee magazine published quarterly, founded in Prague, Czech Republic. It focuses on the cultural, social, and humanistic dimensions of coffee rather than technical instruction, commissioning long-form essays, photography, and design pieces that explore coffee's relationship to place, identity, labour, and community — filling a niche between trade publications and consumer lifestyle media. Its distinctive visual design and literary tone have made it a collectible object as much as a trade resource.
