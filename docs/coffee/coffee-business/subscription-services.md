## Overview

Specialty coffee subscription services deliver freshly roasted coffee directly to consumers on a recurring schedule — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly — and were among the first direct-to-consumer e-commerce models in specialty coffee. Services such as Trade Coffee, Mistobox, and individual roastery subscriptions have accelerated home access to freshly roasted single-origins, allowing consumers to explore diverse origins and roast profiles without visiting a specialty café. Subscription volumes grew significantly during and after the COVID-19 pandemic as home brewing expanded and consumers sought alternatives to closed hospitality venues.
