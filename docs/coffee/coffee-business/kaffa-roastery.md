## Overview

Kaffa Roastery is a Finnish specialty coffee roaster based in Helsinki, founded in 2007 by Svante Hampf and Anne Hed; it is one of the pioneering Nordic roasters and helped establish Finland's specialty coffee culture through direct sourcing, light roasting, and strong competition involvement. Multiple Finnish Barista Championship titles have been won by Kaffa-affiliated baristas, cementing the roastery's position as a cornerstone of Nordic specialty coffee and a significant contributor to the region's global competition success.
