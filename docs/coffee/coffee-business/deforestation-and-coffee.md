## Overview

Deforestation for coffee cultivation is a significant environmental concern particularly in Ethiopia, Brazil, Vietnam, and parts of Indonesia, where forest clearance for new coffee plantations threatens biodiversity and releases stored carbon. Shade-grown and agroforestry coffee models, where coffee is grown under native tree cover, represent a lower-deforestation alternative that also benefits bird populations and soil health. The relationship between coffee expansion and forest loss is complex and varies by region, making blanket generalisations difficult and context-specific analysis essential.
