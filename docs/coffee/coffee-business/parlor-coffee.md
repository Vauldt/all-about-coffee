## Overview

Parlor Coffee is a Brooklyn, New York-based specialty coffee roaster notable for its quality-focused green coffee sourcing and intimate café environment. The roastery represents the growth of artisanal micro-roasters in dense urban markets, where small-batch roasting, direct trade relationships, and neighbourhood-scale retail have created a distinct counterpoint to larger specialty chains operating in major cities like New York.
