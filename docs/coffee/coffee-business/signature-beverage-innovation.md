## Overview

The signature beverage is the creative component of the World Barista Championship format, in which competitors design and serve an original espresso-based drink showcasing a specific coffee's character, incorporating additional ingredients — fruit, spices, botanicals, acids, fermented elements — in ways that highlight the coffee's flavour profile. Signature beverages have driven significant experimentation with processing methods, ingredient combinations, and espresso extraction techniques across the specialty industry, functioning as a research and development platform for the broader field.
