The **International Coffee Organization (ICO)** is the main intergovernmental organization for coffee, bringing together exporting and importing governments to address global coffee‑sector challenges through international cooperation.[web:20][web:25] Its members represent around 93% of world coffee production and a large majority of world consumption.[web:25]

## Mandate and functions

- Strengthens the global coffee sector and promotes its sustainable expansion in a market‑based environment, with the aim of improving outcomes for all participants.[web:20][web:25]  
- Provides a forum for dialogue among governments, private sector, and other stakeholders, while collecting and publishing official statistics on production, trade, and consumption.[web:25]

## Instruments and projects

- Administers the International Coffee Agreement, first concluded in 1962 and currently implemented under the ICA 2007, giving a framework for coffee‑sector cooperation.[web:20][web:25]  
- Supports the development and funding of technical cooperation projects and public‑private partnerships, often in collaboration with organizations such as the Common Fund for Commodities (CFC).[web:25]

## Relevance to France

- Sets part of the international context within which French organizations under the [CFC (French Coffee Council)](cfc-french-coffee-council.md) umbrella operate.[web:20][web:25]  
- Provides data and policy frameworks used by both industrial actors (e.g. ../Syndicat Français du Café) and specialty stakeholders (e.g. [Collectif café](collectif-cafe.md)).[web:11][web:18][web:25]
