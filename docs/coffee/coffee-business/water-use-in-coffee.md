## Overview

Coffee production is water-intensive at every stage: washed processing requires 30–45 litres of water per kilogram of green coffee produced, while a single espresso shot accounts for approximately 200 litres when the full production water footprint is included. Water scarcity is an increasing constraint on coffee-growing regions, particularly in Central America and parts of Ethiopia, where climate change is reducing rainfall reliability and groundwater levels. The specialty sector has driven adoption of eco-pulpers and water-recirculation systems that reduce wet-processing water use by up to 90%, and sustainability certifications increasingly include water management requirements.
