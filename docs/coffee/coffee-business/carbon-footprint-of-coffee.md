## Overview

The carbon footprint of coffee encompasses greenhouse gas emissions across the full supply chain — land use change at origin (deforestation), fertiliser production, harvesting and processing energy, international shipping, roasting, packaging, retail, and end-consumer brewing. Studies estimate that a kilogram of roasted coffee generates approximately 4–17 kg of CO2-equivalent, with land use and processing being the most significant emission sources. Understanding the carbon footprint of coffee is increasingly important for roasters and retailers communicating sustainability commitments to consumers.
