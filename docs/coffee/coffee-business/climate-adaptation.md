## Overview

Climate adaptation in coffee refers to strategies — at farm, sector, and policy levels — designed to maintain coffee production viability as climate change alters temperature ranges, rainfall patterns, and pest and disease pressure in growing regions. Key adaptation pathways include varietal replacement with climate-tolerant cultivars developed through World Coffee Research's F1 hybrid programme, altitudinal migration of cultivation to higher elevations, shade-tree agroforestry to moderate microclimate, and diversification of farmer income away from coffee monoculture. The specialty sector plays a role in adaptation funding through premium prices that provide capital for farm investment, and through direct sourcing relationships that create multi-year income stability enabling producers to plan and invest.
