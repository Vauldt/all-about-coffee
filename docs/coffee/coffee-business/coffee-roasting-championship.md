## Overview

The World Coffee Roasting Championship tests competitors' ability to roast coffee to a consistent standard, perform quality analysis on their roasted output, and create a roast profile for a provided green coffee within competition conditions. Organised by World Coffee Events, the championship highlights the technical craft of the roaster as a skilled profession requiring knowledge of green coffee assessment, heat application, and sensory evaluation, elevating roasting to the same competitive recognition as barista work.
