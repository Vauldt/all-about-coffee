# French Coffee Syndicate

---
 %%
The **Syndicat Français du Café** is a French trade association that brings together 16 companies producing and marketing roasted, soluble, and portioned coffee in France.[web:7][web:11] It is a central representative of the industrial and mainstream coffee segment in the French market.[web:11]

## Mission and activities

- Represents member companies in dealings with public authorities on regulation, labeling, food safety, and trade issues affecting coffee.[web:11]  
- Works to valorize members’ products, support their growth, and promote the economic importance of coffee consumption in France across retail and out‑of‑home channels.[web:11]

## Sustainability and responsibility

- Members commit to limiting environmental impact on French territory (including packaging and waste) and to addressing climate‑change challenges in coffee supply chains.[web:11][web:16]  
- The Syndicat promotes responsible consumption and monitors scientific research on coffee and health to inform communication and practices.[web:11][web:16]

## Position in the encyclopedia

- Treated as a core component of the [CFC (French Coffee Council)](cfc-french-coffee-council.md) umbrella for industrial and mainstream coffee in France.[web:7][web:11]  
- Complements the more specialty‑oriented [Collectif café](collectif-cafe.md), with both influenced by standards and initiatives linked to the International Coffee Organization (ICO) and Common Fund for Commodities (CFC).[web:11][web:20][web:25]

# Syndicat Français du Café

The **Syndicat Français du Café** (French Coffee Syndicate) is a French professional association that brings together companies involved in the roasting, solubilisation (instant coffee), and portion‑controlled preparation of coffee for the French market. It acts as a sectoral body advocating for the interests of its member companies and promoting responsible, sustainable coffee consumption in France and beyond.

## Overview

The Syndicat Français du Café gathers **16 member enterprises** that produce and commercialize roasted coffee, soluble (instant) coffee, and coffee in capsules or pods. [web:4][web:8] These companies range from small family‑run roasteries to large international groups, covering a broad spectrum of coffee formats and price points. [web:8] The syndicate focuses on the domestic market but also supports the export of its members’ products to other countries. [web:8]

## Role and Activities

The Syndicat Français du Café serves several key functions:

- **Representation and advocacy**: It represents its members vis‑à‑vis public authorities, regulators, and other stakeholders in France and the European Union. [web:8]  
- **Market promotion**: It promotes coffee consumption in France through campaigns and events, such as activities around the International Coffee Day and the hashtag **#JaimeMonCafé** aimed at highlighting home coffee ritual and equipment. [web:4][web:10]  
- **Knowledge sharing**: It publishes sectoral reports, such as its **Panorama RSE** (2024), which reviews members’ commitments to social and environmental responsibility across the coffee value chain. [web:4][web:17]  

## Sustainability and Responsibility

The syndicate emphasizes that its members are collectively committed to reducing the environmental footprint of coffee production and consumption and to encouraging responsible consumer behavior. [web:8][web:17] Key areas of engagement include:

- Addressing climate‑change challenges in coffee production and supply‑chain resilience. [web:17]  
- Limiting the impact of packaging, energy use, and waste on French territory. [web:8]  
- Supporting certifications and initiatives that promote environmental and social standards (e.g., fair remuneration, workers’ rights, and preservation of farming communities). [web:8][web:17]  

## Position within the French Coffee Landscape

The Syndicat Français du Café is distinct from the wider **Collectif Café**, which represents the broader French coffee ecosystem from “bean to cup,” including importers, roasters, baristas, and coffee‑shop owners. [web:5] The syndicate focuses more specifically on **industrial and semi‑industrial producers** of roasted, soluble, and portioned coffee, often operating in mass retail and out‑of‑home channels. [web:8]  

In recent years, the organization has also participated in crisis‑response efforts for the French coffee sector, including actions to secure recognition of coffee‑related businesses during periods of economic or health‑related disruption. [web:18]
