# Running a Cafe

A top-level Map of Content for café ownership and management. Each domain has its own subsidiary MoC with full coverage — use this file as the entry point and navigation hub.

## The Six Domains

Running a successful café requires competence across six interdependent domains. Weakness in any one of them limits what the others can achieve.

| Domain | Subsidiary MoC |

## Business Planning & Setup

Pre-opening work determines the ceiling of what a café can achieve operationally and financially. Decisions made before the first customer arrives — location, equipment, supplier relationships, and concept — are hard to reverse.

→ [Cafe Business Planning MoC](../running-a-cafe/cafe-business-planning-moc.md)

---

## Daily Operations

Consistent daily operations are the foundation of everything else. Quality, customer experience, and financial performance all depend on disciplined systems running the same way every shift.

→ [Cafe Operations MoC](../running-a-cafe/cafe-operations-moc.md)

---

## Staff & People Management

Staff are the business. Hiring well, training thoroughly, managing fairly, and developing people over time is the primary driver of both quality consistency and customer experience.

→ [Cafe Staff MoC](../running-a-cafe/cafe-staff-moc.md)

---

## Financial Management

A café that makes great coffee but loses money will not exist long enough to matter. Financial discipline — pricing correctly, controlling costs, and maintaining visibility over the numbers — is non-negotiable.

→ [Cafe Financial Management MoC](../running-a-cafe/cafe-financial-management-moc.md)

---

## Customer Experience

The product and the service are inseparable. Customers return because of how they feel, not just what they drank. Menu development, brand, and marketing extend that experience beyond the four walls of the shop.

→ [Cafe Customer Experience MoC](../running-a-cafe/cafe-customer-experience-moc.md)

## Growth, Quality & Scaling

Sustaining quality as volume grows, managing supply chain, and expanding without breaking what works requires deliberate systems and analytical discipline.
