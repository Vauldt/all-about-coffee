## Overview

The specialty coffee press encompasses trade publications, blogs, magazines, and digital media that serve the specialty coffee industry and its enthusiast audience. Key outlets include Sprudge (founded 2009, US-based news and culture), Perfect Daily Grind (UK-based trade content), Standart Magazine (cultural quarterly), Barista Magazine (trade), and Roast Magazine (roasting professionals), collectively forming the editorial infrastructure through which industry knowledge, trends, and debate circulate.
