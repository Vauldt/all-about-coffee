# James Hoffmann

## Overview

James Hoffmann is a British coffee professional, author, and educator who won the 2007 World Barista Championship and co-founded Square Mile Coffee Roasters in London. He is widely regarded as one of the most influential figures in contemporary specialty coffee, having shaped public understanding of the craft through his books, YouTube channel, and advocacy for evidence-based brewing education. His work consistently prioritises accessibility and scientific rigour over gatekeeping.

## Early Career and World Barista Championship

Hoffmann was born in 1980 in the United Kingdom and developed his career within the British specialty coffee scene during the mid-2000s. He competed at the 2007 [World Barista Championship](world-barista-championship.md) (WBC) representing the United Kingdom, winning the title with a routine that used a single-origin Ethiopian Yirgacheffe coffee. His presentation emphasised transparency about origin, terroir, and processing — themes that would define his broader career. The routine was notable for its focus on communicating coffee's agricultural origins rather than technical showmanship alone.

## Square Mile Coffee Roasters

In 2008, Hoffmann co-founded Square Mile Coffee Roasters in London with Annette Moldvaer. The company became one of the most respected specialty roasters in Europe, known for its focus on green coffee sourcing, light-to-medium roasting, and direct relationships with producers. Square Mile helped establish London as a serious hub of the global specialty coffee scene during the late 2000s and 2010s.

## The World Atlas of Coffee

In 2014, Hoffmann authored *The World Atlas of Coffee*, published by Mitchell Beazley. The book provides a comprehensive survey of coffee origins, processing methods, roasting, and brewing, and became one of the most widely read coffee references for both enthusiasts and professionals. A second edition was published in 2018 incorporating developments in specialty coffee, and a third edition followed in 2023. The atlas is co-credited on some editions as the *World Coffee Atlas* and is considered a foundational text in specialty coffee education.

## YouTube Channel and Public Education

Hoffmann launched his YouTube channel in 2014, and it grew substantially from 2019 onward to become one of the largest coffee education platforms globally, reaching millions of subscribers by 2023. The channel is characterised by systematic testing and scientific methodology applied to everyday brewing questions — equipment comparisons, recipe development, and technique analysis. Notable content includes blind espresso machine tests, analysis of the AeroPress World Championship, espresso recipe optimisation, and a collaboration with Whittard on a cafetière design. The channel is widely credited with democratising high-quality brewing information and making specialty coffee accessible to home brewers without prior professional training.

## Industry Contributions

Hoffmann was an early contributor and supporter of Barista Hustle, the online coffee education platform co-founded by Matt Perger, which expanded professional coffee science education globally. He has contributed to [Specialty Coffee Association (SCA)](../specialty-coffee/specialty-coffee-association-sca.md) education initiatives and served as a public commentator on industry trends, including discussions of sustainability, gender equity, and the commercial pressures facing specialty coffee businesses. His advocacy consistently positioned rigorous, open education as a counter to the gatekeeping tendencies sometimes found in specialty coffee communities.

## Key Facts

- Born 1980, United Kingdom
- Won the 2007 World Barista Championship representing the UK, using a single-origin Ethiopian Yirgacheffe
- Co-founded Square Mile Coffee Roasters in London in 2008 with Annette Moldvaer
- Authored *The World Atlas of Coffee* (2014; 2nd ed. 2018; 3rd ed. 2023), published by Mitchell Beazley
- YouTube channel launched 2014; reached millions of subscribers by 2023
- Known for rigorous, science-grounded coffee education aimed at home brewers and professionals alike
- Supporter and early contributor to Barista Hustle, co-founded by Matt Perger
