## Overview

Celebrity baristas are coffee professionals who have gained widespread recognition through competition success, social media, writing, or education. Figures such as James Hoffmann, Sasa Sestic, Agnieszka Rojewska, and Matt Perger have moved beyond the café to become global industry influencers whose techniques, opinions, and product recommendations shape the specialty market. Their visibility has elevated the professional status of baristas and demonstrated that coffee expertise can sustain careers in media, consultancy, equipment design, and roasting.
