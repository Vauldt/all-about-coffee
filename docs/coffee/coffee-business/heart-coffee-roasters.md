## Overview

Heart Coffee Roasters is a Portland, Oregon-based specialty roaster and café founded in 2009, known for precision roasting, clean single-origin coffees, and a minimalist café aesthetic. Heart is considered one of the defining roasters of the Portland third-wave scene alongside Stumptown, contributing to the city's reputation as a home of uncompromising specialty coffee quality and independent café culture.
