## Overview

Carbon neutrality initiatives in specialty coffee include roastery energy transition to renewable sources, carbon offset purchasing for unavoidable emissions — particularly international shipping — and sustainable packaging programmes. Supply chain measurement tools allow businesses to calculate and report their full carbon footprint across sourcing, roasting, and distribution. Several specialty roasters have published carbon neutrality commitments and third-party verified reports, with businesses such as Cafédirect and select Scandinavian roasteries among the early leaders in transparent carbon accounting.
