# Common Fund for Commodities

## Overview

The Common Fund for Commodities (CFC) is an intergovernmental financial institution that finances development projects in commodity-dependent countries, including projects in the coffee sector. Its focus is on improving value-chain performance, sustainability, and producer livelihoods across agricultural and natural commodity sectors globally.

## Role in Coffee

The CFC finances and supports projects aimed at making coffee value chains more transparent, sustainable, and inclusive — including initiatives on traceability and risk management. It works with producer organisations, governments, and private partners to pilot and scale innovative approaches in commodity sectors.

## Relation to Global Coffee Governance

The CFC often collaborates with the International Coffee Organization (ICO) to develop and implement coffee-related projects that complement the ICO's policy and convening role. Its interventions influence supply-chain practices in exporting countries. The organisation is separate from national trade bodies and regional federations such as those representing the French coffee sector.

## Key Facts

- Intergovernmental financial institution; supports development projects in commodity-dependent countries
- Coffee-sector projects include traceability, sustainability, and value-chain efficiency initiatives
- Collaborates with the ICO on complementary programmes; works with producer organisations and governments
- Interventions primarily target the producing-country side of the supply chain
