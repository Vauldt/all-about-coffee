# Coffee Writer / Journalist / Blogger

A **Coffee Writer**, **Coffee Journalist**, or **Coffee Blogger** produces written content that informs, educates, and engages audiences on the subject of coffee. Coverage ranges from investigative industry journalism and academic-style longform writing to accessible consumer guides, recipe content, and opinionated criticism.

## Role Overview

Writing about coffee spans a broad spectrum of publication contexts — trade magazines, consumer lifestyle media, independent newsletters, personal blogs, brand content studios, and book publishing. Some coffee writers are embedded journalists covering the industry as a beat; others are coffee industry insiders who have transitioned into writing as a primary or secondary vocation. The rise of independent publishing platforms has made it possible to build a sustainable audience without institutional affiliation.

## Key Responsibilities

- Researching and writing articles, features, reviews, and guides on coffee topics
- Interviewing producers, roasters, baristas, scientists, and industry leaders
- Reviewing cafés, roasters, brewing equipment, and coffee products
- Producing educational content such as how-to guides, explainers, and glossaries
- Maintaining a blog, newsletter (e.g., via [Substack](https://substack.com) or [Ghost](https://ghost.org)), or social media presence
- Pitching and placing stories with editors at trade and consumer publications
- Collaborating with brands on commissioned content and copywriting projects
- Covering live events such as competitions, expos, and origin trips

## Key Publications and Platforms

Some of the most prominent coffee-specific media outlets include:

- [Sprudge](https://sprudge.com) — leading independent specialty coffee news and culture publication
- [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com) — trade-focused news, education, and longform features
- [Standart Magazine](https://standartmag.com) — independent quarterly print magazine
- [Barista Magazine](https://baristamagazine.com) — trade publication covering competition, craft, and culture
- [Roast Magazine](https://roastmagazine.com) — focused on the roasting profession
- [Daily Coffee News by Roast](https://dailycoffeenews.com) — industry news wire

## Skills and Knowledge

- Strong writing craft — clarity, structure, voice, and editorial discipline
- Deep coffee knowledge across the supply chain: Coffee Origins, Coffee Processing, [Coffee Roasting](../coffee-roasting/coffee-roasting.md), [Coffee Brewing](../coffee-brewing/coffee-brewing.md), and Coffee Sensory Evaluation
- Research and interview skills
- SEO and digital publishing knowledge for online content roles
- Familiarity with editorial workflows, style guides, and fact-checking practices
- Photography or visual content skills are advantageous for multimedia roles

## Qualifications and Pathways

- Degree in Journalism, Communications, English, or Creative Writing (beneficial but not mandatory)
- Portfolio of published or self-published writing is the primary credential
- Coffee industry experience — barista, roaster, buyer — lends credibility and deep subject knowledge
- [SCA Coffee Skills Programme](https://sca.coffee/education) certification demonstrates formal coffee knowledge
- Participation in origin trips, competitions, and industry events generates firsthand material

## Industry Context

The specialty coffee media landscape is relatively small but highly engaged. Trade publications like [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com) and [Sprudge](https://sprudge.com) command loyal readerships among industry professionals globally. Independent writers have found success through direct-to-audience newsletter models, building subscriber bases around niche topics — from green coffee sourcing to home brewing technique. Coffee book publishing has also seen growth, with titles spanning brew guides, origin narratives, and histories of coffee culture finding mainstream audiences through publishers like [Chronicle Books](https://www.chroniclebooks.com) and [Reaktion Books](https://www.reaktionbooks.co.uk).
