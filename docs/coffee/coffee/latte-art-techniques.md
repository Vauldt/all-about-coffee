# Latte Art Techniques

Summary:: Latte art is the visible expression of well-textured milk and correctly extracted espresso.

- Foundations:
  - Espresso with stable crema.
  - Milk steamed to glossy, fine microfoam with good integration.
- Pouring:
  - Control height, speed, and position to create contrast and shape.
  - Basic patterns: Heart, tulip, rosetta; built from the same core movements.
- Role: Acts as a quick check on milk texture and shot quality as well as aesthetics.
