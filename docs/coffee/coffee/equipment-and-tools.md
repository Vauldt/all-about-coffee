## Overview

Equipment and tools in coffee brewing encompasses the full range of machines and instruments used across grinding, brewing, and serving. The core tools for any quality home setup are a burr grinder, a digital scale, and a brewing device; professional environments extend this to espresso machines, batch brewers, and measurement instruments. Consistent, well-maintained equipment improves repeatability and quality control at every stage of preparation.

## Key Tools

- **Grinder:** A burr grinder is the single most critical tool — it determines particle size distribution and extraction consistency
- **Scale:** A digital scale (minimum 0.1 g resolution) enables precise dose and yield measurement; volumetric measurement is unreliable for specialty coffee
- **Brewer:** Espresso machine, pour-over dripper, French press, AeroPress, or batch brewer depending on the brewing method
- **Water control:** A variable-temperature kettle or thermometer ensures water temperature is within the target range for the brew method
- **Water filtration:** Removes chlorine and scale-forming minerals; improves flavour and protects equipment

For a comprehensive breakdown of equipment by category and stage, see [Equipment](../coffee-equipment/equipment.md).

## Key Facts

- A quality burr grinder and a digital scale typically provide the largest improvement in home brewing consistency
- Blade grinders produce unacceptably variable particle sizes and are unsuitable for specialty coffee
- Regular cleaning and periodic calibration maintain equipment performance and prevent flavour contamination
- Espresso requires the highest equipment investment of any brewing method — both machine and grinder quality are critical
