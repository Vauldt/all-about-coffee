## Overview

Coffee culture encompasses the social, psychological, and economic dimensions of how coffee is consumed, ritualised, and represented across societies. The questions addressed in the Coffeepedia coffee culture domain range from individual daily habits and identity to café economics, regional traditions, sustainability ethics, and the relationship between coffee and community.
