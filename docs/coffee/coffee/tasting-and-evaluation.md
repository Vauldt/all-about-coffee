# Tasting and Evaluation

Summary:: Systematic tasting closes the loop between brewing variables and flavor outcomes.

- Methods: Cupping, side-by-side comparisons, single-variable tests.
- Attributes: Aroma, acidity, sweetness, bitterness, body, clarity, aftertaste.
- Use: Identify under/over-extraction and guide future recipe adjustments.
