⬆️ [Dictionary of Coffee Drinks](../coffee-beverages/dictionary-of-coffee-drinks.md)

**Definition:** A black coffee made by pouring one or more shots of espresso over hot water, preserving crema and aroma.

- **Category:** Espresso-based black coffee  
    
- **Origin:** Australia & New Zealand café culture[sageappliances+1](https://www.sageappliances.com/uk/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/long-black-vs-americano.html)​  
    
- **Brew method:** Espresso over hot water  
    
- **Brew ratio:** Roughly 1 part espresso : 2–3 parts water[sageappliances](https://www.sageappliances.com/uk/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/long-black-vs-americano.html)​  
    
- **Water temp:** Hot water just off the boil; ~65–75 °C in cup  
    
- **Serving size:** ~150–180 ml[alliancecoffee](https://www.alliancecoffee.sg/long-black-vs-americano/)​  
    
- **Texture/body:** Medium body with visible crema layer  
    
- **Flavour notes:** Bold, aromatic, more intense than Americano; clear espresso acidity and bittersweet finish[drinkmorning+1](https://www.drinkmorning.com/blogs/the-morning-journal/americano-vs-long-black-vs-lungo-whats-the-difference)​  
    
- **Strength:** Medium-strong  
    
- **Typical use:** House black in Australian/NZ specialty cafés
