# How to Make a Mocha

A mocha is made by combining espresso, chocolate, and steamed milk to create a coffee drink that is both rich and sweet. It is often built like a latte, but with cocoa powder, chocolate syrup, or drinking chocolate added to the espresso before the milk is poured.

## Basic ingredients

- 1 to 2 shots of espresso, or strong coffee
- 1 to 2 tablespoons of cocoa powder, drinking chocolate, chocolate syrup, or melted chocolate
- About 6 to 8 ounces of milk
- Optional whipped cream, chocolate shavings, or cocoa dusting

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Brew the espresso.** Pull a single or double espresso into your cup. See [BBC Good Food's mocha recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/mocha), [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-mocha), and [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/au/recipes/caffe-mocha).
2. **Add the chocolate.** Stir cocoa powder, drinking chocolate, syrup, or melted chocolate into the espresso until fully dissolved and smooth. See [BBC Good Food's mocha recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/mocha), [Seven Miles](https://www.sevenmiles.com.au/blogs/editorial/3-ways-to-make-a-mocha), and [Home Cafe by Charlie](https://homecafebycharlie.com/make-a-perfect-cafe-mocha-3-ways/).
3. **Steam the milk.** Steam the milk until it is hot and silky, with a light foam similar to a latte. See [BBC Good Food's mocha recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/mocha), [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-mocha), and this [mocha video recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M4bqjmdCwRY).
4. **Pour and finish.** Pour the milk into the chocolate espresso mixture and top with whipped cream or chocolate if you like. See [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-mocha), [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/au/recipes/caffe-mocha), and [Seven Miles](https://www.sevenmiles.com.au/blogs/editorial/3-ways-to-make-a-mocha).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make strong coffee.** Brew a concentrated coffee using a moka pot, AeroPress, instant espresso, or another strong brew method. See [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/23538/mocha-coffee/), [Baking Mischief](https://bakingmischief.com/easy-homemade-mocha/), and [Moms of The Future](https://momsofthefuture.com/food/how-to-make-mocha-at-home-without-a-machine/).
2. **Build the chocolate base.** Mix cocoa powder or chocolate syrup with a little hot water, milk, or the coffee itself so the chocolate dissolves smoothly. See [Delicious Meets Healthy](https://www.deliciousmeetshealthy.com/mocha-coffee/), [Baking Mischief](https://bakingmischief.com/easy-homemade-mocha/), and [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/23538/mocha-coffee/).
3. **Heat and froth the milk.** Warm the milk until hot but not boiling, then froth it with a whisk, frother, French press, or jar method. See [Fork in the Kitchen](https://www.forkinthekitchen.com/homemade-mocha-latte/), [Homemade & Yummy](https://homemadeandyummy.com/chocolate-milk-latte/), and [Thrifty Foods](https://www.thriftyfoods.com/articles/how-to-make-a-cafe-mocha).
4. **Assemble the drink.** Combine the coffee and chocolate base, add the hot milk, then spoon any foam on top. See [Fork in the Kitchen](https://www.forkinthekitchen.com/homemade-mocha-latte/), [Baking Mischief](https://bakingmischief.com/easy-homemade-mocha/), and [Homemade & Yummy](https://homemadeandyummy.com/chocolate-milk-latte/).

## Quick tips

- For a more cafe-style mocha, use espresso and drinking chocolate rather than regular hot chocolate powder. See [Seven Miles](https://www.sevenmiles.com.au/blogs/editorial/3-ways-to-make-a-mocha) and [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-mocha).
- If using cocoa powder, mix it thoroughly to avoid lumps before adding the milk. See [Delicious Meets Healthy](https://www.deliciousmeetshealthy.com/mocha-coffee/) and [Baking Mischief](https://bakingmischief.com/easy-homemade-mocha/).
- Whole milk gives a creamier texture, but oat and other milk alternatives can also work well in a mocha. See [Fork in the Kitchen](https://www.forkinthekitchen.com/homemade-mocha-latte/) and [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/au/recipes/caffe-mocha).
