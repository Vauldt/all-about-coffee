# Why use single dosing with zero retention

Single dosing with a (near) zero‑retention grinder is mainly about maximum freshness, precise dosing, and repeatable extractions, especially when you change beans often.[^1_1][^1_2][^1_3][^1_4]

## What single dosing is

- Single dosing means weighing the exact beans for one shot or brew, putting only that into the grinder, and grinding immediately before use.[^1_2][^1_5]
- Beans are stored in airtight containers instead of sitting in a hopper exposed to oxygen, light, and heat, which slows staling.[^1_5][^1_1]


## Why pair it with zero retention

- Zero (or ultra‑low) retention means what you put in comes out, with no or minimal grounds left in the grinder’s “dead space.”[^1_3][^1_2]
- This avoids yesterday’s stale coffee mixing into today’s shot and makes dose‑in ≈ dose‑out highly accurate for dialing in.[^1_6][^1_3]


## Practical benefits in the cup

- **Freshness**: Every dose is ground on demand with no old grounds contaminating flavor, which improves aroma, flavor clarity, and crema, particularly in espresso.[^1_4][^1_7][^1_1][^1_6]
- **Consistency**: Weighing each dose and eliminating retained grounds gives more predictable extractions and more consistent shot times/taste from pull to pull.[^1_8][^1_3][^1_4]


## Workflow and flexibility gains

- Easy bean switching: You can move between different coffees (e.g., espresso vs filter, light vs dark) without purging or wasting beans, because nothing is left behind in the grinder.[^1_9][^1_4][^1_6]
- Less waste and mess: No need to purge grams of coffee to clear retention; you only grind what you drink, saving money on pricier specialty coffees.[^1_10][^1_4][^1_6]


## When it makes most sense

- Particularly suited to home or low‑volume prosumer setups where you:
    - Pull a few shots a day, not continuous service.[^1_4][^1_5]
    - Regularly switch coffees and want accurate side‑by‑side comparisons.
- In high‑volume café service, hopper‑fed grinders can still be preferable for speed and throughput, at the cost of some freshness and flexibility.[^1_11][^1_4]
