# Grinder Zero Retention

In coffee, “zero retention” refers to grinder designs that are intended to leave virtually no ground coffee trapped inside the grinder after each use, so the weight of coffee out matches the weight of coffee in and no old grounds contaminate the next dose. 

In practice, it usually means extremely low retention (often under about 0.1–0.5 g) rather than literally zero, and is particularly valued for single‑dosing where beans are weighed per shot and users want maximum freshness and accurate dosing without purging.

Because of this retention, allbeit small, coffee grinders need to be regularly cleaned to remove the buildup of stale coffee grounds.
