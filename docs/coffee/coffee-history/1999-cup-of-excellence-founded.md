## Overview

The Cup of Excellence was co-founded in 1999 in Brazil by George Howell and Susie Spindler in collaboration with the Brazilian Specialty Coffee Association (BSCA), establishing the first national quality competition and online auction for specialty coffee lots. It became the most influential quality-discovery and price-premium mechanism in the global specialty market, and has since expanded to over 20 producing countries.
