## Overview

The popularisation of espresso-based drinks — cappuccinos, lattes, macchiatos, and flavoured variations — in the English-speaking world is a central feature of the second wave, driven primarily by Starbucks' rapid expansion from the late 1980s onward. Before Starbucks, espresso drinks were largely confined to Italian-American communities and a few specialist cafés; Starbucks transformed them into everyday items and built a global consumer vocabulary around espresso-based café culture.
