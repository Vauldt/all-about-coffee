## Overview

The 2020s marked a decisive shift in the specialty coffee movement toward sustainability as a central value, driven by escalating evidence of climate change impacts on growing regions, the COVID-19 disruptions of 2020–2022 which exposed supply chain fragility, and growing consumer and investor pressure for credible environmental and social accountability. Major roasters and importers began issuing public transparency reports, setting science-based carbon reduction targets, and engaging with regenerative agriculture programmes at origin — moves that paralleled the broader ESG investment trend but also reflected genuine supply chain risk management. This period is associated with debates about whether sustainability-focused values constitute a "fourth wave" of coffee or represent the maturation of third-wave principles.
