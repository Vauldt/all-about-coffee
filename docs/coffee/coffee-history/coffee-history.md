## Overview

Coffee's history spans more than a millennium, from its legendary 9th-century Ethiopian origins through 15th-century Arabian cultivation, 17th-century European coffeehouse culture, the commodity era of the first wave in the early 20th century, the coffeehouse renaissance of the second wave (1966–2000), and the quality-focused third wave (2000s–present). Each era is covered in its own dedicated article; this note provides an entry point into the history section. For detailed coverage of origins through the pre-industrial era, see [Origins of Coffee](origins-of-coffee.md). For the wave eras, see [First Wave Coffee](first-wave-coffee.md), [Second Wave Coffee](second-wave-coffee.md), and [Third Wave Coffee](third-wave-coffee.md).
