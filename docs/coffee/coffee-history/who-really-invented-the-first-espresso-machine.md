# Who really invented the first espresso machine

The most detailed, debate-aware treatment of this question is a multi-part research series from Barista Hustle titled **“Who really invented the first espresso machine?”**, which argues that the key conceptual inventor is the little-known Frenchman Edouard-Léonard Kessel (patent 1878), with later practical and commercial breakthroughs by Angelo Moriondo (1884), Luigi Bezzera (1901) and Desiderio Pavoni (1903–05).[^1][^2][^3]

## Core specialist articles to read

- **Barista Hustle – Part 1: Who really invented the first espresso machine?**

Deep dive into Moriondo’s 1884 Turin patent and how it prefigured Bezzera and Pavoni; includes patent analysis, contemporary sources and historiographical issues.[^1]
URL: https://www.baristahustle.com/research-papers/part-1-who-really-invented-the-first-espresso-machine/[^1]

- **Barista Hustle – Part 4: Who really invented the first espresso machine?**

Concludes that Kessel’s 1878 patent already contains the essential “espresso principle” (pressurised, on‑demand brewing), and weighs how far a purely patented but possibly unbuilt design can count as the “first espresso machine.”[^2]
URL: https://www.baristahustle.com/research-papers/part-4-who-really-invented-the-first-espresso-machine/[^2]


## Contextual history pieces

- **Smithsonian Magazine – “The Long History of the Espresso Machine”**
Accessible overview tracing the line from Moriondo’s 1884 bulk brewer through Bezzera and Pavoni’s early 1900s bar machines, framed as a technological and cultural history.[^4]
URL: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/the-long-history-of-the-espresso-machine-126012814/[^4]

- **Perfect Daily Grind – “A history of the espresso machine” (2024)**
Summarises recent scholarship, emphasising Moriondo’s use of pressure as the decisive step toward modern espresso, then the later refinements and industrialisation.[^5][^6]
URL: https://perfectdailygrind.com/2024/06/a-history-of-the-espresso-machine/[^5]

- **Coffeeness – “History of the Espresso Machine: A Long and Winding Road” (2026)**

Short, modern recap that gives Moriondo primacy for the original bar machine (1884) but credits Bezzera and Pavoni with the first commercially successful designs from 1901 onward.[^7]
URL: https://www.coffeeness.de/en/history-of-the-espresso-machine/[^7]


## Biographical / reference entries

- **Wikipedia – “Espresso machine”**
Neutral reference outlining the standard narrative: Moriondo’s 1884 “instantaneous” machine; Bezzera’s 1901 improvements; Pavoni’s industrial production; later technological generations.[^3]
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espresso_machine[^3]

- **Wikipedia – “Angelo Moriondo”**
Focused biography with primary patent details and exposition context; useful for confirming dates, patent title wording, and the “bulk brewer vs single customer” distinction.[^8]

URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelo_Moriondo[^8]
