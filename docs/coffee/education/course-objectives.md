By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

1. Explain how green coffee characteristics influence roast outcomes
2. Describe the physical and chemical processes occurring during roasting
3. Identify key roasting stages and roast levels
4. Compare roasting equipment and roast profiles
5. Evaluate roast quality using sensory and analytical techniques
6. Identify and correct common roasting defects
7. Design a basic roast profile for a given coffee
