# How to Make a Cortado

A cortado is made by combining espresso with a small amount of warm steamed milk to reduce acidity while keeping the coffee flavour strong and clear. It is usually built around an equal ratio of espresso and milk, and is often served in a small glass of about 4 ounces.

## Basic ingredients

- 2 shots of espresso, or another concentrated coffee
- About 2 ounces of steamed milk
- Optional small amount of microfoam
- Optional sugar, though many people drink it plain

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Brew the espresso.** Pull a double espresso into a small glass or cup. See [BBC Good Food's cortado recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cortado), [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/recipes/cortado-recipe-ndg), and [Methodical Coffee](https://methodicalcoffee.com/blogs/coffee-culture/what-is-a-cortado-and-how-do-you-make-one).
2. **Steam the milk lightly.** Heat the milk so it becomes smooth and warm, with only a light amount of foam rather than a thick frothy cap. See [Emily Laurae](https://emilylaurae.com/how-to-make-a-cortado/), [Café Barista](https://cafebarista.ca/en/blogs/coffee/how-to-make-a-cortado), and this [cortado video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qkxKUQPacAs).
3. **Keep the ratio balanced.** A cortado is commonly made with equal parts espresso and milk, though some cafés use slightly more milk. See [BBC Good Food's cortado recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cortado), [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/recipes/cortado-recipe-ndg), [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-cortado), and [Café Barista](https://cafebarista.ca/en/blogs/coffee/how-to-make-a-cortado).
4. **Pour and serve.** Pour the steamed milk into the espresso so the drink blends smoothly without becoming overly foamy. See [Emily Laurae](https://emilylaurae.com/how-to-make-a-cortado/), [Methodical Coffee](https://methodicalcoffee.com/blogs/coffee-culture/what-is-a-cortado-and-how-do-you-make-one), and [Catholic Coffee](https://www.catholiccoffee.com/blogs/catholic-coffee-blog/cortado-the-perfect-balance-of-espresso-and-milk-e2-98-95).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make strong coffee.** Use a moka pot or AeroPress to brew a concentrated coffee if you do not have an espresso machine. See [Methodical Coffee](https://methodicalcoffee.com/blogs/coffee-culture/what-is-a-cortado-and-how-do-you-make-one) and [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-cortado).
2. **Heat the milk.** Warm the milk until hot but not boiling. See [Emily Laurae](https://emilylaurae.com/how-to-make-a-cortado/) and [Catholic Coffee](https://www.catholiccoffee.com/blogs/catholic-coffee-blog/cortado-the-perfect-balance-of-espresso-and-milk-e2-98-95).
3. **Froth very lightly.** Use a frother, whisk, or French press to make silky milk with minimal foam. See [Emily Laurae](https://emilylaurae.com/how-to-make-a-cortado/) and [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-cortado).
4. **Assemble the drink.** Pour equal parts coffee and milk into a small cup or Gibraltar-style glass. See [BBC Good Food's cortado recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cortado), [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/recipes/cortado-recipe-ndg), and [Emily Laurae](https://emilylaurae.com/how-to-make-a-cortado/).

## Quick tips

- A cortado should be smaller and less milky than a latte, but softer than a straight espresso. See [Catholic Coffee](https://www.catholiccoffee.com/blogs/catholic-coffee-blog/cortado-the-perfect-balance-of-espresso-and-milk-e2-98-95) and [Canal Coffee](https://canalcoffeecompany.com/cortado-vs-latte-whats-the-real-difference/).
- Keep the milk smooth and lightly textured, not airy like a cappuccino. See [Emily Laurae](https://emilylaurae.com/how-to-make-a-cortado/), [Café Barista](https://cafebarista.ca/en/blogs/coffee/how-to-make-a-cortado), and this [Breville-style home cortado video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kQHJ8JLd_Ys).
- A small 4 ounce glass is a common serving style for a classic cortado. See [BBC Good Food's cortado recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cortado), [Canal Coffee](https://canalcoffeecompany.com/cortado-vs-latte-whats-the-real-difference/), and [Starbucks At Home](https://www.starbucksathome.com/recipes/cortado-recipe-ndg).
