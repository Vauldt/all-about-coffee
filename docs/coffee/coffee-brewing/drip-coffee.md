## Overview

Drip coffee is any coffee prepared by the gravity-driven drip method — hot water poured or dispensed over ground coffee held in a filter, with the brewed liquid collecting below. It is among the most widely consumed coffee styles globally, encompassing both automatic drip machines and manual pour-over preparations.
