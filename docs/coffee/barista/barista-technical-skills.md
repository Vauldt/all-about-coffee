# Barista Technical Skills

Technical skills are the physical craft of coffee preparation — espresso extraction, milk work, manual brewing, and equipment operation. They form the most visible part of barista competence and the foundation on which all other skills are built. See Barista Skills /Barista Skills Development MOC for the full framework.

## Espresso Mastery

**Foundation:** Dosing Technique | Distribution Methods (WDT, Stockfleth, tapping) | Tamping Pressure | Portafilter Preparation

**Development:** Visual Extraction Cues | Timing Consistency | Yield Accuracy | Grind Size Impact

**Advanced:** Extraction Diagnosis | Troubleshooting Channelling | [Pressure Profiling](../coffee-equipment/pressure-profiling.md) | Temperature Surfing

**Mastery:** Competition Espresso | Espresso Innovation | Equipment Modification | Signature Espresso

---

## Milk Work Mastery

**Foundation:** Steam Wand Positioning | Aerating Technique | Temperature Control (60–65°C) | Basic Texture

**Development:** Texture Refinement | Alternative Milk Techniques (oat, soy, almond) | Pour Control | Heart Pouring

**Advanced:** Rosetta Pouring | Tulip Pouring | Free Pour Artistry | [Milk Science](../coffee-science/milk-science.md)

**Mastery:** Competition Latte Art | 3D Latte Art | Etching Techniques | Instructional Ability
