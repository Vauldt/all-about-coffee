# Barista Service and Leadership Skills

Technical excellence creates great coffee; service and leadership skills create great café experiences. As baristas progress, their role expands from personal execution to team development, standards setting, and customer experience design. See Barista Skills /Barista Skills Development MOC for the full framework.

## Customer Service Excellence

**Foundation:** Professional Appearance | Positive Attitude | Active Listening | Clear Communication | Patience

**Development:** Reading Customers | Personalisation | Upselling Appropriately | Building Regulars | Conflict De-escalation

**Advanced:** Service Recovery | Experience Creation | Community Building | Customer Education

**Team communication:** Clear Direction | Constructive Feedback | Active Collaboration | Conflict Resolution
