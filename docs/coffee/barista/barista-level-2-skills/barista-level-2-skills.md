# Barista Level 2 Skills

## Barista Level 2 Skills

With the fundamentals in place, this Intermediate level takes you deeper into the science of espresso. You will learn to analyse extraction variables, diagnose and correct quality issues, produce consistent milk texture and latte art, and manage café workflow under pressure. 

This is widely considered the **employability threshold** for professional café roles, and it marks the transition from following recipes to truly understanding why they work.
