## Overview

A barista is a trained coffee professional responsible for preparing and serving espresso-based and other coffee drinks in a café or commercial setting. The term originates from Italian, where *barista* refers to a bartender or bar worker of any kind; in the English-speaking specialty coffee world, it has come to mean specifically a skilled coffee preparer. The role encompasses technical skills (espresso calibration, milk texturing, grinder operation, recipe consistency) and service skills (customer communication, speed of service, product knowledge), and at the highest levels involves competition, sensory evaluation, and origin knowledge.

## Core Barista Skills

**Espresso preparation:**
- Dosing and grinding: understanding dose, grind adjustment, and ratio targets
- Tamping: even, consistent tamping to produce uniform puck resistance
- Shot timing and evaluation: monitoring extraction time, yield, and visual character
- Machine maintenance: back-flushing, portafilter cleaning, group head care

**Milk texturing:**
- Steaming milk to produce microfoam for latte art and milk drinks
- Controlling temperature (60–65°C) for sweetness and texture
- Producing appropriate texture for different drinks (flat white vs. cappuccino)

**Calibration and consistency:**
- Dialling in grind setting to achieve target extraction time and yield
- Maintaining recipe consistency across a service period as grind setting shifts with temperature and humidity
- Tasting and adjusting espresso throughout service

**Product knowledge:**
- Coffee origins, processing methods, roast profiles
- Brewing parameters and their effects
- Menu items and customer communication

## SCA Barista Education

The Specialty Coffee Association offers structured barista education through its Coffee Skills Programme:
- **Barista Skills Foundation:** Entry-level; espresso and milk fundamentals
- **Barista Skills Intermediate:** Calibration, sensory skills, customer service
- **Barista Skills Professional:** Advanced calibration, competition standards, business context

The World Barista Championship (WBC) and World Latte Art Championship are the flagship international competitions, drawing competitors from over 60 countries.

## Key Facts

- Barista originates from Italian for bartender; in specialty coffee, it denotes a trained coffee preparer
- Core skills: espresso preparation, milk texturing, grinder calibration, consistency across service
- SCA Barista Skills Programme offers three levels (Foundation, Intermediate, Professional)
- World Barista Championship is the international pinnacle of competitive barista performance
- The specialty coffee movement has elevated the barista from a service role to a recognised skilled craft
