## Overview

The 4:6 Method is a structured pour-over brewing recipe developed by Tetsu Kasuya and used to win the 2016 World Brewers Cup. The full description of the method's principles and parameters — including its division of the total water into a 40% flavour-setting phase and a 60% strength-setting phase, each poured in multiple increments — is covered in [Tetsu Kasuya 4-6 Method](tetsu-kasuya-4-6-method.md).
