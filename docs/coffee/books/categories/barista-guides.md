*Category 3 of 4 | [Back to index](../Readme.md)*

---

## Overview

Craft-focused titles bridge theoretical knowledge and the physical reality of the cafe bar. Scott Rao's professional handbooks remain the de facto training texts for baristas in specialty coffee globally. Moldvaer's visually led reference and Colonna-Dashwood's dictionary provide complementary resources suited to both new learners and seasoned professionals.
