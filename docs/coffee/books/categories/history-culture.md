---

## Overview

Coffee is one of history's most consequential commodities -- shaping empires, financing revolutions, and rewiring social life across continents. The books here move between macro (Pendergrast's definitive global arc) and micro (Eggers' intimate portrait of a Yemeni-American coffee entrepreneur). Together they reveal that every cup carries centuries of human drama.
