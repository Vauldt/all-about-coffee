# Brewing Science

*Category 1 of 4 | [Back to index](../Readme.md)*

---

## Overview

Brewing science books sit at the intersection of culinary art and empirical investigation. The titles in this category span the accessible -- Hoffmann's approachable home-brewing bible -- to the intensely technical, including Gagne's astrophysics-informed filter coffee treatise. Whether you brew on a Chemex or calibrate a full espresso set-up, these titles will deepen your understanding of extraction, chemistry, and the variables that separate a good cup from an extraordinary one.
