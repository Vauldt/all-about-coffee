## Overview

The specialty coffee industry has matured into a global profession with its own economics, ethics, and career pathways. Colin Harmon's business guide is required reading for anyone considering opening a cafe; Scott Rao's updated roasting practices monograph reflects the industry's data-driven evolution; and Lonely Planet's tour offers a globe-spanning coffee-culture itinerary for the curious traveller.
