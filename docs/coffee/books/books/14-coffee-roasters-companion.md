## Description

The Coffee Roaster's Companion is widely regarded as the most important book ever written about coffee roasting. Rao draws on decades of consulting experience to provide a rigorous framework for understanding and controlling the roasting process -- from green coffee evaluation through roast curve analysis to sensory assessment. The discussion of Rate of Rise management and common roasting defects is particularly valuable and has shaped how the industry talks about roast quality. At around 90 pages (dense with charts and data), it is compact but comprehensive. Originally priced at $45, it has become the de facto textbook for professional roaster training programs worldwide.

---

## Key Topics

- Roast chemistry: Maillard reactions, caramelisation, and pyrolysis
- Roast profiling and Rate of Rise (ROR) management
- Common defects: baked coffee, scorching, ROR crashes
- Green coffee evaluation and sourcing
- Sensory evaluation and cupping for roasters
- Drum roaster operation and airflow management
