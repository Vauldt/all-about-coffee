## Description

Craft Coffee is the manual that treats home brewing with the seriousness it deserves without ever becoming snobby. Written by a coffee enthusiast rather than a industry insider, it focuses exclusively on manual filter brewing (not espresso), covering ten different devices including the V60, Chemex, AeroPress, and French press. Each method gets its own chapter with theory, parameters, and reproducible recipes. The book also demystifies bean selection, equipment, and the language of coffee bags. Named a top food and drink book of 2017 by Food Network, Wired, Sprudge, and Booklist. The author's follow-up, *How to Taste Coffee* (2023), is also well regarded for developing sensory skills.

## Key Topics

- Introduction to specialty coffee and bean selection
- The science of coffee extraction
- 10 manual brewing methods with step-by-step recipes
- Equipment selection and grinder basics
- Reading and deciphering coffee bag labels
- Flavour development and sensory fundamentals
