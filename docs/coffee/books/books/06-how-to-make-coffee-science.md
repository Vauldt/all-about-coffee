## Description

Lani Kingston's approach to coffee literature is distinctive: she leads with science and backs it with practical technique. The book explains the chemistry of coffee -- acids, sugars, volatile compounds -- alongside the physics of extraction and the biology of flavour perception. Crucially, the science never overwhelms the practical content; the book is beautifully illustrated and accessible to readers without a scientific background. It is shorter and more focused than Hoffmann's atlas, making it an excellent entry point or a concise gift for someone curious about why coffee tastes the way it does.

## Key Topics

- Coffee chemistry: acids, sugars, and compounds
- Physics and thermodynamics of brewing
- Roasting science and Maillard reactions
- Illustrated step-by-step brewing techniques
- Sensory science and flavour perception
