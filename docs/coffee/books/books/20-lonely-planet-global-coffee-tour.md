## Description

Lonely Planet's Global Coffee Tour is a collaboration between Lonely Planet's editorial team and the global specialty coffee community. It surveys the best cafes and coffee culture in 38 countries, with neighbourhood-level guides for major coffee cities, profiles of regional brewing traditions, and itineraries for coffee-focused travel. The scope is genuinely global -- including origin countries in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia alongside consuming markets. Less technical than the other books in this list, but invaluable for the coffee professional or enthusiast who travels and wants to know where to drink and what to look for in each country.
