## Description

Anette Moldvaer's Coffee Obsession -- published by DK -- is a large-format visual guide that balances encyclopaedic breadth with practical instruction. Coverage spans origins (with maps), equipment showcases, brewing guides, and an unusually broad collection of recipes that include creative espresso combinations and international brewing traditions. The DK production values make this one of the most visually impressive coffee books available, and its format makes it a natural coffee-table book as much as a reference. Particularly strong on espresso recipes and global brewing diversity.

---

## Key Topics

- Coffee origins and variety profiles with maps
- Espresso techniques and recipes
- Global brewing methods and equipment showcase
- Coffee cocktails and creative recipes
- International brewing traditions (Vietnamese phin, Turkish ibrik)
