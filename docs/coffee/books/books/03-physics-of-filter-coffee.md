## Description

Jonathan Gagne is an astrophysical researcher at the Montreal Rio Tinto Alcan Planetarium and an adjunct professor -- and he brought that rigour to bear on the humble cup of filter coffee. The result is arguably the most scientifically thorough coffee brewing text ever published. Gagne applies physics-grade analysis to percolation, extraction yield, grind geometry, turbulence dynamics, and water chemistry, backing every claim with data and original research. The book is not light reading, but it is profoundly rewarding for anyone whose brewing is guided by measurement and experimentation. Published in collaboration with Scott Rao, it sits at the extreme technical end of the brewing science genre. Essential for those who find Hoffmann's books too introductory.

---

## Key Topics

- Physics of percolation and extraction
- Grind size distribution and its impact on flow
- Water chemistry and mineral composition
- Pouring kettle design and turbulence
- Paper filter physics and brewer geometry
- Fines and their effect on percolation
