## Description

Hoffmann's follow-up to the World Atlas is more focused in scope but equally authoritative. Structured across six sections, it covers the entire home-brewing journey: selecting beans, choosing equipment, developing your palate, and executing great brews across every popular method. The final chapter on espresso is particularly strong -- demystifying machine selection and extraction theory without overwhelming the reader. Hoffmann writes with warmth and accessibility, avoiding the elitism that can characterise coffee writing. A Sunday Times bestseller in the UK, this book works equally well as a gift for a curious beginner and as a reference text for the advanced home brewer who wants everything in one place.
