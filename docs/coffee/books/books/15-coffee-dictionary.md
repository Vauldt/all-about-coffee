## Description

Maxwell Colonna-Dashwood -- three-time UK Barista Champion -- has produced the coffee world's most useful reference text. Rather than narrative or instruction, the Coffee Dictionary is exactly what it says: a thorough A-Z of coffee terminology spanning cultivation, processing, roasting, brewing, and sensory evaluation. Each entry is concise, clear, and free from unnecessary jargon. The book lives best on the brew bench or beside the cupping table, where it answers the quick definitional questions that arise mid-session. The hardback design is elegant and gift-friendly. An indispensable companion to any more substantial coffee text.

---

## Key Topics

- Varietal and cultivar definitions
- Processing terminology (washed, natural, honey, anaerobic)
- Sensory vocabulary and flavour descriptors
- Brewing and extraction terms
- Equipment and industry terminology
- Country and region-specific coffee terms
