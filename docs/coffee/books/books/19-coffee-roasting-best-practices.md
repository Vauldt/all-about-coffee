## Description

A slimmer, more focused follow-up to The Coffee Roaster's Companion, this 2020 monograph distils Rao's updated thinking on roast development. It concentrates heavily on airflow management -- a topic underserved in the earlier book -- and provides updated guidance on ROR management informed by a decade of industry experimentation since the Companion's publication. Written for practising professionals who already have a foundational roasting vocabulary, it is not an entry point but a refinement. The highest Goodreads rating in this entire list (4.60), though from a small but highly expert readership.

---

## Key Topics

- Advanced roast profiling strategies
- Airflow management and its impact on flavour
- ROR trajectory planning and crash prevention
- Baked coffee: diagnosis and avoidance
- Drum vs air roaster considerations
- Third-wave roasting methodology
