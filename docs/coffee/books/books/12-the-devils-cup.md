## Description

Stewart Lee Allen travels to the places where coffee's history unfolded -- Ethiopia, Yemen, Turkey, Vienna, and the Americas -- and weaves his firsthand observations with the broader historical narrative. The result is an irreverent, opinionated, and entertaining read that sits somewhere between travel writing and cultural history. Allen has a contrarian's eye and is not afraid to challenge the romantic myths of coffee's past. Less comprehensive than Pendergrast, but considerably more fun to read. A good companion to the more scholarly histories in this list.
