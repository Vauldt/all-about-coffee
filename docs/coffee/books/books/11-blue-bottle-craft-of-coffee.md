## Description

Written by Blue Bottle founder James Freeman with his partner Caitlin Freeman and food writer Tara Duggan, this book is equal parts coffee guide and brand manifesto. It covers the full arc from farm to cup through Blue Bottle's specific lens: their sourcing relationships, roasting philosophy, and the precise brewing methods deployed in their cafes. The recipe section -- contributed by Caitlin Freeman -- is a practical bonus. The book is visually distinctive and reflects the aesthetic that made Blue Bottle synonymous with the third wave. A worthwhile read for understanding what made the San Francisco specialty movement so culturally influential.
