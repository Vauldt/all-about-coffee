## Description

Scott Rao's Professional Barista's Handbook was the first book to treat espresso with the rigour it demands, and it remains the reference text against which all subsequent espresso literature is measured. Published in 2008 and still relevant today, it covers extraction theory, grind management, milk steaming, and equipment calibration in systematic depth. The prose is dense and data-rich -- this is not a casual read -- but the information is precise and actionable. Widely assigned in barista training programs globally. Note that as a self-published title, availability on Amazon fluctuates; specialty coffee retailers are often the more reliable source.

## Key Topics

- Espresso theory: extraction, variables, and diagnosis
- Milk steaming and texture for latte art
- Calibration methodology for espresso
- Equipment selection and maintenance
- Grinder and dose management
