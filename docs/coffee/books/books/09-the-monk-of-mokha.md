## Description

Dave Eggers -- best known for A Heartbreaking Work of Staggering Genius -- tells the remarkable true story of Mokhtar Alkhanshali, a young Yemeni-American who dreamed of reviving Yemen's ancient coffee trade. What begins as a business story becomes a survival narrative as the Yemeni civil war erupts around Mokhtar while he attempts to export his first coffee harvest. Eggers renders the journey with novelistic tension and intimacy, though some reviewers have noted the book leans more on narrative drama than coffee detail. A New York Times Book Review Notable Book that brought Yemeni coffee to global attention.
