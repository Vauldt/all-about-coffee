## Description

Jordan Michelman and Zachary Carlsen are the founders of Sprudge -- the most widely read specialty coffee media outlet in the world -- and they bring that publication's voice to this compact, confident guide. The New Rules of Coffee is less a technical manual than a manifesto: it explains the values and vocabulary of specialty coffee and gives newcomers the confidence to navigate the modern third-wave cafe with curiosity rather than intimidation. The tone is casual and accessible without being dumbed-down. Best as an introduction before moving to more technical texts.

- Coffee origin literacy and sourcing transparency
- Cafe culture etiquette and navigation
