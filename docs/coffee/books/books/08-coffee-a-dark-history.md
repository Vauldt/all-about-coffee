## Description

Where Pendergrast's Uncommon Grounds offers a panoramic history, Antony Wild's Coffee: A Dark History is more polemical and unsparing in its critique of the exploitation underpinning the global coffee industry. Wild -- a former coffee trader -- traces the commodity from its Ethiopian origins through Arab trade monopolies, European colonialism, slave-labour plantations, and the ongoing power imbalances between producing and consuming nations. The book is as much a moral argument as a historical narrative, challenging readers to confront the human cost behind their cup. Counterbalances the rose-tinted origin stories common in coffee marketing.

## Key Topics

- Colonial exploitation and the slave trade's role in coffee production

- The dark underside of the modern coffee industry
