## Description

James Hoffmann's *World Atlas of Coffee* is the single most widely recommended entry point into serious coffee knowledge. Updated to a third edition in 2023, it takes readers through the complete journey of coffee -- from seed to cup -- with dedicated chapters on cultivation, processing, roasting, and brewing, followed by an extensive origin-by-origin survey of 29 coffee-producing nations. Hoffmann writes with clarity and authority honed over a career as World Barista Champion and founder of Square Mile Coffee Roasters. The book is visually rich, with maps, photography, and diagrams that make complex information immediately accessible. Whether you are a curious beginner or an experienced professional looking for a structured reference, this is the book that earns a permanent place on every coffee person's shelf.
