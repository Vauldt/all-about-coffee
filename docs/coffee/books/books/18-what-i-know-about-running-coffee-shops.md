## Description

Colin Harmon is a four-time World Barista Championship finalist and the founder of 3fe Coffee in Dublin -- one of Europe's most respected specialty coffee businesses. His book is the antithesis of aspirational cafe romanticisation: it is bluntly practical, financially literate, and honest about the challenges of running a coffee business. Chapters cover everything from staff recruitment and training to menu engineering and financial controls. Harmon writes from hard-won experience, and the book's tone reflects that -- direct, occasionally wry, and always useful. Essential reading for anyone seriously considering opening a cafe or managing an existing one more effectively.

## Key Topics

- Cafe business planning and financial modelling
- Staffing, training, and team culture

- Hospitality philosophy and service standards
- Lessons from 3fe and the Dublin specialty scene
