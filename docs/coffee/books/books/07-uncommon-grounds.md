## Description

Mark Pendergrast's Uncommon Grounds is the most comprehensive single-volume history of coffee ever written. Spanning from coffee's mythic origins in Ethiopian highlands to the Starbucks era and the rise of third-wave specialty coffee, it traces the commodity's profound impact on global trade, politics, colonialism, and culture. The revised 2019 edition updates the story through the Coffee Crisis, the Fair Trade movement, and the emergence of modern specialty coffee. Pendergrast writes with a historian's rigour and a journalist's storytelling flair, making dense economic and political history genuinely gripping. Essential reading for anyone who wants to understand the full context of their morning cup.

## Key Topics

- Coffee's origins in Ethiopia and Arab world
- European coffeehouse culture and intellectual life
- Coffee's role in colonial economies
- The rise of commodity trading and futures markets
- Starbucks and the emergence of third-wave coffee
- Fair Trade, sustainability, and the Coffee Crisis
