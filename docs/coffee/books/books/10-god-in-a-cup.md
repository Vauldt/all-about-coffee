## Description

Weissman's book is a journalistic account of the people who built the specialty coffee world from the inside -- the obsessive buyers, farmers, and importers who chase the world's finest lots. The narrative centres on the famous 2007 Hacienda La Esmeralda Geisha auction and follows key figures at Intelligentsia, Stumptown, and Counter Culture. The writing quality has divided readers -- some find it compelling, others find the style cluttered -- but the access Weissman had to industry insiders makes it a valuable historical document of specialty coffee's formative years. Particularly interesting for understanding how the third wave actually came together.
