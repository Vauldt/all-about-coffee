## Description

Scott Rao's filter-focused companion to The Professional Barista's Handbook applies the same systematic, data-driven approach to all non-espresso brewing methods. Covering extraction theory, water chemistry, grind science, and method-specific parameters, it gives brewers the framework to understand *why* their brew works and how to diagnose and fix problems. Like all Rao titles, it is self-published, which keeps price high but ensures the technical content is uncompromised. Note: availability can be limited -- check specialty coffee retailers and Scott Rao's own site in addition to Amazon.

---

## Key Topics

- Coffee extraction theory for filter methods
- Optimal brewing ratios and parameters
- Water quality and its effect on extraction
- Grind and its relationship to brew time
- Brewing equipment overview and calibration
