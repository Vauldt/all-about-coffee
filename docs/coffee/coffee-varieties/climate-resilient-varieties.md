## Overview

Climate-resilient coffee varieties are Arabica cultivars bred or selected for tolerance to higher temperatures, drought, and disease — particularly coffee leaf rust (Hemileia vastatrix). Varieties developed by World Coffee Research (WCR) and CATIE, such as Centroamericano, Starmaya, and Marsellesa, are being distributed to farmers in climate-vulnerable regions to protect future production, representing one of the most important long-term responses to the existential threat that climate change poses to Arabica cultivation.
