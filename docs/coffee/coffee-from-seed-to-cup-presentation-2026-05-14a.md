# Coffee From Seed to Cup (Presentation 2026 05 14a)

## Suggested Outline: _Coffee — From Seed to Cup_

**30-minute community talk**

---

## Opening Hook (2 min)

Start with a provocative question or surprising fact to grab attention immediately:

> _"Did you know your morning flat white has travelled further than most people ever will — crossing oceans, changing hands a dozen times, over 3–4 years?"_

Briefly introduce yourself and your connection to coffee.

---

## Part 1 — Where Coffee Comes From (7 min)

- **The Coffee Belt** — countries between the Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn; why altitude, rainfall, and soil matter
    
- **Two species** — Arabica vs. Robusta in plain language (mild & complex vs. strong & robust); why your local café likely serves Arabica
    
- **The coffee cherry** — explain that coffee is a _fruit_, not a bean, and the seed is what we roast; this surprises most people
    
    > 💡 _Visual aid opportunity: bring a small branch with cherries, or show photos of a farm_
    

---

## Part 2 — Processing: How Flavour Is Made (6 min)

- The three methods — **natural, honey, washed** explained with accessible analogies
    
    - Natural = "sun-dried, fruity, wine-like"
        
    - Washed = "clean, bright, terroir-forward"
        
    - Honey = "the best of both"
        
- Why the same bean from the same farm tastes different depending on processing
    
- Brief mention of fermentation and how it mirrors winemaking or cheesemaking
    
    > 💡 _Analogy opportunity: compare to wine grapes — same grape, different winemaking = different wine_
    

---

## Part 3 — Roasting & the Flavour Spectrum (5 min)

- Green bean → roasted bean: the Maillard reaction in simple terms
    
- Light vs. medium vs. dark roast — what actually changes (acidity, body, sweetness, bitterness)
    
- Common misconception: **dark roast ≠ stronger caffeine** — light roasts actually retain more
    
- Specialty coffee's push toward lighter roasts to preserve origin character
    

---

## Part 4 — Brewing: Why Method Matters (6 min)

- The four variables every brew method controls: **grind size, water temperature, ratio, time**
    
- Quick tour of popular methods and what they deliver:
    
    - Espresso — concentrated, fast, pressure-driven
        
    - Filter/pour over — clean, nuanced, gravity-driven
        
    - French press — full-body, immersive
        
    - AeroPress — versatile, forgiving, travel-friendly
        
- Water quality: 98% of your cup _is_ water — mineral balance matters more than most people realise
    

---

## Part 5 — The People Behind the Cup (3 min)

- The supply chain: farmer → mill → exporter → roaster → barista — and why each step matters
    
- The specialty coffee movement's focus on **direct trade and farmer livelihoods**
    
- How paying more for specialty coffee connects to better outcomes at origin
    
- Brief mention of SCA's role in setting quality standards globally
    

---

## Closing (1 min)

Bring it back to the personal:

> _"Every cup you choose is a vote for how coffee is grown, traded, and made. Drinking well means drinking with intention."_

Invite questions and mention any follow-up resources (e.g., your All-About-Coffee encyclopaedia if you want to share it).
