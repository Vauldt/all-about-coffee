## Overview

This page documents the philosophy, design principles, and recent structural changes behind **All About Coffee** — the encyclopaedia of all things coffee. Understanding how the site is organised helps readers navigate it more effectively and find what they need, whether for home brewing, professional development, or academic reference.

## Philosophy

- **Connected knowledge** — links create understanding that isolated notes cannot
- **Multiple pathways** — entry from any point; there is no single correct path through the content
- **Practical focus** — theory serves practice; every concept connects to application
- **Continuous improvement** — the vault evolves as coffee knowledge evolves
- **Personal and professional** — designed to serve home brewers and industry professionals alike

## Maps of Content (MOC) Design Principles

- **Map of Content** files (MOC) function as navigation hubs, the detail lives in the atomic data files
- Extensive internal linking to create a connected knowledge graph
- Clear structure and consistent hierarchy across all Maps of Content ( a work in progress!)
- Multiple entry points so readers can arrive from any direction
- Standalone but interconnected — each Map Of Content is useful independently and richer as part of the whole

## Key Facts

- **All About Coffee** is published via Obsidian Publish
- All notes follow one of two file types: data files (encyclopedia entries) or MOC files (navigation hubs)
- Internal navigation uses wikilinks; every note connects to at least one MOC
- The vault covers all aspects of coffee from botany and processing through to sensory science and café culture
