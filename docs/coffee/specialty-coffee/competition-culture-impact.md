## Overview

Competition culture has had a profound impact on specialty coffee, driving technical innovation — including pressure profiling, experimental processing, and precision extraction — while elevating barista professional status and creating aspirational role models. Competitions have established a global vocabulary for coffee quality and technique, and routinely introduce new equipment, varietals, and preparation methods to wider industry awareness. The format has also attracted criticism for prioritising exotic and expensive ingredients over everyday café relevance, and for structural barriers that favour well-resourced competitors.
