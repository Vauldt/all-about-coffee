## Overview

Ethical coffee refers to sourcing and trading practices that prioritise fair compensation, transparency, environmental responsibility, and respect for producer communities — encompassing direct trade relationships, fair trade certification, living income commitments, gender equity programmes, and transparency reporting. It represents the intersection of specialty coffee's quality focus with a broader commitment to justice throughout the supply chain. The term is contested; some critics note that "ethical" claims can function as marketing without substantive change to producer livelihoods.
