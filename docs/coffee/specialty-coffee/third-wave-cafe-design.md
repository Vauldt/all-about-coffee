## Overview

Third-wave café design typically features exposed brick, concrete, wood, minimal signage, a prominent brew bar where preparation is visible, and simple menu boards focusing on coffee — often single-origins and manual methods. This design language, influenced by Nordic minimalism and Japanese restraint, communicates craft, transparency, and seriousness of purpose, distinguishing specialty cafés from the branded visual identity of chain coffee outlets.
