## Overview

The home brewing boom of the 2010s saw millions of consumers invest in quality coffee equipment for home use, purchasing burr grinders, gooseneck kettles, V60s, AeroPresses, and precision scales previously used only by professionals. This shift was supported by mail-order subscription services, YouTube education — most prominently through James Hoffmann's channel — and the increasing availability of freshly roasted specialty coffee online, collectively enabling a café-quality brewing experience at home.
