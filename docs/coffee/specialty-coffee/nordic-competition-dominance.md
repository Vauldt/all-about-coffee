## Overview

Nordic countries — particularly Norway, Denmark, Sweden, and Finland — dominated the World Barista Championship from 2000 through the early 2010s, with multiple Norwegian and Danish champions establishing these small nations as the global benchmarks for specialty coffee excellence. This dominance helped spread the Nordic roasting philosophy internationally and positioned Scandinavia as the intellectual and creative centre of the third-wave specialty coffee movement.
