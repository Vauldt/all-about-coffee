## Overview

Specialty coffee cafés are retail venues prioritising high-quality espresso and filter coffee prepared with specialty-grade beans by trained baristas. They differ from mainstream cafés and chains in their emphasis on single-origin coffees, transparent sourcing, skilled preparation, and typically a minimalist aesthetic — ranging from the informal Melbourne neighbourhood model to the more austere Nordic-influenced brew bar. The specialty café is the primary consumer-facing interface of the specialty coffee industry, translating producer quality into customer experience.
