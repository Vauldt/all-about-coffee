## Overview

A fourth wave framing centred on equity emphasises producer rights, fair income distribution, racial equity within the specialty sector, and the decolonisation of coffee narratives as the next evolution beyond the third wave's consumer-facing quality focus. This topic is covered in full in [Fourth Wave Discussion](fourth-wave-discussion.md). See [Fourth Wave Discussion](fourth-wave-discussion.md) for full coverage.
