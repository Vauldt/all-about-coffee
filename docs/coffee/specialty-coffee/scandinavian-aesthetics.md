## Overview

Scandinavian café aesthetics — characterised by minimalism, natural materials, clean lines, and an absence of visual clutter — have been highly influential on specialty café design globally. The austere, functional beauty of Nordic cafés was adopted by Blue Bottle Coffee and became the template for the international third-wave café look, communicating a seriousness of craft and a deliberate absence of the chain-café formula.
