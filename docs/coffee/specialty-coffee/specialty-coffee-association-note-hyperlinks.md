# Specialty Coffee Association (SCA)

The **Specialty Coffee Association (SCA)** is the world's largest coffee trade association. According to the SCA, it exists to foster a global coffee community and support specialty coffee as a thriving, equitable, and sustainable endeavor across the value chain. Source: [About SCA](https://sca.coffee/about)

## Overview

The SCA was formed in January 2017 through the unification of the Specialty Coffee Association of America and the Speciality Coffee Association of Europe. The association presents itself as a global body serving coffee professionals across producing, trading, roasting, retail, and education contexts. Sources: [About SCA](https://sca.coffee/about), [What is Specialty Coffee?](https://sca.coffee/research/what-is-specialty-coffee)

## What SCA does

SCA’s work is commonly grouped into several major areas:

- Research and thought leadership for the specialty coffee sector. Source: [SCA website](https://sca.coffee)
- Standards development for coffee evaluation, brewing, and related technical practices. Source: [SCA Coffee Standards](https://sca.coffee/research/coffee-standards)
- Education and professional development through certificate programs and learning pathways. Sources: [SCA Education](https://education.sca.coffee), [Coffee Skills Program](https://education.sca.coffee/coffee-skills-program)
- Community building through events, networks, and shared professional frameworks. Sources: [About SCA](https://sca.coffee/about), [SCA website](https://sca.coffee)

## Standards

SCA states that its standards are developed through a formal standards process involving a technical office, a Standards Development Panel, and expert drafting groups. It also notes that standards are published and revised periodically, and that some standards can support certification programs. Source: [SCA Coffee Standards](https://sca.coffee/research/coffee-standards)

A recent example is the association’s adoption of Coffee Value Assessment cupping standards, which replaced the older 2004 cupping protocol with updated standards for sample preparation, descriptive assessment, and affective assessment. Source: [SCA Officially Adopts CVA Descriptive and Affective Assessment Standards](https://sca.coffee/sca-news/sca-new-cva-cupping-standards)

## Education

SCA Education provides structured learning for coffee professionals. The education platform states that it offers a clear path for learners, trainers, and campuses, while the broader program listing includes certificate courses such as the Coffee Skills Program, Coffee Sustainability Program, Coffee Technician Program, and Q Grader Program. Sources: [SCA Education](https://education.sca.coffee), [New Pricing Model](https://education.sca.coffee/new-pricing-model)

The Coffee Skills Program is one of the most visible SCA education tracks and focuses on practical development in coffee preparation, tasting, and related skills. Source: [Coffee Skills Program](https://education.sca.coffee/coffee-skills-program)

## Specialty coffee definition

SCA defines specialty coffee as a coffee or coffee experience recognized for its distinctive attributes. That definition makes the association an important reference point for how quality, value, and professional standards are discussed in specialty coffee. Sources: [What is Specialty Coffee?](https://sca.coffee/research/what-is-specialty-coffee), [About SCA](https://sca.coffee/about)

## Why it matters

For coffee professionals, the SCA provides a common language for education, standards, and quality evaluation. For coffee businesses, trainers, and serious enthusiasts, its frameworks are widely used as benchmarks for skill development and technical practice. Sources: [About SCA](https://sca.coffee/about), [SCA Coffee Standards](https://sca.coffee/research/coffee-standards), [SCA Education](https://education.sca.coffee)
