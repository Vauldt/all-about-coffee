## Overview

Consumer education in specialty coffee refers to the deliberate effort by cafés, roasters, and industry media to help coffee drinkers understand origin, processing, roasting, and brewing — enabling informed purchasing decisions and building the appreciation that sustains premium pricing. Methods include thoughtful menu design, well-trained café staff, tasting events, brewing workshops, and online content; James Hoffmann's YouTube channel and European Coffee Trip's video guides are among the most influential digital consumer education resources in the English-language specialty market. Effective consumer education is widely considered essential to the long-term commercial viability of specialty coffee.
