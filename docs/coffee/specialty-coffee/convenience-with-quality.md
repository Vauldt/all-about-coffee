# Convenience with Quality

## Overview

Convenience with quality describes the challenge and opportunity of delivering specialty-grade coffee through convenient channels — ready-to-drink canned cold brew, high-quality supermarket bags, automatic espresso machines producing specialty results, and app-based ordering at quality-focused chains — without compromising the quality standards that define specialty coffee. It is a central concern of the fourth-wave discussion, reflecting a tension between craft and scale that the specialty industry has not yet fully resolved.
