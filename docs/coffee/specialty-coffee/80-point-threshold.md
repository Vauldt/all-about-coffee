## Overview

The 80-point threshold is the minimum score on the SCA 100-point cupping scale that a coffee must achieve to be classified as specialty grade. This topic is covered in full in [Specialty Grade Requirements](../specialty-grade-requirements.md). See [Specialty Grade Requirements](../specialty-grade-requirements.md) for full coverage.
