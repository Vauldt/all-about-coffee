## Overview

The popularisation of pour-over coffee as a café and home brewing method is one of the defining features of the third wave, reversing decades of automatic drip machine dominance. The Hario V60 (from Japan), Chemex, and Kalita Wave became symbols of craft brewing, with baristas using precise water temperature, controlled pour patterns, and measured ratios to produce clean, nuanced filter coffees that expressed a single-origin bean's character without the interference of milk or flavouring.
