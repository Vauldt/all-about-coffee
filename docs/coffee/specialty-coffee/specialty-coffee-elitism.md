## Overview

Specialty coffee elitism refers to criticism of the specialty coffee industry for creating cultural and economic barriers to participation — through high drink prices, arcane vocabulary, intimidating café environments, and an implicit hierarchy of taste that can make consumers feel judged for their preferences. The tension between maintaining quality standards and making specialty coffee welcoming and accessible is an ongoing debate within the movement, with critics arguing that gatekeeping behaviour undermines the industry's growth and ethical mission.
