## Overview

Specialty coffee affordability is an ongoing challenge for the industry — a high-quality café drink typically costs £4–7 in major cities, placing it beyond the daily budget of many consumers. The tension between the genuine costs of quality (premium green coffee, skilled labour, equipment investment) and the goal of broadening access drives debate about pricing, scaling, and alternative models such as subscriptions and ready-to-drink products.
