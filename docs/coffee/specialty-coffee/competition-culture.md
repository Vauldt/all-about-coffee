## Overview

Competition culture in specialty coffee refers to the ecosystem of national and international barista and brewing competitions that drive innovation, elevate professional standards, and create aspirational role models within the industry. The World Barista Championship, World Brewers Cup, and national qualifying competitions are central to this culture, and have historically been the primary channels through which new techniques, equipment innovations, and processing discoveries enter the broader specialty market.
