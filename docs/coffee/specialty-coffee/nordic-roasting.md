## Overview

Nordic roasting refers to the ultra-light, high-development roast style pioneered by Scandinavian specialty roasters — characterised by minimal roast colour, preserved acidity, and varietal transparency — that became one of the defining aesthetics of the third-wave movement globally. This topic is covered in full in [Nordic Coffee Philosophy](nordic-coffee-philosophy.md). See [Nordic Coffee Philosophy](nordic-coffee-philosophy.md) for full coverage.
