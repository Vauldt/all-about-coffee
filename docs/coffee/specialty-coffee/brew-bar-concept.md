## Overview

A brew bar is a dedicated counter section within a specialty café where baristas prepare individual filter coffees using manual methods — V60, AeroPress, Chemex, siphon — to order. The brew bar makes the preparation process visible to the customer and serves as an educational and theatrical element of the café experience, distinct from the espresso bar, signalling a café's commitment to filter coffee as a craft in its own right.
