## Overview

The Australian model of specialty coffee refers to the approach developed in Melbourne and other Australian cities of combining high technical quality in espresso and milk beverages with a welcoming, unpretentious service culture and neighbourhood café identity. It is frequently cited as an alternative to both the corporate second wave and the sometimes-elitist third wave, demonstrating that quality and accessibility can coexist. The model's influence has spread internationally, with Australian baristas and café operators opening venues in London, New York, and Singapore that explicitly reference Melbourne's café culture.
