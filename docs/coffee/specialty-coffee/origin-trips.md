## Overview

Origin trips are visits by roasters, buyers, or enthusiasts to coffee-producing farms and regions, serving both commercial and educational purposes. They enable direct relationship-building with producers, on-site assessment of crop quality and processing practices, verification of claimed certifications or methods, and the gathering of narrative content used in roastery marketing and consumer education. Origin trips became a hallmark of the direct trade model and are widely regarded as a marker of authentic sourcing engagement — though critics note the high carbon cost of international travel relative to the volume of coffee transacted.
