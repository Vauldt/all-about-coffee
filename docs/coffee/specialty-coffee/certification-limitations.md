# Certification Limitations

## Overview

Certification limitations in coffee refer to the structural shortcomings of third-party quality and ethical certification systems — including fair trade, organic, and Rainforest Alliance — such as certification costs that exclude smallholders, auditing gaps that allow non-compliant practices, standards that do not improve cup quality, and the risk that certification becomes a marketing tool rather than a genuine indicator of practice. These limitations have led some specialty buyers to favour direct trade relationships over certification as a more reliable and contextually appropriate quality and ethics mechanism. Critics and proponents debate whether improved certification design or alternative models better serve producer and consumer interests.
