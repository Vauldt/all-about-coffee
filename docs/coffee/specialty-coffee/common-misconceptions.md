## Overview

Common misconceptions about specialty coffee include the belief that dark roasting produces stronger or better coffee (dark roasting produces more roast flavour, not more caffeine), that expensive coffee is always specialty-grade (premium is a marketing term, not an SCA designation), that espresso is a roast level (it is a brewing method), and that Arabica is always superior to Robusta regardless of quality or preparation context.
