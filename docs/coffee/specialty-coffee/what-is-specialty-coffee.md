## Overview

Specialty coffee is coffee that scores 80 points or above on the SCA 100-point cupping scale, produced with attention to quality at every stage from seed to cup. This topic is covered in full in [Specialty Coffee](specialty-coffee.md). See [Specialty Coffee](specialty-coffee.md) for full coverage.
