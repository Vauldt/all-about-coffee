## Overview

The emergence of direct trade as a formalised sourcing model occurred primarily in the early 2000s, when a cohort of third-wave roasters began purchasing green coffee directly from producers outside the commodity chain to secure quality and build producer relationships. This topic is covered in full in [Direct Trade Definition](direct-trade-definition.md). See [Direct Trade Definition](direct-trade-definition.md) for full coverage.
