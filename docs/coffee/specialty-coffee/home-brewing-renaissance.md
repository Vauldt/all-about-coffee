## Overview

The Home Brewing Renaissance describes the rapid growth from approximately 2010 onward in consumer interest in preparing high-quality coffee at home using specialty equipment and techniques. It was driven by accessible brewing devices such as the V60, AeroPress, and Chemex, alongside affordable precision scales and gooseneck kettles, subscription coffee services, and online education through YouTube channels such as James Hoffmann's, which collectively lowered the barrier to producing café-quality results at home.
