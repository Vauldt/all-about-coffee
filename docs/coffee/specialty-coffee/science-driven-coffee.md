## Overview

Science-driven coffee describes an approach to specialty coffee production, roasting, and brewing in which decisions are guided by measurement, replicable methodology, and scientific understanding rather than intuition or tradition. It encompasses the use of refractometry, particle size distribution analysis, controlled extraction experiments, and peer-reviewed research on coffee chemistry — associated with figures such as Scott Rao, the Barista Hustle platform, and research from institutions including the University of Oregon's Mathematics department.
