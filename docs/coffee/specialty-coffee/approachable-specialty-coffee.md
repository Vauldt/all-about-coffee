## Overview

Approachable specialty coffee describes an ethos of presenting high-quality coffee without intimidation, gatekeeping, or jargon — associated particularly with the Australian café model, where exceptional espresso is served in relaxed, friendly environments. It is a deliberate counter-position to the elitist, austere, or correction-prone culture that some consumers and industry critics associate with certain third-wave café environments. The approachable model argues that quality and hospitality are not in tension — that the best coffee experience is one in which the drinker feels welcome regardless of their knowledge level.
