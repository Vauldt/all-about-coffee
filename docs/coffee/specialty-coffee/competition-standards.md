## Overview

Competition standards in specialty coffee refer to the rulebooks, judging criteria, and sensory evaluation protocols governing SCA-sanctioned competitions including the World Barista Championship, World Brewers Cup, World Cup Tasters Championship, and World Latte Art Championship. Standards are maintained by the World Coffee Events (WCE) organisation and define technical requirements (equipment, timing, quantities), sensory evaluation criteria (scored by certified technical and sensory judges), and presentation requirements; they are published publicly and updated annually. The detailed specification of competition standards has had a significant influence on specialty coffee practice more broadly, as competition-tested techniques in espresso preparation, milk texturing, and filter brewing have diffused into café and home practice through barista training culture.
