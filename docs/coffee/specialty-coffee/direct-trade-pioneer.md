## Overview

Intelligentsia Coffee is widely credited as the first roaster to formalise direct trade as a defined sourcing programme, establishing direct relationships with producers and publishing price transparency requirements from the early 2000s. This topic is covered in full in [Intelligentsia Coffee](../coffee-business/intelligentsia-coffee.md). See [Intelligentsia Coffee](../coffee-business/intelligentsia-coffee.md) for full coverage.
