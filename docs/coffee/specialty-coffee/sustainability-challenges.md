## Overview

Sustainability challenges in coffee encompass environmental threats (climate change reducing suitable growing land, coffee leaf rust spreading, water scarcity), economic pressures (C-market volatility, production cost increases, insufficient price premiums), and social issues (gender inequity, labour rights, land tenure insecurity). The specialty coffee industry acknowledges these challenges but debates the adequacy of current responses. Addressing these interconnected challenges requires systemic change across policy, supply chain structure, and consumer behaviour — not merely incremental improvement at the roaster or café level.
