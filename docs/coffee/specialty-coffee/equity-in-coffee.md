## Overview

Equity in coffee refers to the goal of ensuring that value, recognition, and economic benefit are fairly distributed throughout the coffee supply chain — that farmers and workers receive just compensation and credit, that gender equity is achieved at all levels, that indigenous and minority producers have access to specialty markets, and that the cultural knowledge of producing communities is respected rather than appropriated. It has become a central organising concern for the specialty industry alongside quality and sustainability.
