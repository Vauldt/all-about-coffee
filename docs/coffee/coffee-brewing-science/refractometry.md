## Overview

Refractometry in coffee uses a hand-held or digital refractometer to measure the total dissolved solids (TDS) of a brewed coffee, expressed as a percentage. Combined with the brew ratio (dose to water), TDS enables calculation of extraction yield — the percentage of the coffee's mass that was dissolved into the cup — allowing brewers to assess whether a coffee is under-, over-, or optimally extracted. The VST refractometer became widely adopted in specialty coffee from around 2010, bringing objective measurement to a domain previously governed by taste alone.
