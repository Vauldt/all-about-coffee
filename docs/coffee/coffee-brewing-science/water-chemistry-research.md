## Overview

Water chemistry research in coffee investigates how dissolved minerals — primarily magnesium, calcium, bicarbonate (alkalinity), and sodium — interact with coffee compounds during extraction. Landmark work by Dr Christopher Hendon (University of Oregon) and others has demonstrated that magnesium concentration in particular enhances extraction of flavour compounds, while high alkalinity buffers acidity and can suppress brightness. These findings have informed practical water recipes used by competition baristas and specialty cafés seeking to optimise extraction quality beyond grind and dose adjustments.
