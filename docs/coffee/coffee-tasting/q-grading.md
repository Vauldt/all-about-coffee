# Q Grading

**Q Grading**
Professional coffee quality certification system. Administered by Coffee Quality Institute (CQI). "Q Grader" certification for cuppers. Standardized evaluation methodology. Global coffee quality benchmarking. Essential for specialty coffee trade. Objective quality measurement. Professional credibility marker.
**Coffee Quality Institute (CQI)**
**Organization**
Founded 1996, nonprofit. Partner of Specialty Coffee Association. Global coffee quality mission. Training and certification body. Research and education. Standard-setting organization. Industry credibility high.
**Mission**
Improve coffee quality globally. Establish objective standards. Train professional cuppers. Create market transparency. Connect quality to value. Support farmer livelihoods. Industry infrastructure building.
**Global Reach**
Q Grading in 60+ countries. Training centers worldwide. Licensed instructors network. Certifications recognized internationally. Trade standard accepted. Quality language universal.
**Q Grader Certification**
**Requirements**
Attend 6-day Q Grader course. Pass 20 tests/exams. Sensory skills testing (triangulation, matching). Olfactory testing (smell identification). Cupping calibration. Grading evaluation. Organic acids test. Roasting basics. Green grading. Written exams. Intensive and demanding.
**Sensory Tests**
Triangulation: Identify different sample in three. Matching: Match samples by taste. Threshold testing: Detect compounds at low concentration. Smell identification: 36 coffee aromatics. Skills validation objective. Pass/fail scoring. Professional standard confirmed.
**Cupping Calibration**
Cupping 6+ coffees. Scoring each attribute. Comparing to instructor scores. Acceptable range required. Consistency demanded. Scoring reliability tested. Multiple rounds. Calibration to global standard. Industry alignment verified.
**Practical Grading**
Green coffee grading. Defect identification. Screen size determination. Sample preparation. Following SCA protocols. Hands-on assessment. Real-world simulation. Professional competency.
**Written Exams**
Coffee botany and processing. Roasting fundamentals. Sensory science. Cupping protocols. Green coffee grading. Quality standards. Industry knowledge. Theoretical foundation. Multiple exams, each must pass.
**Certification Validity**
Valid for 3 years. Recalibration required for renewal. Maintains skill consistency. Professional currency. Continuing education encouraged. Industry evolution tracked. Quality assurance ongoing.
**Q Arabica vs Q Robusta**
**Q Arabica**
Standard Q Grader certification. Arabica coffee evaluation. SCA protocols used. 100-point cupping form. Specialty grade 80+. Global standard established. Most common certification.
**Q Robusta**
Separate certification (newer). Robusta-specific protocols. Recognizes Robusta quality potential. "Fine Robusta" category (80+ points). Different cupping form. Robusta market development. Growing importance. Separate training and certification needed.
**Q Processing**
Specialized certification. Focuses on processing impact on quality. Post-harvest handling. Fermentation understanding. Drying management. Processing defects. Experimental processing evaluation. Advanced specialization.

**Q Grader Challenges**
**Intensive Course**
6 full days training. 20 tests demanding. High failure rate (30-40% typical). Expensive ($1,500-2,500). Travel and accommodation additional. Time commitment significant. Preparation recommended. Not guaranteed pass.
**Recalibration**
3-year renewal required. Retesting necessary. Cost and time repeated. Professional currency maintenance. But ensures ongoing competency. Industry standard justified. Commitment to excellence.
**Subjective vs Objective**
Cupping still has subjectivity. Q Grading reduces but doesn't eliminate. Individual palate differences. Cultural background affects perception. Calibration minimizes variation. But perfect objectivity impossible. Best available standard though.
**Cost**
Initial certification expensive. Recalibration every 3 years. Ongoing investment. But industry standard for professionals. Cost vs benefit calculation. ROI for serious professionals positive. Hobby cuppers may not need.
**Q Grading Protocol**
**Sample Preparation**
12g coffee per cup (bowl). 5 cups per sample (consistency check). Medium-coarse grind. 200ml water, 93°C. 3-4 minute steep. Breaking crust at 3-4 minutes. Standardized SCA protocol. Repeatability essential.
**Cupping Form**
SCA 100-point cupping form. 10 attributes scored (each 10 points maximum). Fragrance/Aroma, Flavor, Aftertaste, Acidity, Body, Balance, Sweetness, Clean Cup, Uniformity, Overall. Final defects deducted. Total score calculated. 80+ = specialty grade.
**Scoring Calibration**
Multiple cuppers score independently. Results compared. Acceptable variance (±0.5 points per attribute typically). Discussion if major disagreement. Consensus or average. Quality assurance. Reduces individual bias.
**Green Coffee Grading**
350g sample standard. Visual defect count. Category 1 and 2 separation. Screen size determination. Moisture measurement. Color and aroma assessment. Physical evaluation. Complements cupping. Complete quality assessment.
