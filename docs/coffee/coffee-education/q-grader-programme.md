## Overview

The Q Grader Programme is the Coffee Quality Institute's (CQI) flagship professional certification system for coffee quality evaluation. It encompasses Q Arabica (evaluating Coffea arabica), Q Robusta (Coffea canephora), and Q Processing designations, each requiring a rigorous multi-day examination — the full description of examination structure, recalibration requirements, and industry significance is covered in [Q Grader Certification](../coffee-tasting/q-grader-certification.md).
