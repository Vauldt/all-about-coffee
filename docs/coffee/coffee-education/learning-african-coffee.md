# Learning African Coffee

A structured progression from first encounter to deep expertise in African coffee origins. Follow these paths in sequence or jump to the level that matches your current knowledge.

## Beginner — Start Here

1. African Coffee Overview — Quick introduction to the continent and why it matters
2. [Ethiopia vs Kenya](../around-the-world/african-coffee/africa-in-general/ethiopia-vs-kenya.md) — Floral vs blackcurrant; understanding the two dominant origin styles
3. Cup both origins side by side — taste the brightness before reading further
4. Around the World/African Coffee/Africa in General/African Coffee Quick Reference — Price points, what to look for, common mistakes

## Intermediate — Build Knowledge

1. Explore Ethiopian regions — ../Yirgacheffe Coffee, [Guji Coffee](../coffee-geography/guji-coffee.md), ../Sidamo Coffee, ../Harrar Coffee
2. [Kenya Coffee Grading Standards](../coffee-geography/kenya-coffee-grading-standards.md) — AA vs AB, why AB is better value, how the auction works
3. Broaden to Rwanda, Burundi, and Tanzania — Around the World/African Coffee/Africa in General/African Coffee Thematic Index
4. Around the World/African Coffee/Africa in General/African Coffee Thematic Index — Processing methods (washed, natural, experimental) and their impact on cup

## Advanced — Master the Continent

1. All regional distinctions and individual washing station reputations
2. Around the World/African Coffee/Africa in General/African Coffee Comparisons — Value opportunities, Burundi vs Rwanda, AB vs AA
3. ../Sourcing African Coffee — Direct trade, verification, factory relationships, freshness management
4. Around the World/African Coffee/Africa in General/African Coffee Thematic Index — Heirloom varieties, SL types, Bourbon characteristics in depth
5. Around the World/African Coffee/Africa in General/African Coffee Market — Auction systems, ECX, direct trade economics

## Cupping Exercises

- **Session 1**: Ethiopian washed (Yirgacheffe Grade 1) vs Ethiopian natural (Harrar or Guji) — same origin, different processing
- **Session 2**: Kenyan AA vs Kenyan AB from the same factory — testing whether the grade premium is audible
- **Session 3**: Kenya vs Rwanda — SL variety vs Bourbon, brightness vs elegance
- **Session 4**: Ethiopia vs Yemen natural — familiar vs ancient, equatorial vs high-desert
