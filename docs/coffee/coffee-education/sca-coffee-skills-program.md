# SCA Coffee Skills Program

The **Coffee Skills Program** is the [Specialty Coffee Association](../specialty-coffee/specialty-coffee-association.md)'s internationally recognized professional education and certification system. It provides structured learning pathways covering all aspects of the coffee industry, from production to preparation.

## Overview

**Purpose**: Standardized professional development for coffee industry workers  
**Recognition**: World's most widely recognized coffee certification  
**Structure**: 6 educational pathways, 3 certification levels each  
**Delivery**: Through SCA Authorized Trainers worldwide  
**Credential**: Points-based system leading to SCA Coffee Diploma

The program ensures consistent coffee education globally while allowing flexibility for different career focuses and learning paces.

## Program Structure

### Six Educational Pathways

#### 1. Introduction to Coffee
**Level**: Entry-level foundation  
**Points**: 10 points  
**Duration**: Typically 1 day  
**Prerequisites**: None

**Content**:
- Coffee plant basics and Coffee Varieties
- Overview of Supply Chain from farm to cup
- Introduction to Coffee Processing methods
- Basic [Roasting](../coffee-roasting/roasting.md) concepts
- Simple brewing and tasting
- Specialty coffee industry overview

**Ideal For**: Complete beginners, coffee enthusiasts, new industry workers

#### 2. Barista Skills
**Focus**: Espresso preparation and milk-based beverages  
**Levels**: Foundation, Intermediate, Professional

**Foundation (10 points)**:
- [Espresso](../coffee-brewing-espresso/espresso.md) basics and machine operation
- Grinder setup and ../Maps of Content/Grind Size MOC adjustment
- Basic milk steaming and pouring
- Workflow and customer service
- Safety and cleanliness
- Sensory evaluation introduction

**Intermediate (25 points)**:
- Advanced espresso techniques
- Dialing In for different coffees
- Milk science and advanced texturing
- Recipe formulation and modification
- Equipment calibration and maintenance
- Sensory skills for quality control

**Professional (25 points)**:
- Expert-level espresso mastery
- Training and management skills
- Menu development
- Competition-level techniques
- Advanced troubleshooting
- Business and operations understanding

#### 3. Brewing
**Focus**: Manual and automatic brewing methods  
**Levels**: Foundation, Intermediate, Professional

**Foundation (10 points)**:
- [Brewing Variables](../brewing-methods/brewing-variables.md) overview
- ../Maps of Content/Grind Size MOC, ../Water Temperature, brew time
- [Coffee-to-Water Ratio](../coffee-brewing/coffee-to-water-ratio.md) fundamentals
- Basic brewing methods ([Pour-over](../coffee-brewing/pour-over-2.md), [French Press](../brewing/french-press.md))
- Tasting and evaluation basics
- Equipment introduction

**Intermediate (25 points)**:
- [Extraction](../brewing-fundamentals/extraction.md) science deep dive
- Advanced Pour Technique and [Agitation](../coffee-brewing/agitation.md)
- Brewing Coffee Control Chart application
- Multiple brewing method mastery
- Water chemistry and ../Water Quality
- Recipe development and optimization

**Professional (25 points)**:
- Expert brewing across all methods
- [Extraction Yield](../brewing-fundamentals/extraction-yield.md) and [TDS](../coffee-brewing/total-dissolved-solids.md) measurement
- Advanced water chemistry customization
- Troubleshooting and problem-solving
- Training and education skills
- Research and innovation

#### 4. Roasting
**Focus**: Coffee roasting theory and practice  
**Levels**: Foundation, Intermediate, Professional

**Foundation (10 points)**:
- [Roasting](../coffee-roasting/roasting.md) basics and equipment types
- Heat application and energy transfer
- Roast Level development
- Basic roast profiling
- Safety procedures
- Sensory changes during roasting

**Intermediate (25 points)**:
- Advanced roast profile development
- Roast Curves and thermal dynamics
- Green coffee selection for roasting
- Roast defects identification and correction
- Production roasting techniques
- Quality control systems

**Professional (25 points)**:
- Expert roasting and profile optimization
- Complex thermal management
- Business operations and production planning
- Equipment selection and maintenance
- Research and innovation in roasting
- Training and education capability

#### 5. Sensory Skills
**Focus**: Tasting, evaluation, and sensory science  
**Levels**: Foundation, Intermediate, Professional

**Foundation (10 points)**:
- How taste and smell work
- Flavor Wheel and flavor vocabulary
- Basic [Cupping](../tasting-coffee/cupping.md) technique
- Taste identification exercises
- Sensory evaluation introduction
- Flavor description basics

**Intermediate (25 points)**:
- [SCA Cupping Protocol](../coffee-tasting/sca-cupping-protocol.md) mastery
- Advanced sensory evaluation techniques
- [100-Point Scale](../tasting-coffee/100-point-scale.md) scoring practice
- Triangulation and discrimination testing
- Sensory panel calibration
- Defect identification

**Professional (25 points)**:
- Expert sensory analysis
- Panel management and training
- Statistical analysis of sensory data
- Sensory test design and implementation
- Research methodology
- Q Grader-level competency

#### 6. Green Coffee
**Focus**: Coffee sourcing, grading, and quality assessment  
**Levels**: Foundation, Intermediate, Professional

**Foundation (10 points)**:
- [Key Producing Regions and Their Character](../coffee-geography/key-producing-regions-and-their-character.md) and growing regions
- Coffee Varieties and cultivars
- Coffee Processing methods overview
- Green coffee grading basics
- SCA Grading Standards introduction
- Basic green coffee evaluation

**Intermediate (25 points)**:
- Advanced Coffee Grading and defect classification
- Processing method impacts on quality
- Origin characteristics and terroir
- Supply chain and sourcing practices
- Contract and pricing basics
- Quality assessment for purchasing

**Professional (25 points)**:
- Expert green coffee evaluation
- Origin relationship development
- Advanced processing knowledge
- Market analysis and trading
- Sustainability and certifications
- Training and education skills

## Certification Levels

### Foundation (10 Points)
**Target**: Beginners or those wanting basic knowledge  
**Depth**: Fundamental concepts and basic skills  
**Assessment**: Written exam, practical demonstration  
**Typical Duration**: 1-2 days per pathway  
**Prerequisite**: None

### Intermediate (25 Points)
**Target**: Experienced practitioners seeking advancement  
**Depth**: Advanced skills and deeper understanding  
**Assessment**: Comprehensive written exam, extensive practical exam  
**Typical Duration**: 2-3 days per pathway  
**Prerequisite**: Foundation level (same pathway) or equivalent experience

### Professional (25 Points)
**Target**: Industry experts and future educators  
**Depth**: Mastery-level expertise and teaching capability  
**Assessment**: Rigorous written and practical exams  
**Typical Duration**: 3-5 days per pathway  
**Prerequisite**: Intermediate level (same pathway)  
**Capability**: Qualified to teach Foundation-level courses

## Point System

**Points Awarded**:
- Introduction to Coffee: 10 points
- Foundation level: 10 points each
- Intermediate level: 25 points each
- Professional level: 25 points each

**Accumulation**:
- Points accumulate across all pathways
- Track progress toward SCA Coffee Diploma (100 points)
- No expiration on earned points
- Certificates issued for each level completed

## Assessment & Certification

### Exam Components

**Written Exams**:
- Multiple choice and short answer questions
- Theory and knowledge assessment
- Standards and protocols understanding
- Problem-solving scenarios

**Practical Exams**:
- Hands-on skill demonstration
- Timed performance assessments
- Quality evaluation and scoring
- Technique execution

**Scoring**:
- Pass/Fail based on minimum score threshold
- Retake policies for failed attempts
- Immediate feedback in most cases

### Certification Process

1. **Course Enrollment**: Register with SCA Authorized Trainer
2. **Course Attendance**: Complete required instruction hours
3. **Exam Completion**: Pass written and practical assessments
4. **Certificate Issuance**: Digital and/or physical certificate
5. **SCA Registration**: Certification recorded in SCA system

## Learning Pathways

### Career-Focused Paths

**Barista Career**:
- Introduction to Coffee → Barista Skills (all levels) → Sensory Skills

**Roasting Career**:
- Introduction to Coffee → Roasting (all levels) → Green Coffee → Sensory Skills

**Quality Control Career**:
- Introduction to Coffee → Sensory Skills (all levels) → Green Coffee → Brewing

**Café Owner**:
- Introduction to Coffee → Barista Skills → Brewing → Business focus

**Coffee Buyer/Trader**:
- Introduction to Coffee → Green Coffee (all levels) → Sensory Skills → Origins

### Comprehensive Path (SCA Coffee Diploma)

Complete all pathways at multiple levels to reach 100 points, demonstrating comprehensive coffee industry expertise.

## Comparison to Other Certifications

### vs. Q Grader (Coffee Quality Institute)
- **CSP**: Broader coffee education, multiple pathways
- **Q Grader**: Specialized in green coffee grading
- **Overlap**: Sensory Skills Professional similar to Q prep
- **Complementary**: Many professionals hold both

### vs. Barista Guild Certifications
- **CSP**: Official SCA credential, globally recognized
- **Barista Guild**: Community-focused, specialized
- **Relationship**: Barista Guild offers additional resources beyond CSP

## Continuing Education

**After Certification**:
- Attend SCA Events for ongoing learning
- Pursue SCA Coffee Diploma if not yet achieved
- Consider SCA Authorized Trainer pathway
- Engage in competitions and community
- Stay current with industry developments

**Recertification**:
- No formal recertification required currently
- Knowledge updates through events and resources
- Professional development encouraged
