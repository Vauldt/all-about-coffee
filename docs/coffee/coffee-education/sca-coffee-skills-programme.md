## Overview

The SCA Coffee Skills Programme (CSP) is the Specialty Coffee Association's modular professional education framework, covering six subject areas — Introduction to Coffee, Barista Skills, Brewing, Green Coffee, Sensory Skills, and Roasting — each offered at Foundation, Intermediate, and Professional levels. Completing all six modules at all three levels earns the SCA's highest individual credential, the Q Coffee Diploma. The programme provides a globally recognised pathway for coffee professionals to develop and certify their knowledge across the full supply chain.
