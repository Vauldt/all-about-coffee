# Navigate by Goal

### "I want to make better coffee"
1. Start: [Coffee Extraction Fundamentals MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-extraction-fundamentals-moc.md)
2. Then: [Extraction Optimization & Troubleshooting MOC](../maps-of-content/extraction-optimization-and-troubleshooting-moc.md)
3. Method-specific: [Brewing Methods MOC](../maps-of-content/brewing-methods-moc.md) or [Espresso MOC](../maps-of-content/espresso-moc.md)

### "I'm buying equipment"
1. Grinders (most important): [Coffee Grinders MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-grinders-moc.md)
2. Other equipment: [Coffee Equipment MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-equipment-moc.md)
3. Understand the technology: [Grinder Technology & Performance MOC](../maps-of-content/grinder-technology-and-performance-moc.md)

### "My coffee tastes bad"
→ Go directly to: [Extraction Optimization & Troubleshooting MOC](../maps-of-content/extraction-optimization-and-troubleshooting-moc.md)

### "I want to understand the science"
1. Extraction: [Coffee Extraction Fundamentals MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-extraction-fundamentals-moc.md)
2. Chemistry: [Coffee Chemistry MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-chemistry-moc.md)
3. Processing: [Coffee Processing MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-processing-moc.md)
4. Roasting: ../Roasting MOC

### "I'm learning about specific coffees"
1. Origins: [Coffee Origins MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-origins-moc.md)
2. Processing: [Coffee Processing MOC](../maps-of-content/coffee-processing-moc.md)
3. How to brew them: [Extraction by Brewing Method MOC](../maps-of-content/extraction-by-brewing-method-moc.md)

### "I need to fix or maintain my grinder"
→ Go directly to: [Grinder Maintenance & Care MOC](../maps-of-content/grinder-maintenance-and-care-moc.md)
