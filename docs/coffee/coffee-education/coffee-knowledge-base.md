## Overview

The Coffee Knowledge Base is the organisational structure of the Coffeepedia encyclopedia, mapping the full domain of coffee knowledge across eleven subject areas: plant botany and genetics, origins and terroir, coffee processing, roasting science, brewing and extraction, water chemistry, sensory evaluation and cupping, quality control, equipment, café business and operations, and professional development. Each domain is covered by a primary Map of Content (MOC) that links to the relevant encyclopedia articles.

## Key Facts

- Coffeepedia is organised into eleven primary subject domains, each with a dedicated MOC
- The Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) Coffee Skills Programme provides the canonical framework for professional coffee education
- The vault contains approximately 1,100+ encyclopedia articles covering all domains
