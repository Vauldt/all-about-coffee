```

       ↓             ↓           ↓          ↓           ↓           ↓
   Geography    Methods    Profiles   Particle    Chemistry   Technique
```

```
Bad Coffee → Troubleshooting MOC → Identify Issue
                                         ↓
                                   Extraction?

                        Grinder MOCs      Water MOC
```
