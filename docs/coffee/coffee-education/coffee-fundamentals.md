## Overview

Coffee fundamentals encompass the essential scientific and practical concepts that underpin all aspects of coffee quality — from growing and processing, through roasting and grinding, to brewing and sensory evaluation. Core subject areas include plant botany and agronomy, green coffee chemistry and quality, roast development and chemical transformation, extraction theory, water chemistry, and sensory science. Conceptual understanding of these fundamentals enables systematic improvement at every stage of the coffee value chain. For detailed coverage of each domain, see the linked MOCs below.

## Key Facts

- Coffee fundamentals span five primary domains: agronomy and botany, green coffee quality and processing, roasting science, brewing and extraction, and sensory evaluation
- The Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) Coffee Skills Programme is the primary structured curriculum for professional fundamentals education
- Conceptual understanding of cause-and-effect relationships translates directly to practical troubleshooting across all brewing and roasting contexts
