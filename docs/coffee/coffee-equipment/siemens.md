## Overview

Siemens is a German multinational engineering conglomerate. In the domestic coffee appliance context, the Siemens brand refers to consumer kitchen appliances produced by BSH Home Appliances GmbH (Bosch Siemens Hausgeräte GmbH) — the same company that produces [Bosch](coffee-equipment/bosch.md) appliances. BSH was a joint venture between Robert Bosch GmbH and Siemens AG until 2015, when Bosch acquired Siemens's stake outright; Siemens continues to licence its name to BSH for consumer appliance products. Siemens coffee machines are positioned in the upper-mass-market to premium consumer segment, slightly above Bosch in product positioning.

## Company Background

- **BSH Home Appliances GmbH:** Produces both Bosch and Siemens branded appliances; owned by Robert Bosch GmbH (100% since 2015)
- **Siemens AG** licences its brand to BSH; the Siemens consumer appliance brand is no longer directly operated by Siemens AG
- **Relationship to Bosch:** Both brands share the same manufacturing, technology platforms, and many product designs — Siemens models are typically positioned slightly above Bosch equivalents in design and features
- **Headquarters:** Munich, Germany (BSH)

## Coffee Machine Range

Siemens produces automatic espresso machines under the EQ series:

| Product Line | Segment | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| EQ.300 | Entry automatic | Basic one-touch; compact; entry bean-to-cup |
| EQ.500 | Mid automatic | LatteGo-style milk system; larger drink menu |
| EQ.700 | Premium automatic | Home Connect app control; personalised profiles; auto milk |
| EQ.900 | Ultra-premium | Top-of-range; Premium Grinder; full touchscreen; ultra-quiet |
| iQ700 Filter | Premium filter | Automated filter coffee; SilentBrew technology |

The **EQ.900** is Siemens's flagship automatic espresso machine — featuring the Premium Grinder (capable of detecting bean freshness and adjusting settings), a full-colour touchscreen, ultra-quiet grinding, and automatic milk system. It is one of the most technically advanced mass-market automatic machines available.

**Home Connect** — Siemens's smart home connectivity platform; allows machine control, diagnostics, and recipe customisation via smartphone app on EQ.700 and EQ.900.

## Siemens vs. Bosch

Since both Siemens and Bosch appliances are manufactured by BSH:
- **Siemens** is positioned as the premium brand (design-forward, feature-rich, higher price)
- **Bosch** is positioned as the practical/reliable brand (fewer design features, lower price)
- In coffee machines specifically, the Siemens EQ series is positioned above the Bosch VeroCafe series

## Market Position

Siemens EQ automatic machines compete with [Jura](jura.md), [De'Longhi](de-longhi.md) PrimaDonna, [Miele](miele.md), and [Philips](philips.md) Saeco EP series in the premium automatic segment. The EQ.900 is positioned at the top of the mass-market automatic category.

## Key Facts

- Siemens domestic appliances (including coffee machines) are produced by BSH Home Appliances, which also produces [Bosch](coffee-equipment/bosch.md) appliances
- Siemens licences its brand to BSH; Siemens AG no longer directly operates consumer appliance products
- EQ.900 is the flagship ultra-premium automatic machine; includes a Premium Grinder, full touchscreen, and Home Connect app control
- Siemens EQ series is positioned above Bosch in BSH's brand hierarchy — more design features, higher price
- Competing brands: Jura, De'Longhi PrimaDonna, Miele, Philips/Saeco
