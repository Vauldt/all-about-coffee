### **Notes on Classification**

- **Automatic vs Super-Automatic:**  
    _Automatic_ can mean programmed dosing and extraction, while _super-automatic_ typically includes integrated grinders, milk frothing, and one-touch drinks. Most brands listed produce _super-automatic_ machines at least in part of their lineup. ([Seattle Coffee Gear](https://www.seattlecoffeegear.com/collections/superautomatic-espresso-machines?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Top Rated Super Automatic Espresso Machines"))
