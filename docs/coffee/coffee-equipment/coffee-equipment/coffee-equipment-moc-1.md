## Equipment Philosophy

### Core Principles

*Equipment amplifies technique - it cannot replace it.*

*The best equipment is the equipment you'll use consistently and maintain properly.*

*Grinder first, everything else second.*

*Buy once, cry once - quality equipment lasts.*

*Perfect technique with modest equipment beats poor technique with premium gear.*

### Investment Wisdom

**Priority Order:**
1. **Grinder** - 40-50% of budget
2. **Brewer/Machine** - 30-40% of budget
3. **Accessories** - 10-20% of budget
4. **Upgrades** - Remaining funds

**Upgrade Strategy:**
- Start with good enough
- Identify actual limitations
- Upgrade what matters
- Master before upgrading
- Quality over quantity

**Longevity Focus:**
- Research thoroughly
- Buy quality
- Maintain properly
- Service regularly
- Appreciate what you have

---

*Coffee equipment is means, not end. The goal isn't to own impressive gear - it's to make excellent coffee. The best equipment setup is the one that matches your needs, fits your space, suits your budget, and most importantly, brings you joy in the process of making coffee every single day.*

*Don't chase equipment for equipment's sake. Chase better coffee. Let the pursuit of quality guide your equipment choices, not the reverse. And remember: the most important equipment in coffee isn't on your counter - it's between your ears. Knowledge, technique, and attention to detail will always matter more than any machine.*
