## Overview

Astra is an espresso machine brand best known in the North American market, producing commercial and semi-commercial espresso machines designed for café, restaurant, and high-volume food service use. Astra machines are positioned in the value-to-mid commercial segment, offering reliable professional-grade espresso equipment at price points accessible to smaller cafés and food service operators. The brand is distributed primarily in the United States and Canada.

## Machine Range

Astra produces a focused range of commercial espresso machines:

| Product Line | Segment | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Astra Mega 1 | Entry commercial | Single group; heat exchanger boiler; compact café |
| Astra Mega 2 | Commercial | Dual group; heat exchanger; mid-volume café |
| Astra Mega 3 | Commercial | Triple group; heat exchanger; high-volume service |
| Astra Gourmet | Prosumer / commercial | Single or dual group; compact design; smaller café and restaurant |
| Astra Automatic | Commercial | Volumetric dosing; programmable shots for consistency |

Common technologies across the Astra range:
- **Heat exchanger (HX) boiler** — simultaneous brewing and steaming from a single boiler system
- **Vibration pump** — used on some models (typical of mid-commercial machines)
- **Rotary pump** — used on higher-tier models for quieter, more consistent operation
- **Volumetric dosing** — available on automatic variants; programmable shot volumes

## Market Position

Astra is positioned below premium Italian brands such as La Marzocco, [Rocket Espresso](../rocket-espresso.md), and [Astoria](astoria.md), and competes directly with other value-commercial brands in the North American market. The brand is known for straightforward reliability and accessible pricing for food service contexts rather than specialty coffee or barista competition use.

Astra machines are commonly found in:
- Independent cafés with budget constraints
- Restaurants and hotels requiring reliable commercial espresso
- Office coffee service and food service contexts

## Key Facts

- Astra is a commercially focused espresso machine brand distributed primarily in the North American market
- Machines are positioned in the value-to-mid commercial segment; below premium Italian brands
- Heat exchanger boiler design is standard across the range; both vibration and rotary pump variants exist
- Volumetric dosing is available on automatic models for consistent shot output in high-volume environments
- Astra competes primarily on price-to-reliability ratio rather than specialty coffee performance features
