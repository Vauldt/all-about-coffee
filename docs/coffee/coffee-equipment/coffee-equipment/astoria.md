## Overview

Astoria is an Italian espresso machine manufacturer based in Treviso, Veneto, founded in 1969 as part of the Wega Group — a group that also produces [Wega](../wega.md) espresso machines. Astoria manufactures a range of professional espresso machines for café, restaurant, and high-volume commercial use, positioned in the mid-to-upper segment of the professional espresso machine market. The brand is distributed globally and is particularly established in European, Australian, and Latin American markets.

## Company Background

Astoria was established in 1969 in Treviso, Italy — a region with a deep espresso culture and a concentration of espresso machine manufacturers (including La Marzocco, Faema, and others). The company operates within the Wega Group and shares manufacturing infrastructure and technology across both brands. Astoria machines are manufactured in Italy and position themselves in the professional commercial segment.

## Machine Range

Astoria produces machines across several product families targeting different commercial contexts:

| Product Line | Segment | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Astoria Tanya | Entry commercial | Traditional Italian design; single-boiler or heat exchanger; mid-volume café |
| Astoria Gloria | Mid commercial | Dual boiler available; multiple group options; mid-to-high volume |
| Astoria Core | Commercial | Compact design; restaurant and bar use |
| Astoria Plus 4 You | High volume | High-capacity commercial; multi-group |
| Astoria Storm | Prosumer / commercial | Traditional lever-style design; specialty café use |

Machine technologies offered by Astoria include:
- **Heat exchanger (HX) boiler** — allows simultaneous brewing and steaming from a single boiler
- **Dual boiler** — independent boiler for brew group and steam; precise temperature stability
- **Traditional lever group** — spring-lever or direct-lever group head options on select models
- **Volumetric dosing** — programmable shot volumes for consistency in high-volume settings
- **Electronic temperature control** — PID-style temperature management on upper-range models

## Market Position

Astoria occupies a traditional Italian-style position in the commercial espresso machine market, competing with brands such as La Marzocco, [Rocket Espresso](../rocket-espresso.md), [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](../ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md), [Wega](../wega.md), [Bezzera](bezzera.md), and [Profitec](../profitec.md). The brand is generally considered a reliable mid-to-upper commercial machine rather than a premium prosumer or barista-competition machine.

## Key Facts

- Astoria was founded in 1969 in Treviso, Italy; operates as part of the Wega Group alongside [Wega](../wega.md)
- Machines are positioned in the professional commercial segment; distributed globally
- Key technologies include heat exchanger, dual boiler, lever group, and volumetric dosing options
- Competing brands include La Marzocco, Rocket Espresso, Bezzera, Profitec, and ECM
- Astoria machines are common in European, Australian, and Latin American café markets
