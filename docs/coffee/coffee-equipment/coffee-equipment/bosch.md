## Overview

Bosch is a German multinational engineering and technology conglomerate, with its consumer appliance division — BSH Home Appliances (Bosch Siemens Hausgeräte GmbH) — producing a range of domestic coffee machines. BSH is a joint venture between Robert Bosch GmbH and Siemens AG; both the Bosch and Siemens appliance brands operate under BSH. In the coffee context, Bosch produces filter drip machines, bean-to-cup espresso machines, and capsule-compatible systems targeted at the mass-market domestic consumer segment.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** Robert Bosch GmbH founded 1886 (Stuttgart, Germany); BSH Home Appliances formed 1967
- **Headquarters:** Munich, Germany (BSH)
- **Ownership:** BSH Home Appliances is a wholly owned subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH (since 2015, when Siemens exited the joint venture)
- **Relationship to Siemens:** Both Bosch and [Siemens](../siemens.md) consumer appliance brands are owned and manufactured by BSH, sharing platforms and technology

## Coffee Machine Range

Bosch coffee machines are sold under both the Bosch and the Tassimo (capsule system) brands:

| Product Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bean-to-cup (VeroCafe, VeroSeries) | Fully automatic; integrated grinder; espresso and filter-style coffee |
| Filter drip machines | Programmable filter coffee machines; thermal and glass carafe options |
| Tassimo capsule machines | Bosch-branded capsule system compatible with Tassimo T-Disc pods |
| Espresso machines (pump) | Pump-driven semi-automatic machines; 15-bar pump |

The **VeroCafe** and **VeroSeries** bean-to-cup lines are Bosch's flagship automatic coffee product — fully integrated grind-to-cup systems with programmable drink settings, milk texturing, and adjustable brew strength.

**Tassimo** is a capsule beverage system co-developed by Bosch and Kraft Foods, using a barcode-read T-Disc pod system to brew a range of coffee, tea, and hot chocolate formats.

## Market Position

Bosch (via BSH) occupies the upper-mass-market domestic appliance segment, competing with [Philips](../philips.md), [Siemens](../siemens.md) (same parent company), [De'Longhi](../de-longhi.md), [Jura](../jura.md), and [Miele](../miele.md) in the bean-to-cup category. Bosch bean-to-cup machines are positioned above entry-level but below the premium prosumer segment occupied by Jura and Miele.

## Key Facts

- Bosch consumer appliances (including coffee machines) are produced by BSH Home Appliances GmbH, a wholly owned subsidiary of Robert Bosch GmbH
- Both Bosch and [Siemens](../siemens.md) appliance brands are manufactured by BSH — they share technology platforms
- The VeroCafe / VeroSeries range are Bosch's flagship bean-to-cup automatic machines
- Tassimo is Bosch's capsule system; uses T-Disc pods with barcode-reading technology to control brew parameters automatically
- Bosch coffee machines occupy the upper-mass-market segment; below premium prosumer brands such as Jura and Miele
