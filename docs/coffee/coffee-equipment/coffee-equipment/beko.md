## Overview

Beko is a Turkish consumer electronics and home appliance brand founded in 1955, operating as a subsidiary of the Arçelik Group — itself a subsidiary of Koç Holding, one of Turkey's largest industrial conglomerates. In the coffee context, Beko produces a range of domestic coffee machines — including filter coffee machines, bean-to-cup espresso machines, and capsule machines — targeted at the mass-market consumer segment. Beko coffee appliances are distributed across Europe, the Middle East, and other markets where the brand has established appliance retail presence.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 1955 (as Arçelik; Beko brand established later)
- **Headquarters:** Istanbul, Turkey
- **Parent company:** Arçelik Group / Koç Holding
- **Key markets:** Europe (especially Eastern Europe, UK), Middle East, Asia

Beko is one of the largest appliance manufacturers in Europe by volume, known primarily for white goods (refrigerators, washing machines, ovens). Coffee machines form a secondary product category within its broad domestic appliance range.

## Coffee Machine Range

Beko's coffee machine range covers mass-market domestic needs:

| Product Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Filter drip machines | Automated filter coffee machines; programmable timers; thermal or glass carafe |
| Bean-to-cup machines | Integrated grinder; fully automatic espresso preparation |
| Capsule machines | Compatible with Nespresso-style capsules on some models |
| Espresso machines (pump) | Pump-driven espresso machines; 15-bar pump pressure; semi-automatic |

Beko coffee machines are positioned in the entry-to-mid mass market, prioritising price accessibility over specialty coffee performance features.

## Market Position

Beko occupies the value consumer segment of the coffee machine market, competing with similarly positioned brands such as [Krups](../krups.md), [Bosch](bosch.md), [Philips](../philips.md), and [Siemens](../siemens.md) at the mass-market level. The brand does not target the specialty coffee or prosumer segment; its machines are designed for general household use rather than precision espresso preparation or barista-level performance.

## Key Facts

- Beko is a Turkish appliance brand (Arçelik Group / Koç Holding); one of Europe's largest appliance manufacturers by volume
- Coffee machines are a secondary product category; Beko is primarily known for white goods (refrigerators, washing machines)
- Product range covers filter drip machines, bean-to-cup machines, capsule machines, and pump espresso machines in the mass-market segment
- Distributed across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia; not a specialty coffee brand
- Competes in the value-to-mid consumer segment with Krups, Bosch, Philips, and Siemens
