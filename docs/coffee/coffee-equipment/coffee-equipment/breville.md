## Overview

Breville is an Australian consumer appliance brand, founded in Sydney in 1932, known internationally for its range of espresso machines, grinders, and kitchen appliances. In the coffee equipment market, Breville occupies a distinctive position — producing prosumer-grade home espresso machines at accessible price points, making it one of the most commercially significant brands in the home specialty coffee segment. Breville espresso machines are sold under the Breville name in Australia, the United States, Canada, and Asia; and under the **Sage** brand in the United Kingdom and Europe.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 1932 in Sydney, Australia (originally as the Breville Radio Company)
- **Headquarters:** Sydney, Australia
- **Current ownership:** Breville Group Limited (ASX: BRG); publicly listed
- **European brand:** Sage by Breville — the same products sold under the Sage brand in the UK and Europe

## Market Position

Breville is one of the most significant brands in the global home espresso market, competing directly with [De'Longhi](../de-longhi.md), [Gaggia](../gaggia.md), [Sage](../sage.md) (which is itself Breville in Europe), [Jura](../jura.md), and [Niche Zero](../niche-zero.md) in the home grinder segment. The brand is notable for:

- Offering prosumer-level features (dual boiler, PID temperature control, pressure profiling) at price points below traditional European prosumer brands
- Strong presence in the US, Australian, and Asian home coffee markets
- The Breville Dual Boiler is frequently cited as offering the best value-to-performance ratio in the home dual-boiler espresso machine category

The Breville Barista Express — combining an integrated grinder and espresso machine in one unit — is among the best-selling home espresso machines globally.

## Key Facts

- Breville is an Australian brand (founded Sydney, 1932); sold as Sage by Breville in the UK and Europe
- The Barista Express (integrated grinder + espresso machine) is one of the best-selling home espresso machines globally
- The Breville Dual Boiler is widely regarded as one of the best-value dual-boiler home espresso machines available
- The Precision Brewer is SCA-certified for the Gold Cup standard — one of the best batch brew machines at its price point
- Breville competes with De'Longhi, Gaggia, and Jura in the home espresso market; and with Niche Zero and Eureka in the home grinder market
