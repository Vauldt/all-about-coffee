### Equipment Investment Priority

**First Priorities:**
1. Quality grinder (most important)
2. Brewing device for your preferred method
3. Scale (0.1g precision)
4. Kettle (temperature control for pour over)
5. Fresh coffee beans

**Second Wave:**
6. Better water (filtration/mineralization)
7. Upgraded brewing device
8. Specialty accessories (tamper, WDT, etc.)
9. Multiple brewing methods
10. Temperature measurement tools

**Later Additions:**
11. Backup equipment
12. Specialized tools
13. Training equipment
14. Aesthetic upgrades
15. Convenience features

### Common Equipment Mistakes

**Avoid These:**
- Expensive machine, cheap grinder (should be reversed)
- Too many brewing methods before mastering one
- Latest gadgets without fundamentals
- Neglecting water quality
- Skipping maintenance routines
- Buying based on looks over function
- Over-complicating with unnecessary tools
- Ignoring proper storage solutions

*Coffee equipment ranges from simple to extraordinarily complex, from affordable to investment-grade. The best equipment for you depends on your budget, space, experience level, and coffee preferences. *Remember: The most expensive equipment won't compensate for poor technique or stale coffee. Master the fundamentals with good equipment before chasing upgrades.*
