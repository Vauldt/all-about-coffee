## Overview

Sage Appliances is the European brand name for [Breville](coffee-equipment/breville.md) espresso machines and coffee equipment. Breville — the Australian appliance brand — sells its coffee equipment under the Sage brand in the United Kingdom and Europe due to existing trademark registrations for the Breville name in those markets. Sage and Breville products are identical in engineering and design; only the brand name and packaging differ by region. Sage is positioned in the prosumer and premium consumer espresso market across Europe, competing with [De'Longhi](de-longhi.md), [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md), and [Jura](jura.md).

## Brand Background

- **Sage Appliances** is a trading name of Breville Group Limited (ASX: BRG)
- **The Breville name** is not available for use in the UK and Europe due to pre-existing trademark registrations; Sage was adopted as the European brand name
- **Products:** Identical to Breville (Australia, USA, Canada, Asia) — same engineering, same SKUs, different branding
- **Headquarters (Sage):** operates from the UK for European market management

## Market Position

Sage occupies the same prosumer market position as Breville in its other markets — bridging the gap between mass-market brands (De'Longhi, Delonghi consumer, Philips) and traditional premium European prosumer brands (ECM, Rocket Espresso, Bezzera). The Sage Dual Boiler is frequently cited as one of the best value dual-boiler home espresso machines available in the UK and European markets.

## Key Facts

- Sage is the European trading name for [Breville](coffee-equipment/breville.md) products; identical engineering and design, different branding
- The Breville name is trademarked in the UK and Europe by another party; Sage was adopted as the European brand name
- All Sage products have Breville equivalents sold in Australia, the USA, Canada, and Asia under the Breville brand
- Sage Dual Boiler and Sage Oracle are the flagship prosumer products; Sage Precision Brewer is SCA-certified
- Sage competes with De'Longhi, ECM, Rocket Espresso, and Jura in the UK and European market
