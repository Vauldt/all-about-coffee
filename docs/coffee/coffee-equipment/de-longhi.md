## Overview

De'Longhi is an Italian domestic appliance manufacturer founded in 1902 in Treviso, Veneto — the same region associated with Astoria and Bezzera — and one of the world's largest producers of espresso machines by volume. De'Longhi produces a broad range of coffee equipment spanning entry-level capsule machines, pump espresso machines, and bean-to-cup super-automatics, targeting the mass-market domestic and prosumer segments. The brand has significant global distribution and is among the best-known consumer coffee equipment brands internationally.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 1902 in Treviso, Italy (as a small industrial parts manufacturer; pivoted to domestic appliances post-WWII)
- **Headquarters:** Treviso, Italy
- **Listed:** Milan Stock Exchange (BIT: DLG)
- **Key acquisitions:** Kenwood (UK) and Braun (domestic appliances division from Procter & Gamble, 2012)
- **Notable subsidiary:** De'Longhi holds a licence for the Braun domestic appliance range; also owns Kenwood appliances

## Market Position

De'Longhi is positioned across multiple market segments — from entry-level capsule machines through to high-prosumer semi-automatics. The brand competes with [Breville](coffee-equipment/breville.md) (and its European equivalent [Sage](sage.md)), [Jura](jura.md), [Miele](miele.md), [Philips](philips.md), and [Siemens](siemens.md) in the automatic and prosumer segments. De'Longhi's breadth of range and global distribution make it one of the most commercially significant brands in home espresso.

The La Specialista line targets serious home baristas seeking manual control with some automation assistance; the PrimaDonna and Eletta Explore target convenience-focused high-end consumers.

## Key Facts

- De'Longhi is an Italian brand founded in 1902 in Treviso; publicly listed on the Milan Stock Exchange
- One of the world's largest coffee machine manufacturers by volume; broad global distribution
- Range covers entry capsule machines through prosumer dual-boiler semi-automatics and premium super-automatics
- De'Longhi manufactures machines for the Nespresso capsule platform (under licence)
- Key competing brands: Breville/Sage, Jura, Miele, Philips, Siemens
