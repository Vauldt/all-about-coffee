## Overview

Miele is a German premium domestic appliance manufacturer founded in 1899 in Gütersloh, Westphalia, by Carl Miele and Reinhard Zinkann. Miele is the largest family-owned and -managed appliance company in the world, and occupies the very top of the domestic appliance market in quality and price. In the coffee context, Miele produces bean-to-cup fully automatic coffee systems — both freestanding and fully integrated (built-in) models — positioned at the premium to ultra-premium end of the market. Miele is particularly distinguished by its built-in, cabinet-integrated coffee machine systems designed for high-end kitchen installations.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 1899 in Herzebrock (later Gütersloh), Westphalia, Germany
- **Headquarters:** Gütersloh, Germany
- **Ownership:** Private; Miele and Zinkann family shareholders; no external investors; family-controlled since founding
- **Manufacturing:** All Miele products manufactured in Germany and Austria; no outsourcing of core production
- **Market position:** Premium segment; significantly above mass-market brands in price and quality

## Coffee Machine Range

Miele's coffee machines are exclusively fully automatic (bean-to-cup), available in both freestanding and built-in formats:

### Built-In (Integrated) Coffee Machines

Miele is particularly known for its built-in coffee systems — fully integrated into kitchen cabinetry at eye level, designed to operate alongside other Miele built-in appliances. Key models:

| Product | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| CVA (Built-in Coffee Machine) series | Fully integrated; plumbed-in water supply option; stainless steel finish |
| CM (Countertop) series | Freestanding bean-to-cup; same technology as built-in but standalone |

### Key Features Across the Miele Range

- **OneTouch for Two** — simultaneous preparation of two cups of the same beverage
- **EasyClick milk system** — one-click connection and automatic cleaning of milk system
- **Plumbing connection option** — some models can be directly plumbed to a water supply (no tank refilling)
- **Automatic cleaning and descaling programmes** — comprehensive automated maintenance cycles
- **AromaticSystem** — aroma-preserving bean storage and grinding system

## Market Position

Miele coffee machines are positioned above [Jura](jura.md), [De'Longhi](de-longhi.md) PrimaDonna, and [Siemens](siemens.md) in both price and perceived build quality. The brand competes primarily on:
- Long-term durability (Miele's "Immer Besser" — Forever Better — philosophy; machines tested to 20 years of use)
- Build quality and German manufacturing
- Integration with the broader Miele kitchen ecosystem (ovens, dishwashers, refrigerators)
- Built-in installation capability — a segment very few brands serve

Miele is the most expensive mass-market automatic coffee machine brand, with machines typically priced EUR 1,500–3,500+.

## Key Facts

- Miele is a German family-owned company (founded 1899); the largest family-owned appliance manufacturer in the world
- Coffee machines are exclusively fully automatic (bean-to-cup); both built-in (integrated) and freestanding formats
- Miele is the market leader in built-in, cabinet-integrated automatic coffee systems
- All Miele appliances are manufactured in Germany and Austria; the brand positions quality and durability above all
- Machines are tested to 20 years of use; marketed with the "Immer Besser" (Forever Better) quality standard
- Key competing brands: Jura, De'Longhi PrimaDonna, Siemens EQ.900
