## Overview

Philips is a Dutch multinational conglomerate founded in 1891 in Eindhoven. In the coffee context, Philips produces automatic espresso machines and bean-to-cup systems through its domestic appliances division — most significantly through the **Saeco** brand, which Philips acquired in 2009. Philips/Saeco automatic espresso machines are among the most widely distributed in the world, particularly in the mid-to-premium home automatic segment. The Philips brand also markets its own co-branded automatic coffee machine range (the EP series).

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 1891 in Eindhoven, Netherlands
- **Headquarters:** Amsterdam, Netherlands
- **Key acquisition:** Saeco International Group (acquired 2009) — the Italian espresso machine manufacturer
- **Domestic appliances division:** Versuni (formerly Philips Domestic Appliances) — spun off as an independent entity in 2021 and sold to Hillhouse Capital Group; continues to operate under Philips brand licence

## Coffee Machine Range

Philips and [Saeco](saeco.md) are sister brands under the same ownership (Versuni). The distinction between the two brands varies by market:

### Philips EP Series (Automatic Espresso)

| Product | Segment | Key features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| EP1200 | Entry automatic | One-touch espresso and coffee; compact |
| EP2200 | Entry–mid | LatteGo milk system; programmable |
| EP3200 | Mid | LatteGo; Classic Milk frother; intuitive display |
| EP5400 | Mid-premium | SilentBrew technology; LatteGo; 12 drink menu |
| EP5900 | Premium | Bean Adapt sensor; adjustable aroma; bean performance tracking |

**LatteGo** — Philips's proprietary automatic milk system; allows one-touch lattes without a traditional milk frother jug; easy-clean design.

**Bean Adapt** — automatic sensor on higher models that detects the bean type and adjusts grinding and extraction settings accordingly.

### Saeco Sub-Brand

[Saeco](saeco.md) machines are sold under the Saeco brand in many markets; they share technology with the Philips EP range. See [Saeco](saeco.md) for the brand-specific article.

## Market Position

Philips/Saeco occupies the mid-to-premium automatic espresso market, competing with [Jura](jura.md), [De'Longhi](de-longhi.md) PrimaDonna, [Miele](miele.md), and [Siemens](siemens.md) EQ series. The Philips EP range is frequently cited as offering the best combination of features and price in the mid-range automatic segment, particularly the EP2200 and EP5400 models.

## Key Facts

- Philips acquired Saeco (Italian espresso brand) in 2009; both brands now operate under Versuni, spun off from Philips in 2021
- Philips EP series are fully automatic bean-to-cup machines; LatteGo is the signature automatic milk system
- EP range spans entry to premium; the EP5900 includes Bean Adapt automatic bean-type sensing
- Competing brands: Jura, De'Longhi PrimaDonna, Miele, Siemens EQ series
- Philips/Saeco machines are among the most widely distributed automatic espresso machines globally
