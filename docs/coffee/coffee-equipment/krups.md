## Overview

Krups is a German-origin kitchen appliance brand, founded in Solingen in 1846 by Robert Krups — originally producing precision scales. The brand pivoted to small kitchen appliances in the 20th century and is now owned by the French group SEB (Société d'Emboutissage de Bourgogne), one of the world's largest small appliance manufacturers. In the coffee context, Krups produces a broad range of espresso machines, automatic coffee makers, and bean-to-cup machines at accessible to mid-market price points. Krups has a significant licensed arrangement with Nespresso, manufacturing machines for the Nespresso capsule platform.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 1846 in Solingen, Germany (Robert Krups)
- **Current ownership:** SEB Group (France); acquired Krups in 1991
- **Parent group:** SEB also owns Tefal, Rowenta, Moulinex, and All-Clad
- **Key market relationship:** Krups manufactures Nespresso-compatible machines under licence from Nestlé / Nespresso

## Market Position

Krups occupies the mass-market to mid-range consumer segment in coffee equipment, competing with [Bosch](coffee-equipment/bosch.md), [Beko](coffee-equipment/beko.md), [Philips](philips.md), and [De'Longhi](de-longhi.md) at entry and mid-market levels. The brand is not positioned in the specialty or prosumer coffee segment. Its strongest market position is in Nespresso-compatible capsule machines and mid-range automatic coffee makers.

## Key Facts

- Krups is a German-origin brand (founded Solingen, 1846); owned by the French SEB Group since 1991
- SEB also owns Tefal, Rowenta, and Moulinex — significant scale in European small appliances
- Krups manufactures Nespresso and Dolce Gusto capsule machines under licence from Nestlé
- Range spans capsule machines, pump espresso, bean-to-cup automatics, and filter drip coffee makers
- Positioned in the mass-market to mid-range consumer segment; not a specialty coffee brand
