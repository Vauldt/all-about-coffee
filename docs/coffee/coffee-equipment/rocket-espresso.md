## Overview

Rocket Espresso Milano is an Italian espresso machine manufacturer founded in 2007 in Milan by Andrew Meo and Daniele Berenbruch. Rocket is positioned in the premium prosumer and small commercial espresso machine segment, known for high build quality, classic Italian aesthetic, and performance-oriented design. Rocket machines are widely used by serious home baristas and in specialty cafés globally, competing with [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md), [Profitec](profitec.md), [Bezzera](coffee-equipment/bezzera.md), and Lelit.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 2007 in Milan, Italy
- **Headquarters:** Milan (Milano), Italy
- **Founders:** Andrew Meo and Daniele Berenbruch
- **Position:** Premium prosumer and small commercial espresso machines
- **Distribution:** Global; through specialty coffee equipment dealers

## Machine Range

Rocket Espresso produces a focused prosumer and professional range:

| Product | Boiler type | Key features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Appartamento | Heat exchanger | E61 group; HX boiler; compact; iconic round side panels |
| Mozzafiato | Heat exchanger | E61 group; larger capacity; timer-controlled on some versions |
| Giotto | Heat exchanger | E61 group; PID; commercial-grade internals |
| R Nine One | Dual boiler | Flagship; dual boiler; flow control; prosumer / small commercial |
| Cinquantotto (R58) | Dual boiler | Dual PID; E61 group; popular prosumer dual-boiler choice |
| Boxer | Commercial | Commercial single or double group; café use |

**Common features:**
- **E61 group head** — the standard thermal group; passive pre-infusion
- **Rotary pump** — standard across the range; quiet and pressure-stable
- **PID temperature control** — on most models above entry
- **Italian design aesthetic** — stainless steel; distinctive design; often compared favourably to Italian luxury goods in build character

## The Appartamento

The Rocket Appartamento is one of the most recognisable prosumer espresso machines globally — notable for its circular side panels (available in multiple colours and materials) and compact footprint. It is a heat-exchanger machine with E61 group, rotary pump, and a signature visual design that has made it one of the most photographed home espresso machines in the specialty coffee community.

## Market Position

Rocket Espresso is positioned in the premium prosumer segment, typically priced EUR 1,500–3,500+. The brand competes directly with [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md), [Profitec](profitec.md), [Bezzera](coffee-equipment/bezzera.md), and Lelit. Rocket is particularly valued for:
- Italian craftsmanship and aesthetic
- Build quality and durability
- The Appartamento's compact footprint and distinctive design
- The R58 and R Nine One as performance-focused dual-boiler options
