## Overview

Wega is an Italian commercial espresso machine manufacturer based in Treviso, Veneto — the same region as [Astoria](coffee-equipment/astoria.md) — and operating as part of the Wega Group, which includes both the Wega and Astoria brands under shared ownership and manufacturing. Wega produces a range of professional commercial espresso machines for the café and restaurant market, positioned in the mid-to-upper commercial segment. The brand is distributed internationally with particularly strong presence in European, Middle Eastern, and Latin American markets.

## Company Background

- **Headquarters:** Treviso, Veneto, Italy
- **Group:** Wega Group — the parent group encompasses both the Wega and [Astoria](coffee-equipment/astoria.md) brands; shared manufacturing and R&D infrastructure
- **Market position:** Professional commercial espresso machines
- **Distribution:** Global; strong in Europe, Middle East, Latin America, Australia

## Machine Range

Wega's commercial machine range targets café and restaurant use:

| Product Line | Segment | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Wega Polaris | Commercial standard | Multi-group; heat exchanger or dual boiler; volumetric dosing |
| Wega Orion | Commercial | Compact two-group; traditional Italian design |
| Wega Pegaso | High commercial | High throughput; multi-group; commercial café |
| Wega Nova | Entry commercial | Compact; single or double group; smaller cafés |
| Wega Urban | Commercial | Mid-range; versatile; bar and restaurant use |

**Common technologies across Wega commercial range:**
- **Heat exchanger (HX) boiler** — simultaneous brewing and steaming from a single boiler
- **Dual boiler options** — independent temperature control on premium models
- **Volumetric dosing** — programmable shot volumes for consistency in high-volume service
- **E61 group head variants** — traditional Italian group head technology on selected models

## Wega and Astoria

Since Wega and [Astoria](coffee-equipment/astoria.md) operate under the same Wega Group umbrella, both brands share:
- Manufacturing in Treviso, Italy
- Technology platforms (boiler designs, electronic systems)
- Distribution network in many markets

The two brands are distinguished by design aesthetic and product positioning rather than fundamental technology differences — similar to the relationship between [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md) and [Profitec](profitec.md) in the German prosumer market.

## Key Facts

- Wega is an Italian commercial espresso machine brand based in Treviso; operates under the Wega Group alongside [Astoria](coffee-equipment/astoria.md)
- Wega and Astoria share manufacturing infrastructure and technology platforms
- Machines are positioned in the professional commercial segment; widely distributed in Europe, Middle East, and Latin America
- Heat exchanger and dual boiler configurations; volumetric dosing standard on commercial models
