## Overview

Terra Kaffe is an American espresso machine brand founded in 2018, based in New York City. The company produces a single premium automatic espresso machine — the TK-01 — designed to combine the convenience of a bean-to-cup super-automatic with specialty coffee performance standards. Terra Kaffe positions its machine at serious home users who want high-quality automatic espresso without the compromise in cup quality typical of mass-market bean-to-cup machines.

## Company Background

- **Founded:** 2018 in New York City, USA
- **Headquarters:** New York City, USA
- **Product focus:** Single SKU — the TK-01 automatic espresso machine
- **Brand positioning:** Premium home automatic espresso; specialty-oriented; sustainability-focused

## The TK-01

Terra Kaffe produces one machine — the TK-01:

| Feature | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| Boiler | Thermoblock heating system |
| Pump | 19-bar vibration pump |
| Grinder | Integrated flat burr grinder; ceramic burrs |
| Grind settings | Adjustable; 10 settings |
| Espresso output | Single and double espresso; lungo; Americano |
| Milk system | Automatic milk frother (integrated); latte and cappuccino programmes |
| Display | OLED touchscreen |
| App connectivity | Terra Kaffe app for personalisation, maintenance reminders |
| Portafilter | Proprietary; internal; not a traditional 58 mm portafilter |

**Key design features:**
- Clean, minimalist aesthetic with a focus on premium materials
- Ceramic burr grinder (more durable and lower heat generation than steel burrs at this scale)
- App-connected with maintenance reminders and recipe customisation

## Market Position

Terra Kaffe occupies a niche between mass-market automatic machines ([De'Longhi](de-longhi.md), [Philips](philips.md), [Jura](jura.md) entry-level) and premium prosumer machines ([Breville](coffee-equipment/breville.md) Dual Boiler, [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md) Synchronika). The TK-01 targets US consumers who want more than a push-button machine but do not want the investment in a semi-automatic setup.

The brand differentiates on:
- Ceramic burr grinder (versus steel burrs in most competitors at the price point)
- App connectivity and maintenance transparency
- Premium aesthetic and build quality

## Key Facts

- Terra Kaffe was founded in 2018 in New York City; produces a single automatic espresso machine, the TK-01
- The TK-01 features ceramic flat burrs — more durable and less heat-generating than steel burrs
- App-connected (iOS/Android) for recipe customisation, grind setting, and maintenance reminders
- Positioned between mass-market automatic machines and prosumer semi-automatic setups
- Distributed primarily in the United States
