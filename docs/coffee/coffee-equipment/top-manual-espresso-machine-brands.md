## Key Technology Notes

All brands in the prosumer segment share common technologies as industry standards:
- **E61 group head** — the thermally stable group head (1961 Faema design); used across ECM, Profitec, Rocket Espresso, Bezzera, Gaggia Classic
- **Rotary pump** — commercial-grade; quiet; stable pressure; standard in prosumer and commercial
- **PID temperature control** — electronic precision brewing temperature; standard in all modern prosumer machines
- **Dual boiler** — independent brew and steam boilers; the gold standard for temperature stability and simultaneous brewing and steaming
