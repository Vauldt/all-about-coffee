## Overview

The affordable grinder category in specialty coffee refers to burr grinders priced under approximately AU$300 that nonetheless deliver grind consistency sufficient for filter brewing and, at the higher end of the range, acceptable espresso grinding — a category that expanded significantly in the 2010s with products from Baratza (Encore, Virtuoso), Eureka, Timemore (C2, Chestnut), and 1Zpresso. Prior to this period, the entry point for a quality burr grinder was considerably higher, and affordable options were dominated by blade grinders that produce uneven particle size and diminish cup quality. The development of affordable quality grinders is cited as a key driver of the home specialty coffee movement, enabling consumers to achieve café-standard extraction without professional equipment.
