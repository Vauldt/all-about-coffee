## Overview

The availability of affordable quality coffee equipment has been a key enabler of the home brewing boom, putting professional-level brew control within reach of everyday consumers. Sub-£200 burr grinders, gooseneck kettles, Hario V60s, AeroPresses, and digital scales allow home brewers to replicate café-standard extraction parameters with modest investment. The AeroPress, launched in 2005 at approximately $30, and the Hario V60, widely available under £20, are particularly significant examples of accessible precision brewing tools that democratised specialty coffee preparation.
