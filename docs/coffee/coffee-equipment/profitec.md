## Overview

Profitec is a German espresso machine manufacturer producing premium prosumer machines for the home barista and small commercial market. Profitec shares ownership and manufacturing infrastructure with [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md) — both brands operate under the same parent company in Mannheim, Baden-Württemberg, Germany. Profitec machines are known for precise German engineering, high build quality, and E61 group head technology, positioning the brand directly alongside ECM in the upper-prosumer segment.

## Company Background

- **Headquarters:** Mannheim, Baden-Württemberg, Germany
- **Relationship to ECM:** Profitec and [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md) share the same parent company and manufacturing infrastructure; machines from both brands often share chassis, group heads, and internal components
- **Market position:** Premium prosumer espresso machines; home and small commercial
- **Distribution:** Global; through specialty coffee equipment dealers

## Machine Range

Profitec produces a focused prosumer and semi-commercial range:

| Product | Boiler type | Key features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pro 300 | Single boiler | PID; E61 group; compact; entry prosumer |
| Pro 400 | Heat exchanger | E61 group; HX boiler; simultaneous brew and steam |
| Pro 500 | Heat exchanger | HX; rotary pump; larger capacity |
| Pro 600 | Dual boiler | Independent brew and steam boilers; PID; flow control |
| Pro 700 | Dual boiler | Dual PID; integrated flow control; flagship model |
| Pro 800 | Dual boiler | E61 group; large capacity; small commercial |

**Common features across the Profitec range:**
- **E61 group head** — thermally stable; passive pre-infusion
- **Rotary pump** (most models) — quiet; stable pressure output; plumbable on commercial models
- **PID temperature control** — electronic precision brewing temperature on all models above entry
- **Stainless steel and chrome construction** — premium finish; long-term durability

## Profitec vs. ECM

Since Profitec and ECM share manufacturing, their machines are closely related:

| Profitec | ECM equivalent | Key differences |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pro 300 | Classika PID | Minor aesthetic and feature differences |
| Pro 600 | Synchronika | Very similar; minor cosmetic differences |
| Pro 700 | — | Profitec flagship; flow control integrated |

The practical choice between Profitec and ECM often comes down to aesthetic preference and specific feature configurations rather than fundamental quality differences.

## Market Position

Profitec competes directly with [ECM (Espresso Coffee Machines)](ecm-espresso-coffee-machines.md), [Rocket Espresso](rocket-espresso.md), [Bezzera](coffee-equipment/bezzera.md), and Lelit in the EUR 1,000–3,500+ prosumer segment. The Pro 600 and Pro 700 dual-boiler machines are frequently cited as among the best-value dual-boiler home espresso machines on the market.

## Key Facts

- Profitec and ECM share the same German parent company and manufacturing; machines often share chassis and internal components
- E61 group head, rotary pump, and PID temperature control are hallmarks of the Profitec range
- The Pro 600 and Pro 700 dual-boiler models are highly regarded in the prosumer segment for build quality and value
- Competing brands: ECM, Rocket Espresso, Bezzera, Lelit
- Distributed globally through specialty coffee equipment dealers; priced EUR 1,000–3,500+
