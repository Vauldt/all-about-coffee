## Overview

VersaLab is a South Korean specialty coffee equipment brand producing a range of premium flat-burr electric grinders for the home barista and prosumer market. VersaLab grinders are positioned in the high-end prosumer segment, known for low retention, high-precision flat burrs, and innovation in grinder design. The brand gained significant attention in the specialty coffee community with its M5 grinder, which offered performance characteristics previously associated only with much more expensive commercial grinders.

## Company Background

- **Origin:** South Korea
- **Market:** Specialty coffee prosumer segment; global distribution through specialty coffee retailers
- **Position:** High-end prosumer grinders; competing with Niche Zero, DF64, Lagom P64, and entry commercial grinders

## Machine Range

VersaLab produces a focused range of grinders:

| Product | Type | Key features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| VersaLab M5 | Flat burr; electric; single dose | 64 mm flat burrs; low retention; anti-static; designed for espresso and filter |
| VersaLab M3 | Flat burr; smaller | Entry to the VersaLab range; 50 mm burrs |

**Key technologies on the M5:**
- **64 mm flat burrs** — the same burr diameter as many commercial single-dose espresso grinders (DF64, Lagom P64)
- **Low retention** — single-dose design; minimal coffee remaining in the grinding chamber between doses
- **Anti-static design** — reduces electrostatic clinging of ground coffee; improves workflow cleanliness
- **Variable RPM** — motor speed can be adjusted; affects grind character (lower RPM = cooler, some argue finer control)
- **Magnetic dosing cup** — magnetically secured dosing cup at the exit chute for low-mess single-dose workflow

## Market Position

VersaLab competes in the high-end prosumer single-dose grinder segment alongside:
- [Niche Zero](niche-zero.md) — conical burr; UK-made; 63 mm conical; similar single-dose philosophy
- [DF64](df64.md) — 64 mm flat burr; Italian; similar price range and feature set
- Lagom P64 — 64 mm flat burr; Australian; similar segment
- Eureka Mignon Specialita — Italian; high-quality prosumer flat burr

VersaLab is particularly notable for bringing South Korean engineering to the specialty coffee equipment market, offering performance competitive with established European brands.

## Key Facts

- VersaLab is a South Korean specialty coffee equipment brand producing premium single-dose flat burr grinders
- The M5 features 64 mm flat burrs, low retention single-dose design, and anti-static engineering
- VersaLab competes with Niche Zero, DF64, and Lagom P64 in the high-end prosumer grinder segment
- Variable RPM motor allows speed adjustment — a differentiating feature not common at this price point
- Distributed globally through specialty coffee equipment retailers; not available in mass-market retail channels
