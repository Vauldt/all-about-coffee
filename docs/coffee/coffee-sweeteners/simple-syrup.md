# Simple Syrup

**Simple syrup** is a liquid sweetener made by dissolving sucrose (cane sugar) in water. It is the most neutral and versatile liquid sweetener used in coffee and cocktail applications, valued for its clean flavour, consistent sweetness, and excellent solubility in both hot and cold beverages. It is the benchmark against which other liquid sweeteners are often compared.

---

## Composition

Simple syrup is a solution of **sucrose (C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁)** in water. Sucrose is a disaccharide composed of glucose and fructose in a 1:1 ratio. It contributes pure sweetness with no additional flavour compounds. In some preparations, particularly with extended heating, partial **inversion** of sucrose into its component monosaccharides (glucose and fructose) may occur, yielding **invert syrup** — slightly sweeter and less prone to recrystallisation.

## Ratios and Grades

| Name | Ratio (sugar:water by weight) | Brix | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simple syrup** | 1:1 | ~48°Brix | Standard; can be made cold by shaking |
| **Rich syrup** | 2:1 | ~65°Brix | ~1.35× sweeter than 1:1 by volume; less dilution added to drink |
| **Light syrup** | 1:2 | ~30°Brix | Lower sweetness; used for subtle sweetening |

**Important:** 2:1 rich syrup is not twice as sweet as 1:1 — it is approximately **1.35× sweeter** when measured by volume. A 10 ml dose of 2:1 syrup is equivalent to roughly 13.5 ml of 1:1 syrup, not 20 ml.

### Conversion guide

| 2:1 Rich Syrup | Equivalent 1:1 Simple Syrup |
|---|---|
| 5 ml | 7.5 ml |
| 10 ml | 13.5 ml |
| 15 ml | 20 ml |
| 20 ml | 27 ml |

## Production

### Cold method (1:1 only)
Combine equal parts sugar and water (by weight or volume) in a sealed container. Shake vigorously for 1–2 minutes until fully dissolved. No heat required. This method preserves a very clean, neutral flavour.

### Hot method (1:1 or 2:1)
1. Combine sugar and water in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Stir until all sugar dissolves completely — do not boil vigorously.
3. Remove from heat and allow to cool before bottling.
4. Optionally add a small amount of vodka (~1 tsp per 250 ml) to extend shelf life.

### Shelf life
- **Unflavoured, room temperature:** 2–4 weeks
- **Refrigerated:** 4–6 weeks
- **With vodka preservative (refrigerated):** up to 3 months

## Flavoured Variations

Simple syrup serves as the base for nearly all flavoured café syrups. Common infusions for coffee applications:

- **Vanilla syrup** — whole vanilla pods or extract added during hot preparation
- **Cinnamon syrup** — cinnamon sticks simmered in the syrup
- **Lavender syrup** — dried lavender flowers steeped off heat
- **Brown sugar syrup** — substitute white sugar for raw or demerara sugar; adds light caramel note
- **Demerara syrup** — golden, slightly molasses-tinged; common in specialty cafés
- **Ginger syrup** — fresh ginger sliced and simmered; used in spiced lattes

Commercial flavoured syrups (e.g. Monin, Torani) are typically 2:1 ratio sugar solutions with added natural or artificial flavourings.

## Performance in Coffee

### Solubility
Simple syrup dissolves instantly in both hot and cold liquids — its primary advantage over granulated sugar, which requires heat or prolonged stirring. This makes it the default sweetener for:
- Cold brew
- Iced coffee and iced lattes
- Shaken espresso drinks
- Any café beverage served cold

### Flavour neutrality
Light syrup and 1:1 simple syrup are the most flavour-neutral liquid sweeteners available, adding only sweetness. They are the preferred choice when the goal is to sweeten without influencing the coffee's flavour profile.

### Recommended starting doses

| Application | 1:1 Simple Syrup | 2:1 Rich Syrup |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso (double) | 10–15 ml | 7–10 ml |
| Filter coffee (250 ml) | 10–15 ml | 7–10 ml |
| Cold brew (200 ml serve) | 15–20 ml | 10–15 ml |
| Iced latte (350 ml) | 15–20 ml | 10–15 ml |

## Nutritional Profile

| Measure | Per 10 ml (1:1 syrup) |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~26 kcal |
| Carbohydrates | ~6.5 g |
| Sugars | ~6.5 g (sucrose) |
| Glycemic Index | ~65 (sucrose) |

Simple syrup has no nutritional benefits beyond caloric energy. It contains no minerals, vitamins, or fibre.
