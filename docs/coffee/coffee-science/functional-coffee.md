## Overview

Functional coffee refers to coffee products enhanced with additional health-focused ingredients — such as adaptogens (ashwagandha, lion's mane mushroom), collagen, MCT oil, or vitamins — marketed for specific wellness outcomes beyond caffeine stimulation. The category has grown rapidly in the consumer market, though specialty coffee professionals often view such additions as compromising the coffee's inherent flavour and masking quality. The scientific evidence for the health claims associated with many functional coffee ingredients varies significantly in strength and reliability.
