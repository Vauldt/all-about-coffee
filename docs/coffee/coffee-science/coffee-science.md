## Overview

Coffee science encompasses the scientific study of coffee across multiple disciplines — chemistry (extraction, Maillard reactions, volatile aromatics), agronomy (plant biology, soil science, climate), microbiology (fermentation, processing), food science (roasting, water interaction), and sensory science (perception, flavour evaluation). Research institutions, universities, and industry bodies such as World Coffee Research and the SCA contribute to a growing evidence base informing specialty coffee practice. The translation of scientific findings into practical brewing and roasting applications has accelerated significantly since approximately 2010, driven by competition culture and the publication of accessible reference texts.
