## Overview

Coffee roasting is the thermal process that transforms raw green coffee beans into the roasted beans used for brewing. The application of heat drives a series of chemical reactions — primarily the Maillard reaction and caramelisation — that develop the hundreds of volatile and non-volatile compounds responsible for coffee's flavour, aroma, colour, and body. Roast level and roast profile are the two principal variables that determine the sensory outcome of the roasted coffee. For comprehensive coverage, see [Roasting MOC](../maps-of-content/roasting-moc.md).
