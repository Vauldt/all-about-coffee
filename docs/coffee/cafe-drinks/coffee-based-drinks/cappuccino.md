# How to Make a Cappuccino

A cappuccino is made by combining espresso, steamed milk, and a thicker layer of milk foam than a latte or flat white. The classic guide is equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and foam, which gives the drink a stronger coffee taste and a more airy texture.

## Basic ingredients

- 1 to 2 shots of espresso, or a strong concentrated coffee
- About 4 to 6 ounces of milk
- Optional cocoa powder, cinnamon, or sweetener

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Brew the espresso.** Pull a single or double espresso into your cup. See [Breville's cappuccino guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-cappuccino.html), [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/), and [BBC Good Food's cappuccino recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cappuccino).
2. **Steam and stretch the milk.** Start with the steam wand near the surface to introduce more air than you would for a latte or flat white, then lower it to heat and smooth the milk. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/), [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-cappuccino), and this [YouTube cappuccino tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Ao5b6uqI40).
3. **Build a thicker foam cap.** A cappuccino should have a more noticeable foam layer, but the foam should still be fine and creamy rather than bubbly. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/) and [Breville's cappuccino guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-cappuccino.html).
4. **Pour and finish.** Pour the steamed milk first, then top with the foam so the drink keeps its classic layered texture. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/), [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-cappuccino), and [BBC Good Food's cappuccino recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cappuccino).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make strong coffee.** Use a moka pot or another concentrated brew method if you do not have an espresso machine. See this [home cappuccino video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdqkBz6d2dw) and [Pam's Daily Dish](https://pamsdailydish.com/no-machine-3-step-homemade-cappuccino/).
2. **Heat the milk.** Warm the milk until hot but not boiling. See [Pam's Daily Dish](https://pamsdailydish.com/no-machine-3-step-homemade-cappuccino/).
3. **Froth generously.** Use a handheld frother, whisk, French press, or jar method to make a thicker foam than you would for a latte. See [Pam's Daily Dish](https://pamsdailydish.com/no-machine-3-step-homemade-cappuccino/) and [My Everyday Table](https://myeverydaytable.com/how-to-make-a-cappuccino/).
4. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the coffee, add the warm milk, and spoon a generous foam layer on top. Dust with cocoa powder if you like. See [My Everyday Table](https://myeverydaytable.com/how-to-make-a-cappuccino/) and [BBC Good Food's cappuccino recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cappuccino).

## Quick tips

- Whole milk usually makes the richest foam and best cappuccino texture. See [My Everyday Table](https://myeverydaytable.com/how-to-make-a-cappuccino/) and [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/).
- A cappuccino traditionally uses a roughly 1:1:1 ratio of espresso, steamed milk, and foam. See [Breville's cappuccino guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-cappuccino.html), [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/), and [My Everyday Table](https://myeverydaytable.com/how-to-make-a-cappuccino/).
- If the foam is full of large bubbles, tap and swirl the milk pitcher before pouring to improve the texture. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/cappuccino-how-to/) and [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-cappuccino).
