# How to Make a Doppio

A doppio is simply a double espresso, meaning two shots extracted into one cup. It is not a milk drink, so the focus is on precise espresso preparation, a balanced extraction, and a rich crema.

## Basic ingredients

- 18 to 20 grams of finely ground espresso coffee, or the amount suited to your double basket
- Filtered water
- Optional sugar, served on the side if desired

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Grind the coffee.** Grind fresh coffee beans to an espresso-fine consistency. See [Voltage Coffee](https://voltagecoffee.com/doppio/), [Latte Art Course](https://www.latteartguide.com/doppio-espresso/), and [Shardor's doppio guide](https://shardorlife.com/blogs/news/doppio-coffee-guide-how-to-brew-at-home-like-a-barista).
2. **Dose the basket.** Add about 18 to 20 grams of ground coffee to a double espresso basket, or follow your basket's recommended dose. See [Shardor's doppio guide](https://shardorlife.com/blogs/news/doppio-coffee-guide-how-to-brew-at-home-like-a-barista), [Wrexham Bean](https://wrexhambean.com/blogs/brewing-guides/what-is-a-doppio-coffee-and-is-it-stronger-than-espresso), and [Green Plantation](https://www.greenplantation.com/a/preparation-espresso-doppia).
3. **Tamp evenly.** Compress the coffee with a level, firm tamp so water flows through the puck evenly. See [Voltage Coffee](https://voltagecoffee.com/doppio/) and [Latte Art Course](https://www.latteartguide.com/doppio-espresso/).
4. **Extract the shot.** Brew the espresso for about 25 to 30 seconds, aiming for roughly 36 to 60 ml depending on your recipe style. See [Wrexham Bean](https://wrexhambean.com/blogs/brewing-guides/what-is-a-doppio-coffee-and-is-it-stronger-than-espresso), [Latte Art Course](https://www.latteartguide.com/doppio-espresso/), [JURA Australia](https://au.jura.com/en/about-coffee/coffee-recipes/espresso-doppio), and [Green Plantation](https://www.greenplantation.com/a/preparation-espresso-doppia).
5. **Serve immediately.** A doppio is usually served straight in a small espresso cup. See [JURA Australia](https://au.jura.com/en/about-coffee/coffee-recipes/espresso-doppio), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppio), and [Wrexham Bean](https://wrexhambean.com/blogs/brewing-guides/what-is-a-doppio-coffee-and-is-it-stronger-than-espresso).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Use a concentrated brew method.** A moka pot is the closest common home substitute if you do not have an espresso machine. See [Voltage Coffee](https://voltagecoffee.com/doppio/) and [Wildkaffee](https://wild-kaffee.com/en/blogs/brewing-of-coffee/espresso-doppio).
2. **Grind fine and brew strong.** Use a fine grind suited to your brewer and aim for a concentrated result rather than a long coffee. See [Voltage Coffee](https://voltagecoffee.com/doppio/) and [Wrexham Bean](https://wrexhambean.com/blogs/brewing-guides/what-is-a-doppio-coffee-and-is-it-stronger-than-espresso).
3. **Serve a double portion.** Pour the concentrated coffee into a small cup and keep the serving compact, as the goal is to imitate a double espresso rather than a regular black coffee. See [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppio), [Wildkaffee](https://wild-kaffee.com/en/blogs/brewing-of-coffee/espresso-doppio), and [Dailymotion's overview](https://www.dailymotion.com/video/xgtlz9).

## Quick tips

- A doppio and a double espresso are the same drink; “doppio” is simply the Italian word for “double.” See [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppio), [Wrexham Bean](https://wrexhambean.com/blogs/brewing-guides/what-is-a-doppio-coffee-and-is-it-stronger-than-espresso), and [Dailymotion's overview](https://www.dailymotion.com/video/xgtlz9).
- In many specialty cafés, a doppio is the standard espresso serving rather than a single. See [Wrexham Bean](https://wrexhambean.com/blogs/brewing-guides/what-is-a-doppio-coffee-and-is-it-stronger-than-espresso) and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppio).
- If the shot runs too fast or too slow, adjust the grind before changing everything else. A balanced doppio depends heavily on grind size and extraction time. See [Shardor's doppio guide](https://shardorlife.com/blogs/news/doppio-coffee-guide-how-to-brew-at-home-like-a-barista), [Latte Art Course](https://www.latteartguide.com/doppio-espresso/), and [Voltage Coffee](https://voltagecoffee.com/doppio/).
