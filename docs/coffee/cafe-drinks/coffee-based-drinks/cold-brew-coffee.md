# How to Make Cold Brew Coffee

Cold brew coffee is made by steeping coarse-ground coffee in cold or room-temperature water for many hours, then filtering the brew to produce a smooth, low-acidity coffee. A practical starting point is either a ready-to-drink ratio around 1:10 to 1:15, or a stronger concentrate around 1:4 to 1:8 that you dilute later.

## Basic ingredients and equipment

- Fresh coffee beans
- Filtered water
- Grinder
- Large jar, pitcher, or cold brew brewer
- Fine mesh sieve, paper filter, cheesecloth, or filter bag
- Scales or measuring cups

## Basic recipe

- **Ready-to-drink cold brew:** about 67 to 100 grams of coffee per litre of water
- **Cold brew concentrate:** about 125 to 250 grams of coffee per litre of water
- Coarse grind
- Steep time of about 8 to 18 hours, depending on ratio, temperature, and taste preference

## Method

1. **Grind the coffee coarse.** Most guides recommend a coarse grind so the coffee extracts evenly without becoming muddy or over-extracted. See [Padre Coffee](https://www.padrecoffee.com.au/pages/cold-brew-coffee-the-definitive-guide), [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), and [Pull & Pour Coffee](https://pullandpourcoffee.com/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/).
2. **Measure the coffee and water.** Choose a ratio based on whether you want a ready-to-drink brew or a concentrate. See [Seven Miles](https://www.sevenmiles.com.au/blogs/editorial/cold-brew-coffee-guide), [Counter Culture Coffee](https://counterculturecoffee.com/blogs/counter-culture-coffee/guide-to-cold-brew), [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), and [Bun Coffee](https://www.buncoffee.com.au/blogs/brew-guides/cold-brew-ratio).
3. **Combine and stir.** Add the coffee and water to your jar or brewer, then stir gently so all grounds are fully saturated. See [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), [Downshiftology](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/), and [Pablo & Rusty's](https://pabloandrustys.com.au/blogs/drinkbettercoffee/what-s-the-best-way-to-make-cold-brew-coffee-at-home).
4. **Steep the coffee.** Cover the container and leave it in the fridge or at room temperature for roughly 8 to 18 hours, adjusting for strength and flavour. See [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), [MACCHIATO](https://coffee.macchiato.com.au/pages/cold-brew-guide), [Pablo & Rusty's](https://pabloandrustys.com.au/blogs/drinkbettercoffee/what-s-the-best-way-to-make-cold-brew-coffee-at-home), and [Pull & Pour Coffee](https://pullandpourcoffee.com/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/).
5. **Strain thoroughly.** Filter out the grounds using a sieve, paper filter, cheesecloth, nut milk bag, or the filter system in your brewer. See [Downshiftology](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/), [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), and [Pull & Pour Coffee](https://pullandpourcoffee.com/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/).
6. **Serve and dilute if needed.** Drink ready-to-drink cold brew as is, or dilute concentrate with water, milk, or ice to taste. See [Counter Culture Coffee](https://counterculturecoffee.com/blogs/counter-culture-coffee/guide-to-cold-brew), [MACCHIATO](https://coffee.macchiato.com.au/pages/cold-brew-guide), and [Robust Coffee](https://robustcoffee.au/blogs/news/cold-brew-ratio).

## Quick tips

- If you are new to cold brew, start with a milder ratio such as 1:8 to 1:12, or use about 100 grams per litre for an easy ready-to-drink batch. See [Bun Coffee](https://www.buncoffee.com.au/blogs/brew-guides/cold-brew-ratio), [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), and [MACCHIATO](https://coffee.macchiato.com.au/pages/cold-brew-guide).
- Shorter steep times tend to produce a lighter cup, while longer steep times usually give a bolder and heavier result. See [Sample Coffee](https://samplecoffee.com.au/brewguides/cold-brew-coffee), [Pull & Pour Coffee](https://pullandpourcoffee.com/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/), and [Melinda Strauss](https://melindastrauss.com/2017/07/27/the-beginners-guide-to-cold-brew-coffee/).
- Store strained cold brew in the fridge and use it within several days for the best flavour. See [Pablo & Rusty's](https://pabloandrustys.com.au/blogs/drinkbettercoffee/what-s-the-best-way-to-make-cold-brew-coffee-at-home) and [Counter Culture Coffee](https://counterculturecoffee.com/blogs/counter-culture-coffee/guide-to-cold-brew).
