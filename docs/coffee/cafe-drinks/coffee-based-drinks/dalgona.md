# How to Make Dalgona Coffee

Dalgona coffee, also called whipped coffee, is made by whipping instant coffee, sugar, and hot water into a thick foam, then spooning it over milk. A classic starting formula is equal parts instant coffee, sugar, and hot water, usually in a 1:1:1 ratio.

## Basic ingredients

- 2 tablespoons instant coffee
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons hot or boiling water
- 1 cup milk of your choice
- Ice, optional for an iced version
- Optional toppings such as cocoa, cinnamon, or vanilla

## Method

1. **Combine the base ingredients.** Add the instant coffee, sugar, and hot water to a mixing bowl in equal parts. See [My Korean Kitchen](https://mykoreankitchen.com/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Jessica in the Kitchen](https://jessicainthekitchen.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee-tips/), and [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/279548/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/).
2. **Whip until thick and fluffy.** Use a hand mixer, stand mixer, frother, or whisk until the mixture turns light brown and forms soft to stiff peaks. This often takes about 2 to 5 minutes with an electric mixer. See [My Korean Kitchen](https://mykoreankitchen.com/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/279548/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Family Fresh Meals](https://www.familyfreshmeals.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee/), and [Alton Brown](https://altonbrown.com/recipes/how-to-make-dalgona-coffee/).
3. **Prepare the milk.** Fill a glass with cold milk and ice for an iced version, or use warm milk if you want it hot. See [Jessica in the Kitchen](https://jessicainthekitchen.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee-tips/), [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/279548/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), and [Sophisticated Gourmet](https://www.sophisticatedgourmet.com/2020/04/dalgona-whipped-coffee-recipe/).
4. **Top with the whipped coffee.** Spoon the thick whipped mixture over the milk. See [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/279548/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Host The Toast](https://hostthetoast.com/whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee/), and this [Dalgona coffee video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9SjCEOWd3PU).
5. **Stir before drinking.** Mix the foam into the milk so the drink becomes balanced and creamy. See [My Korean Kitchen](https://mykoreankitchen.com/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Host The Toast](https://hostthetoast.com/whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee/), and [Barista Courses Australia](https://baristacoursesaustralia.com.au/blog/what-is-whipped-coffee-and-how-to-make-it/).

## Method without an electric mixer

1. **Whisk by hand.** Manual whisking can work, though it takes much longer than using an electric mixer. See [My Korean Kitchen](https://mykoreankitchen.com/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Kimchimari](https://kimchimari.com/dalgona-coffee/), and [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/whipped-coffee-trend-review-23017225).
2. **Use instant coffee for the best structure.** Most guides note that instant coffee whips more reliably than regular brewed coffee. See [Jessica in the Kitchen](https://jessicainthekitchen.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee-tips/), [Kimchimari](https://kimchimari.com/dalgona-coffee/), and [Sophisticated Gourmet](https://www.sophisticatedgourmet.com/2020/04/dalgona-whipped-coffee-recipe/).
3. **Alternative non-instant versions exist.** Some recipes use strong brewed coffee or espresso, though the texture can differ from the classic version. See [Okonomi Kitchen](https://www.okonomikitchen.com/dalgona-whipped-coffee/) and [Always Use Butter](https://alwaysusebutter.com/whipped-coffee-without-instant-coffee/).

## Quick tips

- The equal-parts ratio is the main reason Dalgona coffee is so repeatable and easy to scale up. See [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/279548/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), [Jessica in the Kitchen](https://jessicainthekitchen.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee-tips/), and [My Korean Kitchen](https://mykoreankitchen.com/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/).
- If the drink tastes too bitter, slightly reduce the coffee or increase the sugar, especially when using strong instant espresso powder. See [Family Fresh Meals](https://www.familyfreshmeals.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee/) and [Jessica in the Kitchen](https://jessicainthekitchen.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee-tips/).
- Dairy and plant milks can both work well, and the drink can be served hot or iced. See [Jessica in the Kitchen](https://jessicainthekitchen.com/how-to-make-whipped-coffee-dalgona-coffee-tips/), [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/279548/dalgona-coffee-whipped-coffee/), and [Sophisticated Gourmet](https://www.sophisticatedgourmet.com/2020/04/dalgona-whipped-coffee-recipe/).
