
# How to Make a Macchiato

A traditional macchiato is an espresso marked with a small amount of milk foam rather than a full pour of steamed milk. It is a very espresso-forward drink, usually served in a small cup with only a spoonful or two of foam on top.

## Basic ingredients

- 1 to 2 shots of espresso
- A small amount of milk
- 2 to 3 teaspoons of milk foam, or a small dollop of microfoam
- Optional sugar, though it is usually served without it

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Brew the espresso.** Pull a single or double espresso into a small espresso cup. See [BBC Good Food's macchiato recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/macchiato), [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-macchiato), and [CoffeeGeek's traditional macchiato guide](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/traditional-macchiato-how-to/).
2. **Steam a small amount of milk.** Steam just enough milk to create fine foam or light microfoam. See [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-macchiato), [CoffeeGeek's traditional macchiato guide](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/traditional-macchiato-how-to/), and [Bizewo's milk frothing guide](https://bizewohome.com/blogs/news/how-to-froth-milk-for-a-macchiato).
3. **Keep the drink espresso-forward.** A macchiato should use only a small amount of milk, enough to mark the espresso rather than turn it into a milky drink. See [CoffeeGeek's traditional macchiato guide](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/traditional-macchiato-how-to/), [BBC Good Food's macchiato recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/macchiato), and this [milk steaming video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0vD--H7poxU).
4. **Top the espresso with foam.** Spoon a small amount of foam onto the espresso, or add a tiny splash of milk and then the foam, depending on your preferred style. See [BBC Good Food's macchiato recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/macchiato), [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-macchiato), and [Barista Coffee Roasters](https://cafebarista.ca/en/blogs/coffee/how-to-make-macchiato).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make strong coffee.** Use a moka pot, AeroPress, or another concentrated brew method if you do not have an espresso machine. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-macchiato-the-easiest-most-delicious-recipe) and [Bizewo's milk frothing guide](https://bizewohome.com/blogs/news/how-to-froth-milk-for-a-macchiato).
2. **Heat a little milk.** Warm a small amount of milk until hot but not boiling. See [Bizewo's milk frothing guide](https://bizewohome.com/blogs/news/how-to-froth-milk-for-a-macchiato).
3. **Froth lightly.** Use a handheld frother, whisk, or French press to create a small amount of thick, fine foam. See [Bizewo's milk frothing guide](https://bizewohome.com/blogs/news/how-to-froth-milk-for-a-macchiato).
4. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the coffee into a small cup and add only a spoonful or two of foam on top. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-macchiato-the-easiest-most-delicious-recipe) and [Barista Coffee Roasters](https://cafebarista.ca/en/blogs/coffee/how-to-make-macchiato).

## Quick tips

- Whole milk often produces the best texture for macchiato foam. See [Bizewo's milk frothing guide](https://bizewohome.com/blogs/news/how-to-froth-milk-for-a-macchiato) and [CoffeeGeek's traditional macchiato guide](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/traditional-macchiato-how-to/).
- A traditional macchiato is much smaller and stronger than a latte, flat white, or cappuccino. See [CoffeeGeek's traditional macchiato guide](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/traditional-macchiato-how-to/) and [Caffe Society](https://www.caffesociety.co.uk/how-to-make-a-macchiato).
- The goal is to soften the espresso slightly, not cover it with lots of milk. See [CoffeeGeek's traditional macchiato guide](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/traditional-macchiato-how-to/) and this [milk steaming video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0vD--H7poxU).
