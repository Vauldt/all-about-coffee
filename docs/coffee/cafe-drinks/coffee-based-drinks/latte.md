# How to Make a Latte Coffee

A latte is made by combining espresso with steamed milk to create a smooth, creamy coffee drink. A typical latte uses about 2 ounces of espresso with the rest made up of steamed milk and a thin layer of foam.

## Basic ingredients

- 2 shots of espresso, or 3 to 4 ounces of strongly brewed coffee
- 6 to 8 ounces of milk
- Optional sweetener or flavouring

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Brew the espresso.** Pull two shots of espresso into your cup. See [Breville's latte guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-latte.html).
2. **Steam and froth the milk.** Pour milk into a frothing pitcher, place the steam wand just below the surface, and heat the milk until it is hot and silky. See [Breville's latte guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-latte.html).
3. **Remove large bubbles.** Tap the pitcher gently on the bench to settle the milk texture. See [Breville's latte guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-latte.html).
4. **Pour the milk into the espresso.** Pour steadily so the milk and coffee combine evenly. See [Breville's latte guide](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-make-a-latte.html) and this [YouTube latte tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UxwscVIZ8Qg).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Brew strong coffee.** Make a concentrated coffee as a substitute for espresso. A home method is described in [this homemade latte guide](https://www.forkinthekitchen.com/homemade-latte/) and [Coffee Hero's guide](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-classy-latte-at-home).
2. **Heat the milk.** Warm the milk until hot but not boiling. See [Allrecipes cafe latte recipe](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/96629/cafe-latte/) and [this homemade latte guide](https://www.forkinthekitchen.com/homemade-latte/).
3. **Froth the milk.** You can whisk it, blend it, or shake it in a sealed jar. Examples are shown in [Allrecipes cafe latte recipe](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/96629/cafe-latte/), [Boots & Hooves Homestead](https://bootsandhooveshomestead.com/how-to-make-homemade-lattes/), and [Coffee Hero's guide](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-classy-latte-at-home).
4. **Combine and finish.** Pour the coffee first, add the milk, then spoon foam on top. See [Allrecipes cafe latte recipe](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/96629/cafe-latte/) and [Boots & Hooves Homestead](https://bootsandhooveshomestead.com/how-to-make-homemade-lattes/).

## Quick tips

- Whole milk usually gives the creamiest texture and most stable foam, though other milks can also work well. See [Boots & Hooves Homestead](https://bootsandhooveshomestead.com/how-to-make-homemade-lattes/) and [this homemade latte guide](https://www.forkinthekitchen.com/homemade-latte/).
- A latte is typically milk-forward, so adjust the coffee-to-milk ratio if you want a stronger taste. See [Barista Courses Australia](https://baristacoursesaustralia.com.au/blog/how-to-make-a-latte-like-a-professional-barista/).
