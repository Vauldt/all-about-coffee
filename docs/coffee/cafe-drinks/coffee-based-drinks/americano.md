# How to Make an Americano

An Americano is made by combining espresso with hot water to create a longer black coffee with espresso character. It is usually served without milk, and many guides suggest a ratio of about 1 part espresso to 2 parts hot water, though the strength can be adjusted to taste.

## Basic ingredients

- 1 to 2 shots of espresso
- 90 to 200 ml of hot water, depending on how strong you want it
- Optional sugar

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Heat the water.** Use hot water just below boiling, or around 90 to 93 degrees Celsius if you want more control. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/americano-how-to/), [Coffeebroastery](https://www.coffeebroastery.com/Americano-what-kind-of-coffee-is-it-and-how-to-make-a-perfect-americano-at-home-blog-eng-1775211.html), and [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee).
2. **Add the hot water to the cup first.** Many guides recommend starting with water so the espresso can be poured over it and the crema is better preserved. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/americano-how-to/), [Ember](https://ember.com/blogs/degrees/recipe-how-to-make-americano-at-home), and [Taste of Artisan](https://tasteofartisan.com/how-to-make-americano/).
3. **Pull the espresso.** Brew one or two espresso shots directly over the hot water. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/americano-how-to/), [Ember](https://ember.com/blogs/degrees/recipe-how-to-make-americano-at-home), and this [Americano tutorial video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5aDqNwMVEyc).
4. **Adjust the strength.** Add more or less water depending on whether you want the drink bolder or lighter. See [GROSCHE](https://grosche.ca/blogs/blog/how-to-make-an-americano-hot-or-iced), [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee), and [Coffeebroastery](https://www.coffeebroastery.com/Americano-what-kind-of-coffee-is-it-and-how-to-make-a-perfect-americano-at-home-blog-eng-1775211.html).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make concentrated coffee.** Use a moka pot, AeroPress, or another strong brew method as your espresso substitute. See [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee), [A Couple Cooks](https://www.acouplecooks.com/iced-americano/), and [GROSCHE](https://grosche.ca/blogs/blog/how-to-make-an-americano-hot-or-iced).
2. **Heat the water.** Bring the water to just under boiling rather than a rolling boil. See [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee) and [Coffeebroastery](https://www.coffeebroastery.com/Americano-what-kind-of-coffee-is-it-and-how-to-make-a-perfect-americano-at-home-blog-eng-1775211.html).
3. **Combine water and coffee.** Pour the hot water into a cup, then add the concentrated coffee on top. See [Taste of Artisan](https://tasteofartisan.com/how-to-make-americano/), [GROSCHE](https://grosche.ca/blogs/blog/how-to-make-an-americano-hot-or-iced), and [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee).
4. **Taste and adjust.** Add more water if it tastes too intense, or reduce the water next time for a stronger cup. See [GROSCHE](https://grosche.ca/blogs/blog/how-to-make-an-americano-hot-or-iced) and [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee).

## Quick tips

- An Americano is usually smaller and more espresso-forward than a standard batch-brewed black coffee. See [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/americano-how-to/), [Taste of Artisan](https://tasteofartisan.com/how-to-make-americano/), and [Coffeebroastery](https://www.coffeebroastery.com/Americano-what-kind-of-coffee-is-it-and-how-to-make-a-perfect-americano-at-home-blog-eng-1775211.html).
- Common ratios range from 1:1 to 1:3 espresso to water, depending on taste and café style. See [GROSCHE](https://grosche.ca/blogs/blog/how-to-make-an-americano-hot-or-iced), [CoffeeGeek](https://coffeegeek.com/guides/howtos/americano-how-to/), and [Mystic Monk Coffee](https://www.mysticmonkcoffee.com/blogs/learn-about-coffee/how-to-make-americano-coffee).
- If you want an iced version, pour espresso over cold water and ice rather than adding milk. See [A Couple Cooks](https://www.acouplecooks.com/iced-americano/), [My Everyday Table](https://myeverydaytable.com/americano-coffee-hot-or-iced/), and [Barista Courses Australia](https://baristacoursesaustralia.com.au/blog/iced-americano-recipe-at-home/).
