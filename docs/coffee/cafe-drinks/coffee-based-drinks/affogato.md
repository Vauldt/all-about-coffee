# How to Make an Affogato

An affogato is a simple Italian coffee dessert made by pouring hot espresso over cold gelato or ice cream. A common starting point is 1 shot of espresso with 1 to 2 scoops of vanilla gelato or ice cream, served immediately so the hot-and-cold contrast is preserved.

## Basic ingredients

- 1 shot of hot espresso, or very strong coffee
- 1 to 2 scoops of vanilla gelato or vanilla ice cream
- Optional liqueur such as amaretto, Frangelico, or coffee liqueur
- Optional toppings such as chocolate shavings, cocoa, biscotti, or whipped cream

## Method

1. **Chill the serving glass if you like.** Some guides recommend chilling the cup or glass so the gelato stays cold a little longer. See [Espresso Outlet](https://espressooutlet.com/blogs/news/traditional-affogato-recipe-with-espresso) and [Thrifty Foods](https://www.thriftyfoods.com/articles/how-to-make-an-affogato).
2. **Scoop the gelato or ice cream.** Add 1 to 2 scoops of vanilla gelato or ice cream to a small glass, bowl, or cup. See [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/affogato/), [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/affogato-recipe/), and [Italian Affogato Recipe & Guide](https://italianstreetkitchen.com/au/isabellas-pantry/italian-desserts/italian-affogato/).
3. **Brew the espresso.** Pull a fresh shot of espresso just before serving, or use very strong hot coffee if you do not have an espresso machine. See [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/affogato-recipe-23058717), [Tripod Coffee](https://www.tripodcoffee.com.au/blogs/recipes/responsible-recipes-the-affogato), and [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/affogato-recipe/).
4. **Pour the espresso over the gelato.** Pour the hot espresso directly over the cold gelato or ice cream and serve straight away. See [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/affogato/), [Flavia](https://www.flavia.com/blogs/our-blog/espresso-affogato-recipe), and [Homemade Italian Cooking](https://www.homemadeitaliancooking.com/affogato-espresso-ice-cream/).
5. **Add optional extras.** If desired, finish with a splash of liqueur, grated chocolate, whipped cream, or biscotti. See [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/affogato/), [Inside The Rustic Kitchen](https://www.insidetherustickitchen.com/italian-affogato-recipe/), and [Espresso Outlet](https://espressooutlet.com/blogs/news/traditional-affogato-recipe-with-espresso).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make very strong coffee.** Brew a short, concentrated coffee using a moka pot, AeroPress, pod machine, or similar method. See [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/affogato-recipe/), [Tripod Coffee](https://www.tripodcoffee.com.au/blogs/recipes/responsible-recipes-the-affogato), and [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/affogato-guide-the-perfect-coffee-dessert).
2. **Use high-quality cold ice cream or gelato.** Keep it cold until the last minute so it does not melt too quickly. See [Inside The Rustic Kitchen](https://www.insidetherustickitchen.com/italian-affogato-recipe/), [Homemade Italian Cooking](https://www.homemadeitaliancooking.com/affogato-espresso-ice-cream/), and [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/affogato-guide-the-perfect-coffee-dessert).
3. **Assemble and serve immediately.** Scoop the gelato, pour the strong hot coffee over it, and eat it right away with a spoon. See [Homemade Italian Cooking](https://www.homemadeitaliancooking.com/affogato-espresso-ice-cream/), [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/affogato/), and [The Forked Spoon](https://theforkedspoon.com/affogato-recipe-espresso-ice-cream/).

## Quick tips

- Vanilla is the most common choice, but traditional versions may use fior di latte gelato for a purer dairy flavour. See [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/affogato-recipe/), [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/affogato-guide-the-perfect-coffee-dessert), and [Italian Affogato Recipe & Guide](https://italianstreetkitchen.com/au/isabellas-pantry/italian-desserts/italian-affogato/).
- Use short, strong coffee rather than a diluted long black so the dessert keeps its coffee intensity. See [Inside The Rustic Kitchen](https://www.insidetherustickitchen.com/italian-affogato-recipe/), [Tripod Coffee](https://www.tripodcoffee.com.au/blogs/recipes/responsible-recipes-the-affogato), and [Thrifty Foods](https://www.thriftyfoods.com/articles/how-to-make-an-affogato).
- Affogato is best enjoyed immediately, before the gelato melts fully into the espresso. See [Flavia](https://www.flavia.com/blogs/our-blog/espresso-affogato-recipe), [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/affogato/), and [The Forked Spoon](https://theforkedspoon.com/affogato-recipe-espresso-ice-cream/).
