# How to Make Vietnamese Iced Coffee (Cà Phê Sữa Đá)

Vietnamese iced coffee, or cà phê sữa đá, is made by slowly brewing strong coffee and combining it with sweetened condensed milk and ice. It is traditionally prepared with a Vietnamese phin filter, which produces a bold, concentrated coffee that balances the sweetness of the condensed milk.

## Basic ingredients

- 2 tablespoons Vietnamese ground coffee, or another dark roast ground coffee
- 1 to 3 tablespoons sweetened condensed milk
- 60 to 180 ml hot water, depending on the recipe and filter size
- Ice
- A Vietnamese phin filter, if available

## Method with a phin filter

1. **Add condensed milk to the cup.** Spoon sweetened condensed milk into the bottom of a glass or cup. See [Hungry Huy](https://www.hungryhuy.com/vietnamese-coffee-recipe-cafe-sua-da-recipe/), [Kitchen Confidante](https://kitchenconfidante.com/traditional-vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da-recipe), and [Steamy Kitchen](https://steamykitchen.com/492-vietnamese-iced-coffee.html).
2. **Load the phin filter.** Add the ground coffee to the filter chamber, level it out, and place the press gently on top. See [Kitchen Confidante](https://kitchenconfidante.com/traditional-vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da-recipe), [Nguyen Coffee Supply](https://nguyencoffeesupply.com/blogs/vietnamese-coffee-brew-guide/vietnamese-iced-coffee), and [Nam Coffee](https://www.nam.coffee/blogs/recipes/vietnamese-iced-coffee-recipe).
3. **Bloom the coffee.** Add a small amount of hot water first and wait about 20 to 30 seconds so the grounds can bloom. See [Delightful Plate](https://delightfulplate.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-cafe-sua-da/), [Nguyen Coffee Supply](https://nguyencoffeesupply.com/blogs/vietnamese-coffee-brew-guide/vietnamese-iced-coffee), and this [Vietnamese iced coffee video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fIRl4wfQrzg).
4. **Fill and brew slowly.** Add the rest of the hot water, cover the phin, and let the coffee drip slowly into the cup. Brewing commonly takes about 3 to 10 minutes depending on the filter and grind. See [Kitchen Confidante](https://kitchenconfidante.com/traditional-vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da-recipe), [Steamy Kitchen](https://steamykitchen.com/492-vietnamese-iced-coffee.html), [Nguyen Coffee Supply](https://nguyencoffeesupply.com/blogs/vietnamese-coffee-brew-guide/vietnamese-iced-coffee), and [MICHELIN Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/features/iconic-dishes-a-guide-to-vietnamese-coffee).
5. **Stir with the condensed milk.** Once brewing is finished, remove the filter and stir well so the coffee and condensed milk combine fully. See [Steamy Kitchen](https://steamykitchen.com/492-vietnamese-iced-coffee.html), [Hungry Huy](https://www.hungryhuy.com/vietnamese-coffee-recipe-cafe-sua-da-recipe/), and [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/77768/vietnamese-iced-coffee/).
6. **Pour over ice.** Fill a tall glass with ice and pour the sweetened coffee over it, then stir again if needed. See [Kitchen Confidante](https://kitchenconfidante.com/traditional-vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da-recipe), [Delightful Plate](https://delightfulplate.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-cafe-sua-da/), and [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/77768/vietnamese-iced-coffee/).

## Method without a phin filter

1. **Make very strong coffee.** Brew a concentrated coffee using an AeroPress, moka pot, espresso machine, or another strong brew method. See [Ethnic Spoon](https://ethnicspoon.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da/), [My Cooking Journey](https://www.mycookingjourney.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da/), and [Lord Lucy](https://www.lordlucy.com/post/vietnamese-iced-coffee).
2. **Add condensed milk first.** Place the condensed milk in the serving glass before adding the coffee. See [Hungry Huy](https://www.hungryhuy.com/vietnamese-coffee-recipe-cafe-sua-da-recipe/), [My Cooking Journey](https://www.mycookingjourney.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da/), and [Ethnic Spoon](https://ethnicspoon.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da/).
3. **Mix and chill.** Stir the coffee and condensed milk together, then pour over ice. See [My Cooking Journey](https://www.mycookingjourney.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da/), [Ethnic Spoon](https://ethnicspoon.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-ca-phe-sua-da/), and [Lord Lucy](https://www.lordlucy.com/post/vietnamese-iced-coffee).

## Quick tips

- Traditional Vietnamese coffee is usually bold and dark, often using robusta-heavy blends for a strong, punchy flavour. See [MICHELIN Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/features/iconic-dishes-a-guide-to-vietnamese-coffee) and [Nguyen Coffee Supply](https://nguyencoffeesupply.com/blogs/vietnamese-coffee-brew-guide/vietnamese-iced-coffee).
- If the coffee drips too quickly through the phin, use a finer grind or tighten the press slightly. If it stalls, loosen it a little. See [Steamy Kitchen](https://steamykitchen.com/492-vietnamese-iced-coffee.html) and [Nguyen Coffee Supply](https://nguyencoffeesupply.com/blogs/vietnamese-coffee-brew-guide/vietnamese-iced-coffee).
- You can adjust the sweetness by changing the condensed milk amount, but the drink should stay rich, sweet, and strongly coffee-forward. See [Hungry Huy](https://www.hungryhuy.com/vietnamese-coffee-recipe-cafe-sua-da-recipe/), [Delightful Plate](https://delightfulplate.com/vietnamese-iced-coffee-cafe-sua-da/), and [MICHELIN Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/features/iconic-dishes-a-guide-to-vietnamese-coffee).
