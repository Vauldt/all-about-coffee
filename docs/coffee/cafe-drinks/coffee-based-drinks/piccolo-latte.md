# How to Make a Piccolo Latte

A piccolo latte is a small milk coffee made with a single espresso or ristretto shot and a small amount of steamed milk. It is usually served in a 90 to 120 ml glass, with a higher coffee-to-milk ratio than a standard latte and a thin layer of silky microfoam on top.

## Basic ingredients

- 1 shot of espresso or ristretto, usually about 20 to 30 ml
- About 40 to 60 ml of steamed milk
- A thin layer of microfoam
- Optional sugar, though it is often served without it

## Method with an espresso machine

1. **Brew the espresso.** Pull a single shot of espresso or ristretto into a small glass. See [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/03/what-is-a-piccolo-latte-how-do-i-make-it/), [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte), and [White Horse Coffee](https://whitehorsecoffee.com.au/blogs/blog/make-enjoy-piccolo-latte).
2. **Steam the milk.** Steam a small amount of milk until it becomes silky and glossy, with fine microfoam rather than thick froth. See [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/03/what-is-a-piccolo-latte-how-do-i-make-it/), [Breville NZ's piccolo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lma0g8oireQ), and [Barista Courses Australia](https://baristacoursesaustralia.com.au/blog/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte/).
3. **Keep the drink small and balanced.** A piccolo latte is designed to be stronger than a latte because it uses less milk in a smaller glass. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte), [Artisti Coffee Roasters](https://artisticoffee.com/blogs/training/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte-macchiato-a-long-macchiato), and [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/03/what-is-a-piccolo-latte-how-do-i-make-it/).
4. **Pour and finish.** Pour the milk steadily into the espresso so it blends smoothly and leaves a thin cap of microfoam on top. See [Breville NZ's piccolo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lma0g8oireQ), [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/03/what-is-a-piccolo-latte-how-do-i-make-it/), and [White Horse Coffee](https://whitehorsecoffee.com.au/blogs/blog/make-enjoy-piccolo-latte).

## Method without an espresso machine

1. **Make strong coffee.** Use a moka pot or AeroPress to brew a concentrated coffee if you do not have an espresso machine. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte) and [Nespresso's piccolo recipe](https://www.nespresso.com/au/en/coffee-recipes/piccolo).
2. **Heat the milk.** Warm a small amount of milk until hot but not boiling. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte) and [Wirsh](https://mywirsh.com/blogs/coffee-facts/piccolo-vs-latte-what-s-the-difference).
3. **Froth lightly.** Use a frother, whisk, or French press to create silky milk with a small amount of microfoam. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte) and [Wirsh](https://mywirsh.com/blogs/coffee-facts/piccolo-vs-latte-what-s-the-difference).
4. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the coffee into a small glass, add the milk, and leave a little microfoam on top. See [Artisti Coffee Roasters](https://artisticoffee.com/blogs/training/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte-macchiato-a-long-macchiato), [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte), and this [piccolo preparation video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QJCRPHRmDPI).

## Quick tips

- A piccolo latte is usually served in a 90 ml glass, though some cafés use glasses up to around 120 ml. See [White Horse Coffee](https://whitehorsecoffee.com.au/blogs/blog/make-enjoy-piccolo-latte), [Artisti Coffee Roasters](https://artisticoffee.com/blogs/training/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte-macchiato-a-long-macchiato), and [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte).
- The milk texture should be similar to latte milk, but the drink itself should be smaller and more espresso-forward. See [Coffee Hero](https://coffeehero.com.au/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte), [Breville NZ's piccolo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lma0g8oireQ), and [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/03/what-is-a-piccolo-latte-how-do-i-make-it/).
- If you want a sweeter and more balanced cup, a full espresso shot with carefully controlled milk can work better than too much milk. See [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/03/what-is-a-piccolo-latte-how-do-i-make-it/) and [Barista Courses Australia](https://baristacoursesaustralia.com.au/blog/how-to-make-a-piccolo-latte/).
