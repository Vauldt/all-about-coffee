# Top 20 Cafe Coffee Drinks

### Top Cafe Coffee Drinks

Here's a clean breakdown of the top 20 cafe coffee drinks, drawing on global popularity data, NCA consumption surveys, and specialty coffee ordering patterns:

## Espresso-Based Classics
|#|Drink|What it is|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Latte**|1–2 shots espresso, ~150–200ml steamed milk, thin microfoam layer. The world's most-ordered espresso drink by volume.|
|2|**Cappuccino**|Equal thirds: espresso, steamed milk, thick foam. Most popular drink globally by country count.|
|3|**Flat White**|Double ristretto, ~120ml steamed milk, very thin microfoam. Originating in Australia/NZ; now mainstream worldwide.|
|4|**Espresso**|25–30ml concentrated shot, no milk. Second most popular drink globally; foundation of every espresso-based drink.|
|5|**Americano / Long Black**|Espresso diluted with hot water. Americano = water first; Long Black = espresso over water (preserves crema).|
|6|**Macchiato**|Espresso "stained" with a small amount of foamed milk. Distinct from the sweetened caramel versions popularised by chains.|
|7|**Cortado**|1:1 espresso to steamed milk (~60ml total). Cuts the acidity without adding significant milk volume.|
|8|**Mocha**|Espresso, chocolate syrup or powder, steamed milk, usually topped with whipped cream.|
|9|**Piccolo Latte**|Ristretto in a small glass with ~90ml steamed milk — a compact, intense flat white variant.|

## Milk-Free & Filter

|#|Drink|What it is|
|---|---|---|
|10|**Filter / Drip Coffee**|Brewed through paper filter (V60, Chemex, batch brew). Third most-ordered style by cafe survey.|
|11|**Cold Brew**|Coffee steeped in cold water 12–24 hours. Smooth, low-acid, high caffeine. One of the fastest-growing cafe formats.|
|12|**Iced Coffee**|Brewed coffee or espresso served over ice, often with milk. Broad category; umbrella for many variants.|
|13|**Nitro Cold Brew**|Cold brew infused with nitrogen, served on tap. Creamy texture, no milk required.|

## Specialty & Trending

|#|Drink|What it is|
|---|---|---|
|14|**Affogato**|Single or double espresso poured over a scoop of vanilla gelato. Dessert-coffee hybrid; on menus across specialty and mainstream cafes.|
|15|**Espresso Tonic**|Shot of espresso over tonic water and ice. Bitter, effervescent, popular in third-wave cafes.|
|16|**Iced Latte**|Espresso over cold milk and ice — not to be confused with cold brew. One of the most-ordered drinks at chain cafes.|
|17|**Cafe au Lait**|Brewed filter coffee with hot steamed or scalded milk (~1:1). French origin; common in New Orleans and European cafes.|
|18|**Vietnamese Iced Coffee (Ca Phe Sua Da)**|Strong drip coffee through a phin filter, condensed milk, ice. Now a fixture on specialty cafe menus globally.|
|19|**Dalgona / Whipped Coffee**|Instant coffee, sugar, and water whipped to a foam, spooned over cold milk. Went viral in 2020 and has stayed on menus.|
|20|**Matcha Latte**|Technically not coffee, but now a permanent fixture on nearly every cafe menu as a non-coffee alternative, often ordered alongside espresso drinks.|
