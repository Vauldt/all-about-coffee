# How to Make a Matcha Latte

A matcha latte is made by whisking matcha powder with hot water, then combining it with warm or cold milk. A common starting point is about 1/2 to 1 1/2 teaspoons of matcha with water around 80°C, followed by milk and optional sweetener.

## Basic ingredients

- 1/2 to 1 1/2 teaspoons matcha powder
- 30 to 100 ml hot water, ideally around 80°C
- 150 to 200 ml milk of your choice
- Optional sweetener such as honey, maple syrup, sugar, or vanilla
- Ice, optional for an iced version

## Method

1. **Sift the matcha.** Sifting helps remove clumps and makes the drink smoother. See [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/matcha-latte/), [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/), and [Downshiftology](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/matcha-latte/).
2. **Add hot water.** Pour a small amount of hot water over the matcha. Many guides recommend water around 80°C because boiling water can make matcha taste bitter. See [T2 Tea](https://www.t2tea.com/blogs/how-to-brew/how-to-make-a-matcha-latte), [Ippodo Tea](https://ippodotea.com/blogs/recipes-to-brew-japanese-tea/matcha-latte-recipe), and [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/).
3. **Whisk until smooth and lightly foamy.** Use a bamboo whisk, small whisk, or frother, and whisk in a zig-zag, W, or M motion rather than a circular stir. See [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/matcha-latte/), [T2 Tea](https://www.t2tea.com/blogs/how-to-brew/how-to-make-a-matcha-latte), [Ippodo Tea](https://ippodotea.com/blogs/recipes-to-brew-japanese-tea/matcha-latte-recipe), and [Downshiftology](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/matcha-latte/).
4. **Heat or froth the milk.** Warm the milk gently and froth it if you want a café-style texture, or use cold milk and ice for an iced version. See [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/), [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/matcha-latte), [Oh, How Civilized](https://www.ohhowcivilized.com/matcha-latte/), and this [matcha latte video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOnwuHhLNSo).
5. **Combine and sweeten.** Pour the milk into the whisked matcha, then add sweetener if desired and stir well. See [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/matcha-latte/), [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/matcha-latte), [Naoki Matcha](https://naokimatcha.com/blogs/recipes/our-classic-matcha-latte-recipe), and [Pure Matcha Australia](https://purematcha.com.au/blogs/matcha-recipes/green-tea-matcha-latte).

## Method without special matcha tools

1. **Use a mug and small whisk or frother.** A matcha bowl and bamboo whisk are useful, but a mug and handheld frother can also work well. See [T2 Tea](https://www.t2tea.com/blogs/how-to-brew/how-to-make-a-matcha-latte), [Oh, How Civilized](https://www.ohhowcivilized.com/matcha-latte/), and [Pure Matcha Australia](https://purematcha.com.au/blogs/matcha-recipes/green-tea-matcha-latte).
2. **Make a smooth paste first if needed.** Some recipes recommend starting with a little water to make a paste before thinning it out, which helps avoid lumps. See [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/matcha-latte), [A Cozy Kitchen](https://www.acozykitchen.com/matcha-iced-latte), and [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/matcha-latte/).
3. **Froth milk separately if you want more texture.** A French press or handheld frother can add body to the milk without special café equipment. See [Oh, How Civilized](https://www.ohhowcivilized.com/matcha-latte/), [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/), and [Pure Matcha Australia](https://purematcha.com.au/blogs/matcha-recipes/green-tea-matcha-latte).

## Quick tips

- Good-quality matcha makes a major difference, especially in a simple milk drink where the tea flavour is exposed. See [Tezumi](https://www.tezumi.com/blogs/tezumi-insights/a-better-matcha-latte-our-recipe), [Naoki Matcha](https://naokimatcha.com/blogs/recipes/our-classic-matcha-latte-recipe), and [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/).
- If the latte tastes bitter, try slightly cooler water or a little more milk or sweetener. See [T2 Tea](https://www.t2tea.com/blogs/how-to-brew/how-to-make-a-matcha-latte), [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/), and [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/matcha-latte/).
- Oat milk and dairy milk are common choices for a creamy result, but many plant milks work well. See [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/matcha-latte/), [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/matcha-latte), and [Pure Matcha Australia](https://purematcha.com.au/blogs/matcha-recipes/green-tea-matcha-latte).
