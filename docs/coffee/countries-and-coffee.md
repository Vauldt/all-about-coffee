# Countries and Coffee

## EX 1

Write a note about India. 
The note is to have sections on: 
1. General Introduction to India, where it is, its history, its people, its neighbours, its terrain and its major population centres 
2. List its Coffee Regions and their Terroir 
3. Discuss the Major coffee varieties grown. 
4. How coffee is farmed and processed. 
5. The quality of its coffee 
6. The most popular coffee drinks in this country
7. The major market for its coffee. 
8. Anything else of note about the region.

Also write a file on each of the country's coffee growing regions, using the same structure as that used for the parent country file.

Write an MOC for the country.
