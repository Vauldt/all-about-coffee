# Coffee Social Media Manager / Content Creator

A **Coffee Social Media Manager** or **Coffee Content Creator** builds and maintains a brand's or individual's digital presence through visual storytelling, community engagement, and platform-specific content strategy. In the coffee industry, this role has become central to how roasters, cafés, equipment brands, and individual baristas connect with their audiences.

## Role Overview

Social media and content roles in coffee range from in-house positions at roasters and café groups to freelance creators who have built independent followings through platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. The role combines creative skills — photography, videography, writing — with analytical capability to measure performance and refine content strategies over time.

## Key Responsibilities

- Planning, creating, and scheduling content across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest)
- Photography and videography of coffee products, brewing processes, and café environments
- Writing captions, blog posts, newsletters, and short-form copy aligned with brand voice
- Community management — responding to comments, DMs, and engaging with followers
- Tracking analytics (reach, engagement, conversion) and reporting on performance
- Collaborating with Coffee Brand Manager / Marketing Manager on campaigns and product launches
- Identifying and managing influencer partnerships or ambassador programmes
- Staying current with platform algorithm changes and emerging content formats

## Skills and Knowledge

- Proficiency with photography and video editing tools (Adobe Lightroom, Premiere Pro, CapCut, Final Cut Pro)
- Understanding of platform-specific best practices — aspect ratios, posting cadence, hashtag strategy
- Visual aesthetics sensibility aligned with the coffee industry's design culture
- Strong written communication and storytelling ability
- Coffee product knowledge — brewing methods, equipment, and origin stories — lending authenticity to content
- Familiarity with social media analytics tools (Meta Business Suite, Later, Sprout Social)

## Qualifications and Pathways

Formal qualifications are less prescribed in this role than in others; a demonstrated portfolio and audience growth are the primary credentials. Pathways include:

- Degrees in Communications, Marketing, Graphic Design, or Film/Media
- A self-built social media presence with a demonstrable coffee niche audience
- Barista or roastery background providing authentic product knowledge
- Short courses in digital marketing, content strategy, or photography

## Content Formats in Coffee

Some of the most engaging content formats specific to the coffee industry include:

- **Brewing tutorials** — pour-over, AeroPress, and espresso dialling-in walkthroughs
- **Origin stories** — farm visits, producer profiles, and processing method explanations
- **Roast profiles** — behind-the-scenes roastery content
- **Latte art** — a perennially high-engagement visual format on Instagram and TikTok
- **Equipment reviews** — unboxing, comparison, and long-term use assessments
- **Day-in-the-life** — documenting barista, roaster, or buyer workflows

## Industry Context

The [Specialty Coffee](https://publish.obsidian.md/coffeepedia/Specialty+Coffee) community has a highly active and visually engaged online presence. Platforms like Instagram have been instrumental in driving consumer interest in origin, brewing craft, and café culture. Independent coffee creators — sometimes called "coffeetubers" or coffee influencers — have carved out significant audiences, working with brands on sponsored content, product collaborations, and affiliate programmes. Events like the [World Barista Championship](https://worldbaristachampionship.org) and [World Brewers Cup](https://worldbrewerscup.org) generate substantial content opportunities around competition seasons.
