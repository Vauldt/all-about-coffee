# Coffee Educator / Workshop Facilitator

A **Coffee Educator** or **Coffee Workshop Facilitator** designs and delivers structured learning experiences that build coffee knowledge and practical skills. They operate across a wide range of contexts — from accredited SCA training centres and roastery academies to public consumer workshops, corporate team-building events, and hospitality onboarding programmes.

## Role Overview

Coffee education has grown into a distinct professional discipline as the specialty coffee industry has matured. Educators may work in-house for a roaster or café group, independently as freelance facilitators, or within formally accredited training providers. The role demands both deep subject matter expertise and the pedagogical skill to translate complex technical knowledge into accessible, engaging learning experiences for diverse audiences.

## Key Responsibilities

- Designing training curricula for barista, brewing, sensory, and roasting subjects
- Delivering hands-on workshops, classroom sessions, and one-on-one coaching
- Assessing learner competency and providing structured feedback
- Developing educational materials — workbooks, recipe cards, sensory lexicons, slide decks
- Facilitating accredited SCA examinations and assessments
- Running consumer workshops such as brewing masterclasses, cupping sessions, and origin tastings
- Adapting content for diverse audiences — novice consumers, experienced baristas, and corporate clients
- Staying current with industry developments, research, and best practices

## Skills and Knowledge

- Expert-level practical skills across espresso, filter, and alternative brewing methods
- Deep knowledge of Coffee Sensory Evaluation, [Coffee Roasting](../coffee-roasting/coffee-roasting.md), and [Green Coffee](../green-coffee-beans/green-coffee.md)
- Adult learning principles and facilitation techniques
- Strong verbal communication, public speaking, and group management skills
- Curriculum design and instructional materials development
- Patience, adaptability, and the ability to read a room across different audience levels

## Qualifications and Pathways

- [SCA Coffee Skills Programme](https://sca.coffee/education) — Barista Skills, Brewing, Sensory, Green Coffee, Roasting (all at Professional or Trainer level)
- [SCA Authorised Trainer (AT)](https://sca.coffee/education/authorised-trainers) or [Authorised SCA Campus](https://sca.coffee/education/campuses) status for delivering accredited courses
- [Q Grader Certification](https://www.coffeeinstitute.org/our-work/q/q-arabica/) — highly respected credential for sensory-focused educators
- Certificate IV in Training and Assessment (TAE40116) — required in Australia for nationally recognised training delivery
- Barista competition experience (e.g., [World Barista Championship](https://worldbaristachampionship.org)) is a strong professional credential

## Workshop Types

Coffee educators facilitate a broad range of workshop formats:

- **Barista fundamentals** — espresso technique, milk texturing, [Latte Art](../coffee-brewing-espresso/latte-art.md)
- **Brew bar workshops** — pour-over, AeroPress, Chemex, cold brew
- **Cupping and sensory** — introduction to the [SCA Cupping Protocol](../coffee-tasting/sca-cupping-protocol.md), flavour wheel navigation
- **Origin and processing** — understanding Coffee Processing methods and their sensory outcomes
- **Roast profiling** — for aspiring roasters or roastery production staff
- **Consumer experiences** — café or event-based tastings for the general public

## Industry Context

The proliferation of [SCA-accredited training centres](https://sca.coffee/education/campuses) globally has professionalised coffee education, creating a structured credential pathway. In Australia, cities like Melbourne and Sydney host numerous independent coffee schools and roastery academies offering everything from introductory sessions to full Q Grader preparation courses. Corporate coffee experiences have also become a growing revenue stream for educators, as businesses seek premium hospitality and team engagement activities.
