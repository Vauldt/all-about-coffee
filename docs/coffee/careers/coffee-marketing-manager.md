# Coffee Brand Manager / Marketing Manager

A **Coffee Brand Manager** or **Coffee Marketing Manager** is responsible for shaping and communicating the identity, positioning, and commercial narrative of a coffee brand. They bridge the gap between the product — often deeply rooted in origin, craft, and quality — and the consumer, using strategy, creative direction, and data-led insight to drive awareness and loyalty.

## Role Overview

This role exists across the full spectrum of the coffee industry — from small independent roasters building direct-to-consumer identities, to large multinational companies managing global brand portfolios. Brand and marketing managers work closely with product development, sales, design, and digital teams to ensure a coherent brand experience across every touchpoint.

## Key Responsibilities

- Developing and executing brand strategy, positioning, and messaging frameworks
- Managing product launches, seasonal campaigns, and promotional calendars
- Overseeing creative direction for packaging, visual identity, and retail presence
- Commissioning and interpreting consumer research and market analysis
- Managing brand budgets and reporting on marketing ROI
- Working with Coffee Social Media Manager / Content Creator on digital content strategy
- Coordinating with wholesale and retail partners on co-branded activations
- Representing the brand at trade events such as [World of Coffee](https://worldofcoffee.org) and the [Melbourne International Coffee Expo (MICE)](https://internationalcoffeeexpo.com.au)

## Skills and Knowledge

- Strong understanding of brand architecture, positioning, and consumer behaviour
- Familiarity with the [Specialty Coffee](https://publish.obsidian.md/coffeepedia/Specialty+Coffee) landscape and its distinct values — transparency, traceability, and craft
- Proficiency in marketing analytics platforms and CRM systems
- Creative briefing and agency management experience
- Knowledge of packaging regulations and sustainability labelling
- Ability to translate complex coffee origin stories into compelling consumer narratives

## Qualifications and Pathways

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field
- Experience working within food and beverage or FMCG marketing
- Hands-on coffee knowledge (barista experience, SCA training) is a strong advantage
- Postgraduate qualifications in marketing (MBA, MPRD) are beneficial for senior roles

## Industry Context

Marketing in the specialty coffee sector requires a nuanced understanding of the category's values. Unlike mass-market coffee, specialty brands often lead with origin stories, producer relationships, and processing methods as key differentiators. Effective brand managers must authentically communicate concepts such as [Direct Trade](../specialty-coffee/direct-trade.md), Single Origin, and [Traceability](../specialty-coffee/traceability.md) to an increasingly informed consumer base. The rise of subscription coffee models and direct-to-consumer e-commerce has also expanded the digital marketing demands on brand teams.
