## Key Facts

- Espresso grinders require fine, precise stepless adjustment, low retention, and consistency at very fine settings
- Filter grinders prioritise grind clarity and range across medium to coarse settings
- Manual grinders deliver exceptional burr quality relative to price and are well suited to travel and single-serve use
- Commercial grinders use large burrs (64 mm+) and powerful motors for high-volume continuous operation
