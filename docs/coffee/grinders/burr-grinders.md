# OVERVIEW OF BURR GRINDERS

## What is a burr grinder?

A **burr grinder** is a coffee grinder that crushes coffee beans between **two precisely machined burrs** set at a fixed distance. The gap between the burrs determines the **grind size**, producing far more **uniform particles** than blade grinders.

Burr grinders are the **industry standard** for specialty coffee.

---

## Why burr grinders matter

Extraction quality depends heavily on **particle size consistency**.

Burr grinders provide:

- Predictable extraction
    
- Better flavour balance
    
- Improved clarity and sweetness
    
- Repeatable results across brews
    

They reduce the two biggest problems in grinding:

- **Boulders** (under-extracted sourness)
    
- **Fines** (over-extracted bitterness)
    

---

## How burr grinders work

1. Beans enter the grinding chamber
    
2. Beans are **crushed (not chopped)** between burrs
    
3. Ground coffee exits only when particles are small enough
    
4. Adjusting burr spacing changes grind size
    

Grinding is controlled by **geometry and spacing**, not time.

---

## The two main burr types

---

## 1. Conical burr grinders

### Design

- Cone-shaped inner burr
    
- Ring-shaped outer burr
    
- Beans fall through by gravity
    

### Grinding behaviour

- Slightly wider particle size distribution
    
- Fewer ultra-fine particles
    
- Slower RPM, lower heat
    

### Flavour tendency

- Fuller body
    
- Rounded sweetness
    
- Softer acidity
    

### Typical uses

- Home grinders
    
- All-purpose brewing
    
- Espresso + filter in one grinder
    

---

## 2. Flat burr grinders

### Design

- Two flat, parallel discs
    
- Beans ground outward centrifugally
    

### Grinding behaviour

- Narrower particle distribution
    
- Higher uniformity
    
- More sensitive to alignment and burr condition
    

### Flavour tendency

- Higher clarity
    
- Brighter acidity
    
- Greater flavour separation
    

### Typical uses

- Specialty cafés
    
- Filter-focused brewing
    
- Precision espresso
    

---

## Burr materials

|Material|Characteristics|
|---|---|
|Steel|Durable, neutral flavour|
|Ceramic|Low heat, brittle|
|Coated steel|Long life, consistent edges|

Burr sharpness and geometry matter more than material alone.

---

## Adjustment mechanisms

### Stepped

- Click-based settings
    
- Easy repeatability
    
- Slightly less precision
    

### Stepless

- Infinite micro-adjustment
    
- Essential for espresso tuning
    
- Requires more skill to repeat settings
    

---

## Manual vs electric burr grinders

### Manual burr grinders

- Excellent grind quality per dollar
    
- Quiet and portable
    
- Limited speed and capacity
    

**Best for:** travel, single-cup brewing, teaching fundamentals

### Electric burr grinders

- Fast and scalable
    
- Required for espresso workflows
    
- Greater heat management considerations
    

**Best for:** daily home use, cafés, volume brewing

---

## Burr grinders by brew method

|Brew Method|Grinder Requirement|
|---|---|
|Espresso|High precision, micro-adjustment|
|Pour-over|Consistency, low fines|
|French press|Burr grinder capable of coarse grind|
|Cold brew|Wide burr spacing, low fines|
|Teaching|Visible consistency, repeatability|

---

## Common misconceptions

- “Finer grind = better coffee” → **False**
    
- “Expensive brewer matters more than grinder” → **False**
    
- “All burr grinders are the same” → **False**
    

Grind quality often matters **more than the brewing device**.

---

## Teaching summary

Burr grinders are essential because they control **particle size and uniformity**, which directly govern extraction.

- **Conical burrs** favour forgiveness and body
    
- **Flat burrs** favour precision and clarity
    

In most setups, **investing in a better burr grinder improves coffee more than upgrading any other piece of equipment**.
