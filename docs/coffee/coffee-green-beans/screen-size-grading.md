# Screen Size Grading

**Screen Size Grading**
Classification system based on bean physical size. Uses perforated metal screens with specific hole sizes. Beans sorted by which screens they pass through. Measured in 1/64-inch increments (screens 10-20). Common in international coffee trade.
**Screen Measurement**
**Screen Number**
Represents hole size in 1/64-inch increments. Screen 16 = 16/64 inch = 6.35mm holes. Screen 18 = 18/64 inch = 7.14mm holes. Higher number = larger beans. Range typically 10-20.
**Sorting Process**
Stacked screens, largest holes on top. Green coffee poured into top screen. Shaken/vibrated to sort. Beans remain on screen they can't pass through. Multiple sizes separated simultaneously.
**Measurement System**
North American system (64ths of inch). European system (millimeters). Conversion: Screen number × 0.397 = mm. Example: Screen 16 = 6.35mm, Screen 18 = 7.14mm.
**Common Screen Sizes**
**Screen 20 (7.94mm)**
Extra-large beans. Rare, specialty designation. Maragogipe (elephant beans). Premium pricing sometimes. Limited commercial significance.
**Screen 19 (7.54mm)**
Very large beans. "Supremo" in Colombian grading. Quality indicator (usually). Premium grade. Limited supply typical.
**Screen 18 (7.14mm)**
Large beans. "AA" in Kenya. "Supremo" threshold Colombia. High quality association. Consistent roasting. Desirable commercial size.
**Screen 17 (6.75mm)**
Large-medium beans. "AB" range in Kenya. Colombian "Excelso." Good quality. Most common premium size. Roasts evenly.
**Screen 16 (6.35mm)**
Medium beans. Lower end AB Kenya. Acceptable quality. Common size. Standard commercial. Good for blends.
**Screen 15 (5.95mm)**
Medium-small beans. Lower grading tier. "C" grade in some systems. Still usable. Lower price. Blend component.
**Screen 14 (5.56mm)**
Small beans. Lower quality association. Challenges in roasting. Faster roasting. Often separated out. Budget commercial.
**Screen 13 and below**
Very small beans. "Peaberries" possible. Often defective or immature. Usually separated/discarded. Low commercial value. Exception: Intentional peaberry separation.
**Regional Grading Systems**
**Kenya (Screen-Based)**
- AA: Screen 17-18 (6.75-7.14mm)
- AB: Screen 15-16 (5.95-6.35mm)
- C: Below screen 15
- E (Elephant): Extra large, rare
- PB (Peaberry): Round beans, separated
- TT/T: Lower grades, broken, defects
**Colombia (Screen-Based)**
- Supremo: Screen 17+ (6.75mm+)
- Excelso: Screen 14-16 (5.56-6.35mm)
- UGQ (Usual Good Quality): Mix of sizes
Supremo commands premium over Excelso.
**Tanzania (Similar to Kenya)**
- AA: Largest beans
- A: Medium-large
- B: Medium
- C: Small
- PB: Peaberry
Screen-based like Kenya system.
**Size vs Quality Relationship**
**General Correlation**
Larger beans often (not always) indicate better quality. High-altitude, slow maturation creates density and size. Better development generally. Exceptions exist.
**Density More Important**
Size alone insufficient. Dense small bean > less dense large bean. Altitude and growing conditions matter more. Screen size is convenience, not quality guarantee.
**Even Roasting**
Main practical benefit of screening. Uniform sizes roast evenly. Mixed sizes problematic (small beans burn while large under-roast). Consistency crucial for roasting.
**Market Perception**
Larger beans command premiums. Not always justified by cup quality. Marketing and tradition drive pricing. Informed buyers look beyond size.
**Screen Grading Limitations**
**Doesn't Measure Quality**
Size ≠ quality necessarily. Defects can be any size. Doesn't assess flavor. No processing information. No defect detection. Complementary grading needed.
**Doesn't Measure Density**
Dense small bean vs. large hollow bean. Density more important for quality. Separate density grading exists. Screen sizing misses this critical factor.
**Peaberry Separation**
Round peaberries don't screen like flat beans. Mixed in with regular beans. Requires separate sorting. Shape matters as much as size.
**Variable by Origin**
Different origins have different typical sizes. Ethiopian heirloom: Smaller beans typical. Maragogipe variety: Naturally huge. Context matters for interpretation.
**Density Grading Complement**
**Gravity Separation**
Uses air or water to separate by density. Dense beans sink/resist air. Light beans float/blow away. More accurate quality indicator. Often combined with screen sizing.
**Electronic Sorting**
Modern optical sorters. Detect color, size, density, defects. More sophisticated than screens. Expensive equipment. High-end processing.
**Combined Approach**
Screen + density + visual + cupping. Multiple quality checks. No single system sufficient. Comprehensive grading essential. Commercial coffee standard.
**Practical Applications**
**Roasting Consistency**
Primary reason for screen grading. Uniform size = even roasting. Mixed sizes create problems. Small beans scorch, large under-develop. Quality control essential.
**Commercial Trading**
Standard commodity trading specification. Contracts specify screen sizes. Price differentials by size. International trade requirement. Communication across supply chain.
**Blending**
Different screen sizes for different purposes. Larger for single origin showcase. Smaller for blend components. Economic efficiency. Quality/cost optimization.
