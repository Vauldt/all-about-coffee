## Overview

Coffee quality and grading encompasses the physical and sensory standards used to evaluate and classify green and roasted coffee. Physical grading systems — defect counts, screen sizes, bean density, and altitude designations — vary by origin and enable international trade. Sensory evaluation via cupping and the 100-point SCA scale provides an objective measure of cup quality independent of physical grade. For origin-specific grading systems, see [Coffee Qualities](coffee-qualities.md); for cupping methodology, see [Cupping](../tasting-coffee/cupping.md).
