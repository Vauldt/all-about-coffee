## Overview

Coffee terroir refers to the combination of environmental, botanical, and human factors that give a coffee from a specific place its characteristic flavour. The principal terroir components — altitude, soil, climate, variety, and processing tradition — interact to produce the flavour profiles that distinguish coffees from different origins. For comprehensive coverage of terroir science and regional profiles, see [Key Concepts in Terroir](key-concepts-in-terroir.md) and the linked articles below.
