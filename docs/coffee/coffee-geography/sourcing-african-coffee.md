## Common Pitfalls

**Kenyan AA premium** — AB is the same cup quality at a lower price (£6–10 vs £8–15). The difference is screen size, not flavour.

**Generic "Ethiopian"** — Without a region and washing station name, provenance and cup profile cannot be verified. Always look for specifics like "Yirgacheffe, Koke Washing Station."

**Ignoring freshness** — African coffees, particularly Ethiopian florals, fade quickly. Floral and bright notes disappear rapidly after roast and after the green crop ages. New crop matters.

**Yemen counterfeiting** — Ethiopian natural-processed coffees are sometimes sold as Yemeni due to profile similarities. Verify sourcing through established importers with direct relationships.

**Overlooking Burundi** — Consistently undervalued; terroir and cup quality are similar to Rwanda at lower prices.
