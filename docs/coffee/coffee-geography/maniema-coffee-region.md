# Maniema Coffee Region

**Tags:** #coffee/geography #coffee/geography/africa #coffee/geography/central-africa #coffee/geography/drc
**Aliases:** Maniema coffee, Shabunda coffee DRC, East-central DRC coffee

**Status:** ✅ Complete

## Overview

Maniema is an east-central DRC province occupying the transition zone between the Albertine Rift highlands and the Congo basin, at altitudes of 700–1,400 metres. Coffee production here — a mix of Arabica at higher elevations and Robusta at lower altitudes — is predominantly commercial grade, lacking the volcanic altitude advantage that drives specialty quality in North Kivu and South Kivu. The main towns of Kindu (provincial capital) and Shabunda (western South Kivu / eastern Maniema) are associated with coffee collection and processing. Maniema is among the DRC's more remote and least-serviced provinces, with minimal road infrastructure connecting communities to markets.

---

## Geography and Terrain

Maniema Province covers approximately 132,000 km², stretching from the foothills of the eastern rift highlands westward into the Congo basin's forested lowlands. Elevations range from approximately 400 metres in the western basin areas to 1,400 metres on the eastern highland margins. The Lualaba River (upper Congo) bisects the province. Coffee is grown predominantly in the eastern higher-altitude areas (700–1,400 m), where temperatures are moderated and Arabica cultivation is viable; the lower central and western zones host Robusta.

---

## Farming Systems

Smallholder farming communities with minimal cooperative organisation and limited access to technical support. Road access is poor across much of the province, constraining cherry delivery to processing points and export logistics.

---

## Processing

Predominantly informal farm-level processing for both Arabica and Robusta. Some cooperatives operate basic wet mills in the eastern highland sub-zones.

---

## Varieties

**Arabica** (eastern elevations): Bourbon and Typica at 900–1,400 m, producing commercial-to-lower-specialty quality.
**Robusta** (lower central zones): Semi-wild and cultivated *Coffea canephora* at 400–900 m; commercial grade.

---

## Cup Profile

Maniema washed Arabica (900–1,300 m): mild, balanced; chocolate, caramel, low acidity; commercial to lower-specialty; SCA 78–82 for typical lots. The altitude deficit compared to Kivu limits complexity and distinction.

---

## Key Facts

- East-central DRC; Maniema Province; transition zone between Albertine Rift and Congo basin
- Altitude: 700–1,400 m; Arabica in eastern highland margins; Robusta in lower zones
- Kindu: provincial capital; Shabunda: associated coffee collection point
- Commercial grade dominant; limited processing infrastructure; minimal cooperative organisation
- Profile: mild, balanced, commercial; subordinate to Kivu specialty tier
