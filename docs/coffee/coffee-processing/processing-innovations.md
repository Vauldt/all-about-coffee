## Overview

Processing innovations in specialty coffee refer to the development and refinement of post-harvest methods beyond traditional washed and natural processing — including anaerobic fermentation, carbonic maceration, lactic fermentation, controlled yeast inoculation, and hybrid processing techniques — that have expanded the flavour range achievable from a given green coffee. Pioneered by producers and researchers in Costa Rica, Colombia, and Ethiopia from the 2010s onward, these methods allow producers to target specific fermentation by-products that create distinctive cup profiles, often commanding significant price premiums at auction. Processing innovation is now a major competitive differentiator in the specialty market and a subject of active scientific research into microbiology, water activity, and sensory outcomes.
