## Overview

Coffee processing encompasses the methods used to transform freshly harvested coffee cherries into stable, exportable green beans. The three principal approaches — washed, natural, and honey — differ in how much fruit remains in contact with the seed during drying, governing fermentation activity and the flavour compounds transferred to the final cup. For comprehensive coverage of each method, see the linked articles.
