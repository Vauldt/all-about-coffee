# Channeling and Evenness

Summary:: Channeling occurs when water finds preferential paths through the coffee bed, creating uneven extraction.

- Causes:
  - Uneven distribution.
  - Inconsistent tamping.
  - Clumpy or highly uneven grind.
- Symptoms: Shots that run fast on one side, spurting, thin body with both sour and bitter notes.
- Solutions:
  - Improve puck prep (distribution tools, careful tamping).
  - Adjust grind for uniformity.
  - Refine flow and pressure ramp-up where possible.
