# Training and Education MOC

Coffee education spans formal certification through industry bodies, structured in-house staff training, and self-directed study. The Specialty Coffee Association's Coffee Skills Programme and the CQI's Q Grader system provide the primary professional certification frameworks, while café operations depend on structured staff training across technical skills, product knowledge, and customer service. This MOC maps both the formal certification landscape and the training resources that support ongoing development in coffee practice.
