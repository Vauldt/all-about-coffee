# Variety Characteristics and Evaluation MOC

Coffee varieties differ across hundreds of measurable traits: plant architecture, leaf morphology, cherry and bean characteristics, agronomic performance under different conditions, disease resistance, and cup quality potential. Understanding these characteristics informs variety selection, quality optimisation, and the systematic evaluation protocols used by research institutions and producers to compare varieties across environments. This MOC maps variety traits, disease resistance, quality potential, and the evaluation methods used to assess them.
