# Coffee Chemistry MOC

Coffee is one of the most chemically complex beverages on Earth, containing over 1,000 volatile aromatic compounds alongside carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, phenolics, and alkaloids that collectively determine flavour, aroma, body, and sensory experience. Every stage from cherry to cup involves significant chemical transformation — this MOC maps the chemistry domain from compound composition through reaction mechanisms to practical brewing and roasting applications.

## Learning Paths

**Beginner:** [Maillard Reaction](../coffee-roasting/maillard-reaction.md) and [Caramelisation](../roasting-coffee-beans/caramelisation.md) provide an introduction to the key heat-driven processes; [Caffeine](../coffee-chemistry/caffeine.md), [Organic Acids](../coffee-science/organic-acids.md), and Sugars cover the primary flavour-active compound groups. Green Coffee Composition and Roasted Coffee Composition give the baseline chemical framework.

**Intermediate:** [Volatile Compounds](../coffee-roasting/volatile-compounds.md) and Fermentation Chemistry extend understanding to aroma formation and processing; [Extraction Chemistry](../extraction/extraction-chemistry.md) and [Water Chemistry Basics](../coffee-brewing/water-chemistry-basics.md) connect chemistry to brewing practice.

**Advanced:** Analytical Chemistry methods, Sensory-Chemical Correlation, Flavour Threshold, and Odour Activity Value support research-level engagement with the chemistry-sensory link.
