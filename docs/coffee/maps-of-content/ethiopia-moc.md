# Ethiopia MOC

Ethiopia is the birthplace of *Coffea arabica* and the source of the world's most genetically diverse coffee — an estimated 6,000–10,000 distinct varieties growing wild and in cultivation across its highland forest and smallholder systems. The country's principal growing regions span three geographic axes: the southern highlands (Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Guji), the eastern highlands (Harrar), and the western highlands (Jimma, Kaffa). This MOC navigates the canonical country overview and individual region data files for Ethiopia, covering the full arc from wild forest origins to internationally celebrated specialty lots.
