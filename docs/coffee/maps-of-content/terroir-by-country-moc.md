# Terroir-by-Country MOC

Terroir encompasses the environmental and agricultural factors — altitude, climate, soil, variety, and processing tradition — that give coffee from each country and region its distinctive character. Country-level terroir provides the broadest frame; sub-regional profiles capture the finer distinctions within a producing nation. This MOC maps terroir notes by country and links to the conceptual framework and sub-regional deep dives that explain how these factors interact.
