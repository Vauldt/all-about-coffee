# Specialty Coffee Principles MOC

The specialty coffee movement is defined not just by measurable quality thresholds but by a set of principles — around traceability, direct trade, producer relationships, education, and environmental ethics — that distinguish it from the commodity market. This MOC collects the notes covering these principles: the standards that define quality, the trade models that attempt to distribute value equitably, and the sustainability commitments that have become central to specialty identity. For the institutions that enforce and propagate these principles, see [Specialty Coffee Institutions MOC](specialty-coffee-institutions-moc.md).
