# Coffee History MOC

This MOC covers the history of coffee from its legendary Ethiopian origins in the 9th century through the Arabian cultivation era, the rise of European coffeehouse culture, the commodity decades of the first wave, the coffeehouse renaissance of the second wave, and the quality-focused third wave that defined specialty coffee from the 2000s onward. It brings together biographical notes on the pioneers who shaped the modern industry alongside chronological articles marking the sector's key turning points. Related histories of competition, processing, and equipment are covered in their own MOCs.
