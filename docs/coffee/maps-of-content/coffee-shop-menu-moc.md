# Coffee Shop Menu MoC

The café menu defines the drink range, establishes quality standards through recipe documentation, and communicates the café's identity to customers. This MOC covers menu architecture, drink definitions, recipe specifications, and signature beverage development — from baseline espresso and filter offerings through to milk-based and specialty drinks.
