# Caffè Mocha

⬆️ ../Dictionary of Coffee Drinks

A caffè mocha combines espresso, chocolate, and milk, often topped with foam or cream.

Typical components:
- Espresso
- Chocolate syrup or cocoa
- Steamed milk
    

Sweetness and chocolate intensity vary by recipe.
