# Water in Coffee MOC

Water is the primary ingredient in every cup of coffee, yet its practical management — selecting the right source, treating it for a specific brewing environment, and diagnosing when it is causing problems — is a distinct discipline from understanding its chemistry. This MOC covers the applied side of water knowledge: water by brewing method, treatment systems for home and café, DIY mineral recipes, regional water profiles, and troubleshooting common water-related faults. For foundational water chemistry — mineral composition, TDS, pH, hardness, and the SCA parameters — see [Water MOC](water-moc.md).
