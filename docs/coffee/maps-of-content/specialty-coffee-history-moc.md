# Specialty Coffee History MOC

The specialty coffee movement has a traceable history stretching from the pioneering work of Alfred Peet and Erna Knutsen in the 1960s–70s through three defined waves of development and into the ongoing debates of the 2020s. This MOC maps that history chronologically and thematically, linking the foundational moments, institutional milestones, and cultural shifts that transformed coffee from commodity to craft. It focuses specifically on the movement's history; for broader coffee history see [Coffee History MOC](coffee-history-moc.md).
