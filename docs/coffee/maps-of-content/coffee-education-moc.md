# Coffee Education MOC

Coffee education spans a wide spectrum — from a home brewer taking their first steps into specialty coffee, through to a professional pursuing SCA certification or a café educator designing structured training programmes. This MOC organises the core educational resources in the encyclopedia: beginner guides, reference materials, domain-specific learning paths, and links to the professional certification and staff training landscape. Whether the goal is brewing a better cup at home or building a curriculum for a training academy, this hub maps where to start and where to go next.
