# Extraction by Brewing Method MOC

While extraction fundamentals remain constant across all brewing methods, each method creates a distinct environment that shapes how soluble compounds are released: espresso uses high pressure to accelerate extraction in seconds, percolation methods rely on staged water addition and gravity, immersion methods allow full contact throughout steeping, and cold brew extends extraction over many hours at low temperature. Understanding method-specific extraction behaviour allows parameters to be adapted to any brewing context. This MOC links to method-specific extraction details alongside the coffee variable factors — roast, processing, origin, and freshness — that interact with those methods.
