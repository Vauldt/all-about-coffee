# Coffee Shop Compliance MoC

Coffee shop compliance encompasses health and safety practice, food safety management, allergen obligations, and the jurisdiction-specific legal frameworks that govern café operations. Compliance obligations vary significantly between countries and often between regions within a country; this MOC maps the practical daily guidance alongside the legislative reference material.
