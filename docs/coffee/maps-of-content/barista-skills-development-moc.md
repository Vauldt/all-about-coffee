# Barista Skills Development MOC

Barista skills development is the progressive journey from novice to master, encompassing technical ability, sensory acuity, product knowledge, and customer service excellence. Excellence requires development across five interconnected domains — technical, sensory, knowledge, service, and professional — not technical ability alone. This MOC maps the complete skill landscape and links to focused subsidiary notes for each domain.
