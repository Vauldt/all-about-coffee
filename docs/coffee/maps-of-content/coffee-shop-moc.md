# Coffee Shop MoC

The coffee shop knowledge domain covers the full operational, educational, and cultural dimensions of running a specialty café — from the espresso programme and menu architecture through to staff training, quality systems, compliance obligations, and business management. This MOC serves as the primary navigation hub for all coffee shop content in the vault, delegating to domain-specific sub-MOCs for detailed coverage.
