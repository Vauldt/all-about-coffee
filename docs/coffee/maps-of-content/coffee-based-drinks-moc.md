# Coffee Based Drinks MOC

The coffee-based drinks served in cafés worldwide are built on a small set of foundations — espresso, steamed milk, water, and a few flavour additions — varied primarily by ratio, milk texture, temperature, and serving vessel. Understanding how each drink is constructed, and how the preparation variables interact, is the practical core of café service. This MOC organises preparation guides, drink definitions, and technical references for the major espresso-based and filter-based drinks found on contemporary café menus.
