# Peru MOC

Peru is South America's third-largest coffee producer, cultivating Arabica exclusively in the high-altitude cloud forest zones (*ceja de selva*) on the eastern Andean slopes. The country is defined by its smallholder cooperative model and its position as one of the world's leading certified organic coffee exporters. This MOC organises knowledge about Peruvian coffee geography, regional terroir, variety distribution, and the cooperative and specialty sectors that shape how Peruvian coffee reaches international markets.

## Varieties and Processing

**Varieties:** Typica (dominant), Bourbon, Caturra, Catimor (post-*Roya* replanting), Gesha (specialty estates)

**Processing:** Washed dominant across all regions; cooperative wet mills; natural and honey emerging in specialty lots
