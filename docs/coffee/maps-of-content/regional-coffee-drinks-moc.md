# Regional Coffee Drinks MOC

Coffee drinks vary dramatically across cultures, reflecting local traditions, available equipment, social customs, and historical influences. Italian espresso culture established the core template for milk-based drinks adopted worldwide, while regions from Turkey to Vietnam developed entirely distinct preparation methods and service rituals independently. This MOC maps the world's regional coffee drink traditions, from classic European preparations through Asian, Middle Eastern, and Antipodean styles.
