# MoC — Regional Coffee Drinks

This map of content covers coffee preparations and drink traditions specific to particular countries, regions, and cultures — where local ingredients, social customs, equipment, and coffee culture have produced distinct preparations that differ from the internationally dominant espresso-and-filter model. Regional and cultural coffee drinks represent the diversity of how coffee has been adapted and integrated across the world.
