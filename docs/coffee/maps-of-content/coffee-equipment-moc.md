# Coffee Equipment MOC

Coffee equipment encompasses every device involved in grinding, brewing, and serving coffee, from hand-powered manual tools to precision-controlled commercial machines. The category divides broadly into grinders, espresso machines, manual and automatic brewers, and accessories such as scales, kettles, and distribution tools. Equipment choice is determined by brewing method, volume, budget, and the degree of manual control desired.
